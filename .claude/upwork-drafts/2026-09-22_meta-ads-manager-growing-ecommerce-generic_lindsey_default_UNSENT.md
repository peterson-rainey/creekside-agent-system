## PROPOSAL

How many of the ads live in your account right now got enough spend last month to be judged? I ask because on most stores I audit, the ad count grows faster than the budget, spreading spend so thin that Meta never collects enough sales on any one ad to learn what's working.

One e-commerce account I took over this year, a replacement parts store, came to me with 232 active ads by the owner's own count, about $15,000 a month in spend, and 11 campaigns taking budget. Retargeting was split across six small campaigns, each optimizing for something shallow like page views or add-to-carts. In the first few weeks I rebuilt it, with retargeting folded into one conversions campaign, fewer campaigns overall, tighter targeting and new creative. Cost per conversion went from $34.72 in the month before I started to $19.89 by April, and held near $23 in May. The cost to reach people barely moved, about $8 per thousand impressions in both January and April, so the gain wasn't a cheaper auction.

I built and sold my own e-commerce business, so I read an account the way an owner does: by what it sells, not by what Ads Manager labels a result. Across 10+ years in e-commerce, skincare, meal prep and dental, the accounts that keep improving are the ones where each test answers one question and gets enough budget to finish.

You'd get a written update every two weeks on what changed and why, plus a live dashboard.
