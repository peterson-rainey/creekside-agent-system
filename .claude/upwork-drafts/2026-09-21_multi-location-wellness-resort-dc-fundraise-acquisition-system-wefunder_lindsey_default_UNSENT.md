## PROPOSAL (paste-ready)

Quick question before anything else: if you do open a Wefunder round, do you expect your first investors to come from the guest list you're about to build?

I ask because it changes how that list gets built. Ads asking people to invest will most likely fall under Meta's financial products and services category, which limits lookalikes, exclusions, and age and ZIP targeting but still allows custom audiences like your own email list. So every guest you collect and tag now is someone the raise can reach later. Once the round is live, Wefunder's rules also keep terms like what the money will build and how much you've raised off the ads.

Here's what I've run myself, and where my part stopped.

A dental implant group (cases in the upper $30Ks): Meta instant forms with qualifying questions, UTMs passed into their Salesforce, their call center booking consultations. Late April to mid-July: $64,747 of spend, 2,558 leads, $25.31 each. I checked the CRM for who actually booked, and kept one campaign running even though its leads cost more, because they were booking. A membership or retreat sale would need the same check.

A survival-gear store I run on Meta: the UK and US ran side by side for six weeks. The UK was cheaper to reach and returned 0.59x on ad spend, the US 3.79x, and the blended 2.32x described neither. You have four drive-to markets, so I'd report each one separately from week one.

CRM: GoHighLevel. Email, SMS, forms and the pipeline sit in one place, and a workflow can send pipeline stage changes back to Meta, so Meta can see which sign-ups took a real next step. I work in it now with a dental practice whose leads land there.

Meta: I manage it myself, day to day. I've got a junior ad manager under me, and I work with a tracking specialist.

Creative: I run the ad creative strategy (which angles get tested, briefs for your photographer and designer, what gets cut). I don't run organic social.

Hospitality, wellness, membership or crowdfunding: none. Of the areas you listed, my book is e-commerce and high-value consumer services.

First 30 days: Pixel, Conversions API and GA4 on your landing pages, and every sign-up tagged by interest (stays, membership, retreats, investing) and market. A welcome sequence so people hear from you while you build. Then Meta tests of three or four angles from your existing photography, judged on cost per qualified sign-up by market, with lookalikes of your best sign-ups in the guest ads, since Meta limits them in investor ads.

Roughly what could go into ads each month: under $5,000, $5,000 to $15,000, or more? Anything I'd recommend depends on that range, starting with how many markets to test at once.

I can walk you through any of these accounts on a call. There's a short video on my profile that shows how I run accounts.
