## PROPOSAL
When a lead turns into a booked appointment or a sale inside GoHighLevel, does that stage change get sent back to Meta, or does Meta only ever see the form fill? That gap is usually why lead volume looks healthy while sales stay flat. Meta optimizes toward whatever it's told counts, so if all it hears is "form submitted," it finds more form submitters. There's a quieter version too: HighLevel only files a lead as Facebook paid social when utm_source contains "fb_ad", and the match is case-sensitive, so a funnel tagged "facebook" lands in the wrong bucket and the revenue report blames the wrong campaign.
