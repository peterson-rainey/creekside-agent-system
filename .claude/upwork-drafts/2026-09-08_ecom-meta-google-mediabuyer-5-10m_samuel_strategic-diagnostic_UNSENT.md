At the $5M to $10M+ spend level, the number that decides whether the next million is profitable is not ROAS. It is what CPM and frequency do when budget goes in faster than the creative team can refresh angles. Most accounts at that level are not bid-constrained or audience-constrained, they are creative-constrained, and the ROAS chart hides it until CAC has already moved.

Here is that exact pattern from an account our team ran. Luxury furniture DTC on Meta. Over four months we pushed spend from about $17K a month to just past $40K. Purchase volume grew, so on the dashboard it read like clean scaling. Underneath, CPM roughly doubled, from the mid thirties to near $80, and cost per purchase drifted from about $247 to $313. Nothing was broken. The audience was saturating faster than we could ship new angles, and there was no prospecting pool wide enough to absorb the budget at the old price. At your spend the same curve is steeper and arrives sooner, and the fix is creative throughput and audience architecture, not another pass at bids.

The second thing I would want eyes on is what the account is optimizing toward. On a consumer camera brand, campaigns pointed at shallow events, add to cart and initiate checkout, produced purchases at about $311. Same account, same months, campaigns pointed straight at the purchase event came in near $61. Meta learns from whatever event you feed it. Feed it a browse signal and it gets very good at finding browsers. That leak scales linearly with budget, so it is worth catching before you pour more in.

Straight with you on scale, since you set the bar at $5M to $10M and I would rather you hear it from me than find it later. Our e-commerce accounts have run in the low five to low six figures a month, not the seven-figure brands in your post. What carries over is the diagnosis: the saturation curve, creative supply as the real constraint, and optimizing to the money event instead of the vanity one. Meta is where our team is sharpest, with Google run alongside it, which matches the split you described.

What is the constraint you are actually hitting as you scale, CPM and frequency climbing, creative volume, or CAC drifting while ROAS holds? Tell me which and I will tell you where I would look first.
