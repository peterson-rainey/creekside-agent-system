**Where we can help**

Our team runs Meta and Google Ads every day, and a lot of growth budgets get wasted in the same few places:

1. Paying for cheap views that drop off in the first few seconds. They add to the view count but rarely turn into subscribers or return viewers.
2. Sending Meta traffic to YouTube and not measuring what happens after the click. Without that, you can't tell which traffic turns into subscribers.
3. Promoting every upload the same way. Usually one or two videos are the right entry point for a new viewer, and the budget should sit behind those.

**How I'd start**

First we'd look at your YouTube Analytics to find which videos already hold new viewers and turn them into subscribers. That becomes the ad inventory. Then we'd test paid views on YouTube against Meta traffic for each of those videos, and judge each one by subscribers and watch time gained per dollar, not raw views.
