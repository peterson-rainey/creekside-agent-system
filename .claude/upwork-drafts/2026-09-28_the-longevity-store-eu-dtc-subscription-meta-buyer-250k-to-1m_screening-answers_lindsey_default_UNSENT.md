## ANSWER (paste below this line)

Two Meta accounts I run or ran directly.

The first was a US replacement-parts ecommerce brand spending about $12K to $16K a month. I took it over in February, retired six small inherited campaigns and added a sales-objective campaign. Cost per purchase went from about $28 in January, the inherited month, to about $19 in April with CPM steady near $8, then about $21 in May. The audit also changed targeting and creative, so I can't tell you which change moved it. The client left in May.

The second is an active dental and aesthetics practice spending about $100K a month on Meta since March. Its CPM more than doubled from September to December with spend roughly level, then fell back by March. That is why I read a Q4 result against the auction before blaming the ads. It is lead generation, so I don't quote a cost per lead from it.

Neither is €250k on one brand, and neither is a supplement or subscription business.
