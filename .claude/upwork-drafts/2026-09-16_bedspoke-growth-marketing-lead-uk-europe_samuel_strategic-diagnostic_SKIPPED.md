## Opening angle, kept in case Bedspoke posts a Google or Meta management job
Their lead types are not equal. A free UK sample is the cheapest lead and the easiest one for Google and Meta bidding to find. A €5 EU sample is a paid commitment. A showroom visit starts with a phone call and closes in person, so the ad platforms see it least. First investigation: match 6-12 months of sample requests, showroom bookings and trade sign-ups to orders, by lead type, market and source. Then send orders back to Google and Meta against the lead that started them. Separately, two platform specialists can each count the same person, so their combined lead totals overstate what came in.
