## PROPOSAL

On a B2B account, the conversion column usually counts every form fill and call, and Google can't tell a buyer from a student, a vendor or a job seeker. Rank campaigns by that column and the winners are often the ones producing the cheapest contacts, not the ones your sales team wants more of. If Smart Bidding is on, it's learning from that same list.

So the first session would open in your conversion settings, next to last quarter's leads, each one marked real opportunity, poor fit, or spam. Most of what gets decided after that depends on how those two line up.

Between sessions, the analysis follows that comparison. Usually it turns up search terms that spent without producing a real lead, and campaigns whose cost per lead looks fine until the junk comes out. Because Google logs a conversion on its click date, a long sales cycle makes the newest weeks read worse than they'll settle. And Google won't import an offline conversion uploaded more than 90 days after the click, 63 if you're on enhanced conversions for leads, so deals that close later than that have to be judged in your CRM. Every change goes in on its own, since a bid strategy switch restarts Smart Bidding's learning period.

About a dozen Google Ads accounts run through our team today. The nearest comparison is a paving company whose buyers are property managers and contractors, and the main Search campaign there sits above $17 a click. At that price, a click from a price shopper costs the same as one from a property manager, so keeping price shoppers out is part of how the account is built.

The working sessions are with a Google Ads specialist on our team, with me on strategy, and each one ends with a short write-up of what we found and what changes next. By the last one you'd have a plan your team can run, or, if it makes more sense, our team takes the account over day to day.

Does monthly spend run closer to $5,000 or $25,000, and does your sales team already flag which leads were real? Session one starts from wherever those answers land.
