Have you run the free scorecard on a founder's own brand and put that screenshot in the ad? I ask because a buyer who has never checked what ChatGPT says about their company doesn't know this is a problem yet, and no amount of feature copy fixes that. Seeing a competitor named in the answer where they aren't usually does.

**Where I've seen this before**

I've run Meta for a B2B software company whose buyers didn't know the category existed. Meta's job there was making the problem visible before anyone would go looking for a fix. SourceSignal sits in the same spot: AI visibility is new enough that the first ads have to create the question before they can answer it.

I also built and sold my own e-commerce business, so I write creative the way I did when it was my money: every concept tied to a sign-up, not a view count. In the Meta book I work across, only about 3% of campaigns use a video objective even though most of the creative is video, because view-optimized campaigns find watchers, not buyers.

**How I'd shape the first concepts**

- Demonstration: a 20-second screen recording. Buyer question typed into ChatGPT, competitor named, brand missing, cut to the scorecard.
- Separate angles for founders and for agencies. Someone on your Brand plan and someone on your Agency plan are buying for different reasons, and one ad won't speak to both.
- LinkedIn gets the same concepts cut tighter, with job-title targeting doing the filtering the creative has to do on Meta. My own paid work is mostly Meta, so I'd let the first test show what LinkedIn earns before it takes a big share.

I'd optimize toward the free scorecard first, then shift to paid plans once there's enough volume for Meta to learn from.
