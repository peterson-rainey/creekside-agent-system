## PROPOSAL

With a 30 to 90 day sales cycle, Meta can't really learn from closed-won. Standard attribution counts a click for 7 days at most, so a deal closing on day 45 never gets credited to the ad that started it. Delivery drifts toward the fastest converter, usually the cheap lead. The event worth bidding on is the earliest one that predicts an SQL: a demo request from a company that fits on size and role, checked server-side and sent through the Conversions API within a day or two. SQL and closed-won still come back from the CRM for reporting cost per SQL and payback, not for steering delivery. If you run Instant Forms, Meta's conversion leads goal can optimize on a CRM stage directly, but only if that stage lands within 28 days and you're generating at least 200 leads a month.

Meta won't reliably find a job title through interests, so the creative does the targeting by naming the role and the problem, and lookalikes get seeded from SQL and closed-won contacts rather than every form fill.

On proof, I can't show you a scaled B2B SaaS Meta account with SQL numbers behind it. The closest is a B2B SaaS company selling referral software to home service contractors. In the six months after their seed round, inbound leads and ARR both doubled, with Meta building awareness for a category contractors didn't know existed and Google capturing the search demand that followed. Spend started around $2K per platform and roughly doubled at month three, and the record is lead and ARR growth, not cost per SQL. That case study is attached.

On creative, 20+ concepts a month only pays off if spend can read them. An ad set needs about 50 optimization events a week to exit learning, so concepts get screened on an early signal like hold rate, and only survivors get judged on qualified demos.

The ceiling usually shows in qualified rate before CPL, as frequency climbs inside a finite B2B audience. When Meta's payback or LTV:CAC runs worse than your other channels, I'd rather tell you to hold spend than keep scaling.

Day to day, a Meta specialist on our team runs the account, our conversion tracking specialist sets up the CAPI and CRM wiring, and I stay on strategy.
