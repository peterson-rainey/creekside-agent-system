Quick question before any campaign build: when a U.S. traveler books a stay, does the reservation finish on your own website, or on a separate booking engine page?

I ask because that handoff is where tracking on trips like this usually breaks. If the pixel never fires on the booking engine's confirmation page, Meta only sees people browsing the site, never the ones who reserve, so it goes out and finds more browsers. Before spending for bookings, I'd want a test reservation showing up in Ads Manager as a purchase with its value attached.
