## PROPOSAL

Family law has a budget problem most practice areas don't: the highest-volume searches are people looking for free legal help, divorce forms, or how to file without a lawyer. Broad match and smart bidding keep finding them, and if every short call counts as a conversion, bidding learns to buy more.

So the first thing I'd check is what Google counts as a lead. If every call and form is a conversion, the account is trained on the wrong outcome, and negatives alone won't fix it. Second is location: Google's default reaches people in, or interested in, your area, which can mean paying for out-of-state clicks. Then search terms, to see which parts are booking consults and should be left alone.

To your questions:

Law firms: our team has run Google Ads for two firms with results I can show you, an Arizona personal injury firm and an Orange County bankruptcy firm. We haven't run a family law account. Bankruptcy is the closest match: local, consumer, emotionally loaded, with the same free-help search problem. That was a restructure of an existing account by market, and leads went from 117 to 229 while cost per conversion went from $86 to $50.

Who does the work: a dedicated Google Ads operator on our team runs the account day to day, with a tracking specialist on call and conversion setup. You'd work with the same people every week.

LSA: yes, alongside Search. LSA has no search terms report or negative keywords, so the levers are the service types you select, service area, hours, and rating each lead in the dashboard. On a home services account our team ran, LSA produced 230+ phone leads over twelve months.

Search terms: weekly, and more often in the first few weeks after cleanup.

Qualified leads: your intake marks each lead as consult booked or retained, and we send that back to Google against the original click, so bidding learns from consults instead of calls. Calls can only be matched this way through a Google forwarding number, so that's part of the setup.

Fee: 20% of ad spend with a $1,500 monthly minimum, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee that covers the audit and cleanup, on a 90-day minimum.

Both case studies are attached. On the PI firm, total ad spend across channels came to about $950 per case over six months, and Google alone ran about $91 per conversion. That account has since ended.

Roughly what are you spending per month across Search and LSA combined, closer to $3K or $20K?
