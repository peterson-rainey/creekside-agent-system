Has Meta classified your pixel as health and wellness yet, and if it has, do you know which conversion events you lost when it did? Telemedicine platforms are one of the examples Meta names for that category, the classification cannot be edited from your side, only sent back for review, and the first symptom is usually a campaign quietly optimizing toward something shallower than the event you actually care about.

Healthcare is most of what I run on Meta. Four healthcare ad accounts are mine right now. The one closest in shape to this was a dental group where cold lead forms brought in 2,558 submissions at about $25 each, and cost per lead was never the thing that governed that account. I pulled spend back by roughly three quarters over two months because the booking team could not physically work the volume. The ceiling was capacity, not media, and that only became visible because leads were tracked past the form.

If any part of what you promote is prescription, the gate sits before the media entirely. Meta requires telehealth providers to be actively certified with LegitScript, limits those ads to the US, Canada and New Zealand, and blocks under 18 targeting. Certification does not stop individual ads from being rejected either, which is why copy here gets written against the standard first and optimized second.

Two things would tell me whether I am useful to you. Roughly where is monthly media spend sitting now, closer to five figures or to low four? And is what you advertise prescription, cash pay wellness, or both, since that one answer decides whether this is an ordinary lead gen account or a certification and measurement problem first.
