## PROPOSAL

On a Shopify jewelry store, much of the targeting in Google Ads happens in the product titles, not the campaign settings. Shopping ads don't use keywords. Google matches searches like "14k gold huggie earrings" against each product's title and description, and a piece called Luna gives it little to match. We'd rewrite the titles Google sees with metal, stone, style and size, without renaming anything on your site.

If your range runs from $60 studs to $900 pendants, one return target is too strict for some pieces and too loose for others, because the margins differ. We'd split the catalog into price bands, each with its own budget and target, and I'd hold Performance Max back at launch. PMax reaches further, but it hands Google more control over where the money goes, and a new account has no sales history to steer it.

Jewelry searches also pull in people who aren't shopping, like selling gold, repairs or ring size charts, so negatives go in before launch. Gift searches like "anniversary gift for her" get their own Search ads, apart from someone who already knows they want a gold tennis bracelet. The copy answers what a shopper can't check through a screen, like solid gold or plated, whether the stone is certified, sizing and returns.

Budget follows the gifting calendar, not a flat monthly number, rising before Black Friday, easing off after the last Christmas shipping day, and rising again for Valentine's and Mother's Day.

We haven't run a jewelry account, so the closest examples are two other Shopify stores. On one our team built from scratch, non-brand Shopping and PMax overall came out even, 5.66x against 5.64x over the same 69 days, so I'd keep the control. After launch, the work is mostly moving money toward what pays back. On a survival gear store we run now, UK multi-pack campaigns spent about $2,500 in their first month for about $1,070 in sales. We shut them off in August and moved the budget to the US catalog. Non-brand ROAS went from 1.5x in July to 2.6x in August, about half of that from the UK cut, and September is running around 2.4x.

What price range do most of your pieces sit in, and do you make them or source them? And what monthly range are you planning to spend? What I'd build at $5,000 a month looks different at $20,000.
