## PROPOSAL (paste-ready)

Running Meta across several businesses, the account with the cheapest impressions is rarely the one producing business. A financial-advisory client of ours runs two Meta accounts under one owner, both on the same lead objective. Since June, the account paying about $177 per thousand impressions has produced leads at about $154. The one paying $30 has paid over $2,000 per lead, and it held about 43% of the combined budget. Judged on CPM, you'd scale the wrong one.

The second thing I'd plan around: real estate, financial services and recruiting each fall under a Meta Special Ad Category (housing, financial products, employment). Those campaigns lose lookalikes, age and gender targeting and audience exclusions, get a 15-mile minimum radius, and detailed targeting shrinks to a short approved list. Website, video and engagement audiences still work, so on those offers retargeting carries more of the load than cold targeting can. The category is set when a campaign is built, so one Meta flags later gets rebuilt, not appealed.
