When every customer lives within about 25 miles, the area itself does most of the targeting. Stacking interests inside a radius that small shrinks the audience until Meta struggles to find anyone cheaply, so starting narrow here means one campaign, one ad set and two or three ads, with the creative doing the qualifying.

Two geo details catch most local accounts. Meta's default location setting includes people recently in the area, not only people who live there, so a weekend visitor can see your ad. And a radius drawn from your address usually pulls in towns you don't serve. We'd shape the targeting around where your jobs actually come from, exclude the rest, and add a postcode question to the enquiry form so every lead's location is on record instead of guessed.

GA4 and the Pixel can record someone tapping your number, but not whether a conversation happened. A call tracking number on the site shows which calls came from Meta and how long they lasted, so a three-minute booking call counts as an enquiry and a ten-second hang-up doesn't. Those calls then sit in GA4 as conversions next to the form enquiries. The tracking tool itself is a small monthly subscription on your side.

On creative, yes, a couple of simple images and rewritten copy will do the job. Phone photos of your real work, van or team usually beat stock because they look local. We write the copy and build the ads. The first line names the area and the service so people outside it scroll past, and the variations test different reasons to call, not different colours.

A dental practice we ran on Meta passed its leads to a phone team, and we added qualifying questions to its lead forms, accepting a higher cost per lead because each enquiry arrived already screened.

At around twelve leads a month, two or three weeks tells you whether tracking works, not which ad wins. That's why our minimum term is 90 days, with our team making the day-to-day changes and me reviewing where the leads came from. We don't bill hourly. Management is the greater of $1,500 a month or 20% of Meta spend, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding covering the Pixel, GTM, GA4, call tracking and build.
