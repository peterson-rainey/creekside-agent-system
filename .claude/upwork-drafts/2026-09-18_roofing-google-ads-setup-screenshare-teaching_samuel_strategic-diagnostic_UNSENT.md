Most new roofing accounts are decided by three settings you never get prompted to change.

Worth saying first: we do not have roofing results to show you. What we have is high-ticket home services on Google, including a paving and sealcoating contractor our team runs today, and the numbers from it are below.

The first setting is location. Google's default is Presence or interest, which serves people merely interested in your service area alongside people actually in it. The option that narrows it to people in or regularly in your area is one dropdown away, and it is not what you get by default.

The second is how calls get counted. If the number on your call asset is your own business line rather than a Google forwarding number, those calls can never be imported as conversions, because Google matches an imported call on the caller's number and the call start time. Calls from ads are also tracked on mobile only, not desktop, and only when call reporting is switched on. Calls should count only past a minimum length, or bidding learns from solar reps, job seekers and wrong numbers.

The third is where your clicks come from. Search Partners are included by default on every new Search campaign, and Google's own note on them says clicks from those sites may not always reflect highly targeted traffic. On a first campaign that is money spent before you have enough data to see it.

Those three are worth your screen share time. Where to click is the easy half. The half that keeps it working is knowing which numbers mean something once it is live, so two things to know going in: your report counts conversions on the date of the click rather than the date the lead arrived, so a slow week can read worse than it was, and adding an ad or changing the bid strategy restarts the learning period, which is why changes get made deliberately instead of daily.

The numbers from that paving account, current as of two days ago: the Search campaign has produced 217 leads at $190 each since last November, turning 9.2% of clicks into leads at a $17.53 click. Performance Max in the same account buys clicks at $3.17 and leads at $288, converting 1.1%. Cheap clicks, expensive leads.

On the tracking, our team has a dedicated conversion tracking specialist who owns the call and form setup and confirms it is recording before spend ramps rather than after.

Trade searches are not cheap to buy, as that $17.53 shows, and the first month is where most of the waste happens, so what you can absorb changes how this gets built. What monthly range are you working with, relative to $5,000, and which cities or counties are you covering?
