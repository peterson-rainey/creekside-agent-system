Eight separate event campaigns would each be too thin for Meta to learn from before the doors open, so how the account is structured in week one matters more than any single ad.

**Structure for a short runway**

I would run one Nursing campaign and one Allied Health campaign, with each city as its own ad set, so learning pools by profession while budget can still shift toward whichever city fills slowest. Retargeting your existing followers and video viewers runs separately at a small share of spend, since warm audiences are usually the cheapest to convert close to an event. Because this is relocation recruitment, Meta may class these as employment ads. Where that applies, age, gender and postcode targeting are off the table, so I would build the city radii and audiences around that from day one rather than find out at ad review.

**Registrations are not attendees**

Free events lose a lot of registrants before the door, and show rate moves cost per attendee more than any creative test. I would plan reminders at 48 hours and 2 hours (email or WhatsApp) plus a short "your place is confirmed" ad to registrants. On tracking, Eventbrite supports a Meta pixel, but I would check at setup exactly which event it fires for free tickets, add UTMs per city and profession, and upload each check-in list to Meta afterwards. That gives real cost per attendee for reporting and an audience of people who actually showed up for the next round. Carrying those UTMs into your candidate records is what connects an ad to a placement later.

**Counting leads where they actually land**

On a dental account I run, the client told me leads had dropped for the week. I could see over 50 people had filled out forms and pointed it to where the leads were sitting in Business Manager. Registrations, attendance and candidate records will live in three different places here too, and reporting has to reconcile them or budget decisions can rest on the wrong numbers. Reporting comes every two weeks plus a live dashboard, and I will tell you which creative to film next based on cost per attendee by angle.
