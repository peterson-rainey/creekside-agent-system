## PROPOSAL

Performance Max and Shopping will bid on searches for your own product names, and some of those shoppers were already heading to a retailer that carries you, so the account reports sales the ads didn't really create. Splitting brand and product-name terms out, and reading non-brand return on its own, shows where spend is actually buying new customers.

**On the Claude layer**

The first finding I'd challenge is attribution. Meta and Google will both claim the same Shopify order, so platform revenue added together runs higher than what Shopify actually banked. We judge total spend against Shopify revenue and new-customer orders, then use each platform's own numbers to decide where the money goes inside it. Klaviyo matters too, since a repeat customer who clicks a Meta ad after an email isn't new demand.
