## PROPOSAL

On a repeat-purchase brand, how are you separating new-customer ROAS from what Retargeting and Loyalty report, so Prospecting gets judged on its own numbers instead of the blended ones repeat buyers carry?

**A brand I almost let cut its own top of funnel**
A mattress brand I run wanted Prospecting turned off in favor of Retargeting, since Retargeting's ROAS looked better on paper. I kept it live: the Retargeting pool was still too small to carry volume, and Prospecting itself was still driving sales. We earmarked budget to shift once that audience actually built up. On a repeat-purchase brand like premium pet food or treats, that's the same trap — Loyalty and Retargeting will always out-ROAS Prospecting on a blended number, and cutting it early starves the new-customer pool everything else depends on later.

**Where I've held CPA down without capping spend**
On a meal-prep subscription brand I run, cost per conversion moved from $11.53 in January to $5.50 by July as we narrowed in on what converted, then gave some back to $11.59 in August as we pushed spend to its highest point of the year and CPMs drifted up with it. I'd rather show the full arc than the one good month. Pixel and Conversions API get checked for duplicate or missing events before any of that starts — a scaling call built on bad tracking data is worse than no call at all.
