A comparison marketplace has a trap most dental advertisers don't: you're running two acquisition funnels that feed each other. A patient waiting list with no practices near those patients goes cold, and a practice pitch with no patient demand behind it is hard to close. So the first real decision isn't creative, it's sequencing: which postcodes get practice outreach first, so patient spend lands where someone can actually be booked.

That also changes what counts as a result. A waiting-list signup is cheap and easy for Meta to find. A practice lead that reaches completed onboarding is rare and slow. If both sit in one account optimising toward "leads," Meta will spend toward the cheap one every time.

Two campaigns we'd point you to:

1. A full-arch implant practice (US), Meta lead forms into a call centre, 21 Apr to 15 Jul 2026: $64,746 spend, 2,558 leads, $25.31 cost per lead, measured on Meta's standard lead event with revenue tracked in their Salesforce. Spend was deliberately pulled back mid-run to match call-centre capacity, the same constraint you'll have with practice onboarding. That engagement has ended.
2. A US dental practice, current: one Meta leads campaign produced 123 leads at $15.73 on $1,934 over 12 months, while an engagement campaign in the same account spent $1,826 with no trackable conversions. That split is the first thing we'd look for in your account.

Our team ran both accounts day to day. An anonymised walkthrough is available on a call. You should know upfront: none of our dental work is in the UK, and we don't have a two-sided marketplace case study.

First 30 days:
Week 1: audit the Pixel and Conversions API, agree two separate conversion events (patient signup, practice lead) plus offline stages for "demo booked" and "onboarding complete." Claims and clinical wording get your sign-off before anything runs, given ASA and GDC expectations. Launch patient campaigns on your existing creatives once tracking checks pass.
Weeks 2-4: 3-4 new creative concepts per week on the patient side, practice-owner ads in your priority regions, built from your existing practice contacts where the list is large enough. Pause a concept after roughly 2x target cost with no conversion; scale in 20% steps once cost per signup holds for a week.

Practice acquisition through onboarding: each practice lead gets a stage in your CRM (lead, call, onboarding started, onboarding complete), and those stages get sent back to Meta so optimisation moves toward practices that finish, not ones that fill a form. Weekly report: spend, signups, practice leads, onboarded practices, cost per result, and what we're changing next.

Days 31-90: shift budget toward the postcodes where both sides are converting, test a practice-focused offer, and decide at day 60 whether practice acquisition should stay on Meta or move to direct outreach.

What we'd need from you: 30-60 minutes of practice and patient-facing footage a month (phone video is fine), and a named person for claims approval.

On scope, straight: our team runs paid Meta and Google. We don't do organic posting, Reels editing, daily community management or cold outreach, so that part of the role would need someone else.

Fee: $1,500/month management plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee, ad spend separate. We work on a 90-day minimum rather than a 30-day trial, because a new conversion setup is still in Meta's learning phase at day 30 and the numbers wouldn't tell you much yet. Earliest start: [EARLIEST START].
