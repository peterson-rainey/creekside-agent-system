Paid views are easy to buy on YouTube. What grows a channel is whether those viewers subscribe and come back, so the ads should sit behind the one or two videos that already turn new viewers into subscribers, not behind every upload.

I've worked on over 200 ad accounts and helped manage more than $20 million in spend across Google and Meta. To be upfront, that doesn't include record labels or TikTok, so there's no music or TikTok case study I can send. If that's the bar, I don't clear it.

**How I'd start**

First we'd go through your YouTube Analytics to find which videos hold new viewers best. Those become the ad inventory. Then we'd test paid views on YouTube against Meta traffic sent to the same videos, and judge each one by subscribers and watch time gained per dollar, not raw views. Cheap views that drop off in the first few seconds pad the count and rarely turn into subscribers, so they get cut early.

**One question**

Roughly what monthly ad budget are you working with: $5-10K, $10-25K, or above that?
