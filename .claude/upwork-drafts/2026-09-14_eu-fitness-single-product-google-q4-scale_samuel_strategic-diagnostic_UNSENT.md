# Upwork Proposal - Fitness & health single-product Shopify brand, Google (Search/Shopping/PMax/YouTube) Q4 scale, Meta at EUR 2,500/day

With €2,500 a day already going into Meta, the first Google numbers are going to look better than they are. Branded search and Performance Max will pick up plenty of people Meta already warmed up, report a strong ROAS, and add few customers you wouldn't have gotten anyway. So before scaling anything, our team would set prospecting campaigns to bid higher for new customers using your Shopify customer list, rather than the stricter new-customers-only mode that shrinks the auction right when you need volume, and keep brand in its own campaign with a brand exclusion on PMax. From there Google gets judged on new customers, with conversion values adjusted for margin so bidding chases profit instead of revenue.

YouTube is where I'd be most careful. Video Action campaigns are gone, so cold YouTube now runs through Demand Gen, and Demand Gen will also spend on Gmail, Discover and Display unless each ad group is limited to YouTube. A competitor who looks heavy on YouTube tells you where they're spending, not what's paying back. Prospecting ad groups would stay YouTube-only, with In-stream, In-feed and Shorts split in the placement report. In-stream and Shorts play automatically, so an early drop-off there is a hook problem. In-feed needs a tap first, so a weak number there usually means the thumbnail or title. Viewers who watch through and don't click weren't sold by the offer, and good clicks with weak site conversion point at the landing page, which is where the CRO conversation starts. Those numbers go to your creative team every week.

With one product, Shopping and PMax only have one item to rotate. If your Shopify catalog can carry pack sizes or bundles as their own products, each one becomes a separate feed item with its own title and image, which gives the auction more to test and shows you which offer people actually buy.

A dedicated conversion tracking specialist on our team would handle Enhanced Conversions and server-side tagging, starting with Google's reported purchases against Shopify orders so nothing is double counted next to Meta. If much of the volume is in the EU, Consent Mode v2 decides how much of that signal Google can use, so that gets checked first.

Straight answer on track record. The closest Google account our team runs is a multi-product Shopify brand we built from scratch. Non-brand spend went from about $1,900 a month at 11.5x ROAS to $8,800 at 4.8x and sits around 3.4x now, and total Google spend there has topped out near $12K a month, the low end of the range you described. On YouTube, we've run YouTube inventory through Demand Gen on four accounts. One converted, a healthcare account at about $204 per conversion that has since been paused, and the other three returned nothing. If YouTube cold traffic has to carry the plan from week one, that's a real gap to weigh.

Fees are in USD. Onboarding is $1,500 for Google, then management is 20% of the first $30K of monthly Google spend, 15% of the next $30K and 10% above that, capped at $15K a month, with a 90-day minimum. At $50K a month in Google spend that's $9,000.

Which countries will the Google spend target, and is that the same split Meta runs in today?
