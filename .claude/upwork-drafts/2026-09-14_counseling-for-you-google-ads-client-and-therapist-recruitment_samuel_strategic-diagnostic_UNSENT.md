## PROPOSAL

CFY Google Ads

A prospective client searching "mental health counselor near me" and a therapist searching "mental health counselor jobs" can trigger the same phrase or broad match keywords, and by default every campaign bids toward the same account-level conversion goals. Unless the two sides exclude each other's terms and get separate goals, bidding learns from applicants as if they were clients, and cost per qualified client never becomes a real number.

Google also treats counseling for anxiety, depression and OCD as sensitive health content, so remarketing lists and Customer Match are off the table there. Hiring ads fall under employment rules that bar age, gender and ZIP code targeting, so recruitment near Merrick and Massapequa Park runs on radius targeting.

In your current account I'd start with what counts as a conversion. Your site lists one number for all three offices, so without call tracking by source and a minimum call length, an existing client calling to reschedule can count as a new inquiry. Then whether the careers form shares a conversion with the contact form, then search terms.

On lead quality, your intake team marks each inquiry qualified or not (right service, location, insurance fit), and that status goes back to Google Ads against the click ID, with nothing about the person. Google offers no business associate agreement for GA4, so form contents should never reach it.

For the redesign, page addresses usually change even when the domain stays, and ads pointing at old addresses break, so a redirect map comes first. Your developer also gets a tracking spec, including one application form that takes the resume and license type together, since an emailed resume can't be tracked. Current campaigns keep running as the baseline.

Mental health is thin for us: one small, short 2025 engagement for a therapy practice, where ads naming a diagnosis didn't run. Healthcare is a bigger part of our book, including a naturopathic practice at around $40 per conversion over the last 12 months and a dental practice where tracked calls are the main channel and the main non-converters were patients on plans it doesn't take. Our healthcare Google accounts spend roughly $1,500 to $15,000 a month each. We haven't run recruitment ads before.

Pricing is 20% of monthly ad spend per platform with a $1,500 minimum and a lower rate above $30,000, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee covering the audit and rebuild.

Roughly where does monthly Google spend sit, closer to $3,000 or $15,000? Any client versus recruitment split only helps inside the range you're actually working with.
