## PROPOSAL (paste below this line)

Where do your leads land the moment someone submits a form, and who sees them first? I ask because in August, on a local dental account I ran Meta for, the practice told me leads had dropped sharply over a few days. I could see over 50 people had filled out forms, but they were sitting in the practice's CRM as contacts, not in its pipeline. I pointed the practice to Leads Center in Business Manager, and the practice said it was having its manager call everyone.

**What one report hid**

That same month, two campaigns ran side by side in that account, spending about $1,950 and $1,850. The Leads campaign recorded 124 leads at $15.77 each. The Engagement campaign bought nearly twice the impressions and recorded no leads, and with no call tracking there I couldn't say whether it drove calls. The account rollup blended them to $30.65, which described neither. Before trusting any cost per lead, I'd split it by campaign.

**Where the leads went after the form**

On a dental implant account spending about $32,600 in May, Meta forms fed a call center that couldn't keep up with the lead inflow. Spend dropped to about $15,900 in June, and general dentistry was paused so implant consultations came first in the queue.

**Tests and pages**

I'd test a few clearly different ads at a time rather than one new ad after another, because adding an ad to a live ad set is the kind of edit that sends the ad set back into learning. I'd own the ad-to-page match and where the form's leads go. I don't have a landing-page result to show you, so any page build would be its own conversation.

I've attached one page with the August split, and there's a short video on my profile that explains my process better than text. Is your monthly ad spend nearer $5,000 or $15,000?
