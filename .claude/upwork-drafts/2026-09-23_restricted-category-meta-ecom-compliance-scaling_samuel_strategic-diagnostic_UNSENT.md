The assumption worth testing before you hire anyone is that restricted status costs you performance. In our own book it did not, and the cleanest comparison sits inside one financial services account where a single campaign is built and run under Special Ad Category constraints while the rest of the account is not.

Same account, same twelve months, same lead objective. The unconstrained campaign ran 169,207.66 and returned 746 leads at 226.82 each. The constrained campaign ran 22,378.13 and returned 177 leads at 126.43. CPM was almost identical, 220.32 against 215.81, so the auction was not punishing the constrained side. Where they split was further down: the constrained campaign was worse at earning the click, 3.46 percent CTR against 4.54 and 6.25 CPC against 4.86, and better at converting it, 4.94 percent of clicks became leads against 2.14.

Three caveats, because you should be able to attack this. The unconstrained campaign carried 7.6 times the spend, and budget scale moves cost per result on its own. Those are leads, not purchases, which is a different economic shape from yours. And the constraint label is how that account is structured internally, not something I can show you as a platform level flag.

So I would not present that split as a finding about your account. I would present it as the hypothesis I would want to test first. When targeting is stripped back, the creative and the offer have to do the qualifying the audience used to do, and the split above is at least consistent with that. Seasonality, offer differences between the two campaigns, or the budget gap could explain it just as well, and the account's own data would decide.

On account health, the surface that actually ends accounts is rarely ad copy. Two things worth knowing going in. Ad level review and advertiser standing are separate systems, so clearing an ad does not repair standing, and a clean ad can run on an account already carrying a mark. And the Ad Library hides expired ads outside the UK and EU unless you filter country by country, so the competitor set you can see is survivorship biased. Run those per country filters and a card reading later disabled for not following Advertising Standards is a readable enforcement record on whoever you are benchmarking against.
