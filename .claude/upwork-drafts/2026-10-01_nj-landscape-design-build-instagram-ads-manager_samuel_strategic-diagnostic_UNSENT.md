## PROPOSAL

The Instagram posts that earn the most likes for a landscape design-build firm are rarely the ones that get a $250K backyard signed. Organic reach rewards what designers and out-of-state fans want to save, and the homeowner in your service area with the property and the budget is a small slice of that crowd.

So retargeting engagers can be mostly fans until it's limited to the towns you build in. A lookalike seeded from engagers can copy the fans, while one seeded from past clients copies the buyers, as long as the list reaches the 100 matched people Meta needs.

**Where we'd look first**

1. **Which organic posts get budget.** Saves and shares say more than likes, and Insights shows which towns your reach comes from. We'd test finished-project Reels against process content, with one version that signals project scale, such as where projects typically start. That can raise cost per lead and cut wasted design calls, a tradeoff worth making on purpose.

2. **What Meta is told a good lead is.** A signed project closes months after the click, and Meta only ever sees the form fill. Instant forms that ask town, budget range and timing filter up front. Past about 200 leads a month on instant forms, Meta's conversion leads goal can optimize toward leads your team marks qualified in the CRM, as long as that happens within 28 days.

3. **Placements.** We'd test Instagram-only against all placements and let cost per qualified lead decide.

In the Northeast, fall is usually when spring projects get designed, so the first test is worth running before winter.

We haven't run Meta for a landscape or design-build firm, and our landscaping results are on Google.
