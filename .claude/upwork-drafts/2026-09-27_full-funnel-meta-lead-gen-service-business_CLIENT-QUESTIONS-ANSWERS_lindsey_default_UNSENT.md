The local practice's Leads campaign alone logged 124 leads at $15.77 each in August. Our team has also run Meta lead gen for a mortgage company, a law firm, a gutter installer and a children's coaching company.

2. Yes, on the creative direction side: I set the strategy and check everything before launch, while a designer handles execution.
