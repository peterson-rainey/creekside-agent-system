## Job post

We are a growing service business looking for an expert Google Ads specialist to audit our current setup, optimize our campaigns, and manage ongoing local search ads to drive consistent inbound leads/appointments.

## Proposal

**Where local service accounts often leak**
Many local search accounts for service businesses count any form fill or very short call as a lead. Google's bidding then optimizes toward more of whatever that definition rewards, which is often cheap, low-intent contacts instead of booked appointments. An audit that starts with how conversions are defined, before touching bids or keywords, often surfaces the biggest waste.

**What our team would look at first**
Search terms and match types, because service accounts tend to pay for job seekers, DIY researchers, and out-of-area clicks. Call tracking with a sensible duration threshold (often 60 to 90 seconds, adjusted to the business), and whether calls and forms get imported back to Google from your CRM or call tracking with a real appointment or closed-job status. Whether Local Services Ads run alongside Search, since they capture overlapping intent on a separate budget and the two need to be read together so the same searcher isn't being paid for through both. Zip-level geography, ad schedules matched to when phones are actually answered, and whether Performance Max is taking over branded search or other local-intent queries that Search should own.

**Tradeoffs to know about**
Booked work depends on more than the ads. Speed to answer and follow-up decide how much paid traffic turns into revenue, so we'd want to see that early. Some cleanup changes, like conversion or bid strategy edits, reset part of Google's learning. Expect a dip for a few weeks and then stabilization, not a straight line up.

**What would help us scope this**
Which service lines pay best per job, and which ones do you want more of? Is monthly spend today near $5K, well under it, or well above it? That range matters because at lower spend there is often too little data for confident optimization calls. What's your rough monthly revenue, and what would you like it to look like in 6 to 12 months? And does anyone currently track which leads become paying jobs?

**How we work**
The audit ends with a written list of findings ranked by impact, so you can see what to fix first. Ongoing management is priced as a percentage of ad spend, so the fee scales with the account. Reporting goes out every two weeks, and there's a live dashboard in between.

Once we know the setup, we can talk through what the audit would cover and what the first 30 days look like.
