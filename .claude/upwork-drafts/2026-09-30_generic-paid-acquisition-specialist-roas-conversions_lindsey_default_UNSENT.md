When a platform reports conversions dropping, how do you check whether customers stopped coming or just stopped showing up in the report?

**What gets reported is not always what arrived**

In August a dental client told me leads had fallen off over half a week and Meta had sent one request. I could see over 50 people had filled out forms. They were sitting in the CRM as contacts, never reaching the pipeline, so I pointed the client to where the leads were in Business Manager. Nothing in the ads needed changing that week. Cutting campaigns off the reported number would have cut the ones working.

**The weakest-looking number can belong to the best campaign**

A meal-prep brand I manage runs a small campaign on about $40 a month next to $1,340 on its main sales campaign, so it is not a clean test. The small one holds more than double the click-through rate. The sales campaign, at half a percent, produced every tracked purchase across June and July at roughly $5 each. Judged on clicks, I'd have paused the one paying the bills.

**How I'd start**

First I confirm which event each campaign optimizes for and where a conversion lands after the click. Then I read results by prospecting and retargeting separately. On a mattress account the client wanted prospecting off in favor of retargeting, but the retargeting pool was still building and prospecting was driving sales, so I kept it running and planned to shift budget as traffic grows. Every recommendation I send comes with the number behind it. I've done this for ten years, and I built and sold my own e-commerce business, so I read an account the way an owner does.
