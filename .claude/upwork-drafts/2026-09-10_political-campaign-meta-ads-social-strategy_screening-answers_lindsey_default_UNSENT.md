What I run is Meta lead capture at volume, which is the same mechanic underneath a volunteer list, an event RSVP or a pledge card. Most recently a dental group I managed took 2,558 lead form submissions at $25.31 each over about three months, and we pulled spend back to match what their call center could handle, not because cost rose. Our team also runs a registration-driven account where one signup campaign produces nearly all the conversions at a steady cost while most of the budget sits in top of funnel campaigns recording none. Finding that split is usually where the first real money is, and it matters more on a campaign with a fixed end date than it does for a business that can keep testing next quarter.

**2. Please list any certifications related to this project**

None. I do not hold a Meta Blueprint certificate and I would rather tell you that than pad the answer.

Worth knowing for this category specifically: the credential that actually governs political advertising is not a freelancer certificate. It is advertiser authorization and ID verification, and it attaches to your campaign entity and page, not to the person running the ads. Whoever is named on the "Paid for by" disclaimer has to complete it, and nothing can run until they do. If that is not already finished on your side, it is the single thing most likely to delay a launch, and it is worth starting this week regardless of who you hire.
