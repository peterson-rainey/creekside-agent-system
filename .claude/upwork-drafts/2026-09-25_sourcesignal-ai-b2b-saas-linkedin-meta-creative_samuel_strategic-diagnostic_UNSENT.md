SourceSignal's free plan, no card and no time limit, is the part of this launch that will decide what LinkedIn and Meta learn. If the campaigns optimize to "created a free account," both platforms will get very good at finding people who like free tools, and a £149 to £1,199 subscription needs a different person than that.

The fix is picking a signup event that predicts payment. For a product like yours that is probably something like tracking a first set of prompts, adding a competitor, or connecting an outreach inbox. Send that back as a server-side conversion and optimize toward it once there is enough volume. We deal with this on our own money: our team runs paid acquisition for our own AI product, a per-seat subscription with a free trial, and cheap signups that never convert are always the first thing to show up.

**Three concepts I would test first**

The step after the dashboard. Most AI visibility tools stop at telling you that you are losing. Yours finds the source the answer leaned on and drafts the pitch to the person behind it. That gap is the ad: a lost recommendation, then the ranked action queue with the pitch ready to approve.

One prompt, tracked in public. A short-form series that follows a single buyer question week to week: the answer, the source it cited, the change made, the answer after. It shows the month-over-month loop your whole pitch rests on, and it gives you episodes instead of one-off ads.

Why the answer said that. A 30 to 45 second breakdown of one real citation, like why ChatGPT leaned on a G2 page or a Reddit thread. It teaches before it asks, which is what earns a click from SEO and content leads on LinkedIn.

**What backs this, and what does not**

The closest case study we have is attached: a seed-funded B2B software company, paid acquisition by our team, inbound leads and ARR doubled within six months.

LinkedIn Ads is not a platform our team has run for clients. Meta is where our book sits. That is a real gap and worth weighing. I would run LinkedIn as a capped test judged on cost per activated account, not cost per signup, and let that number decide its share.

**Before I would plan anything**

Is SourceSignal open for self-serve signups now, or still invite-only? That decides whether the first ads fill a waitlist or an account.

Of the free accounts so far, roughly how many have moved to a paid plan?

Are you thinking under £4,000 a month across both platforms, or well above it? And UK buyers only, or the US as well?

Answer those and I can tell you which event I would optimize toward on day one.

**4. Screen recordings and tools**

Yes. For a product like this, screen recordings are usually the best raw material, because the product doing its job on screen is more believable than any motion graphic. Loom footage works fine as a source. Most of the work is reframing to 9:16 and 4:5, adding captions since many people watch with the sound off, and cutting so the first three seconds carry the point.

Statics and quick cut-downs are built in Canva, ChatGPT helps draft script and caption variants, and full video edits go through our creative director. We have not leaned on AI video generators like Runway or HeyGen for SaaS work. Synthetic footage undercuts a product whose whole pitch is showing real answers.
