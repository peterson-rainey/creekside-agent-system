The follower counts and the monthly listener numbers are what most people would build this launch around, and they are what I would set aside for month one. The question that decides more: how many purchases has LegendsOfMusic.com recorded so far, and are they landing in a pixel?

The reason I ask is that Meta learns from purchases, not from fandom. Audience size does not tell the algorithm who will pay $145 for an archival print, and until purchase events are flowing back, spend is buying guesses.

A consumer camera brand I ran on Meta is the closest thing in my book to your price point, a $170 product started from zero spend. Over four and a half months it spent $58,298 and produced 623 purchases at $93.58 each, with just under 1% of clicks turning into a sale. Month one came in around $120 a purchase, settled to $69-73 as monthly spend crossed $11-12K, then went back to roughly $120 when spend hit $20K. Cost did not keep improving as it scaled; it got worse again.

At $290 a bundle there is room to absorb a three-figure acquisition cost. At $145 there is not, for long, which is why your 3 for 2 offer is unit economics rather than a promotion. So the first 30 days point every campaign at purchase, stay broad instead of splitting by band, and test angles rather than audiences. Twenty groups cut into twenty ad sets is how a launch like this runs out of budget before it learns anything.

Two gaps worth naming now rather than later. Fine art and print publishing are not in my account history, and I run Meta and email, not YouTube, so that half of the scope needs someone else on it.

On creative, I write the copy and build the ads from the photos and story material you supply. I do not shoot new work.

The Meta work is mine, on the account daily through the first month while the pixel is learning, then a weekly rhythm after that. I work with a tracking specialist on the pixel and analytics setup.

Is the ad budget closer to $3,000 a month or $10,000? That decides which of the twenty groups is worth launching with at all.
