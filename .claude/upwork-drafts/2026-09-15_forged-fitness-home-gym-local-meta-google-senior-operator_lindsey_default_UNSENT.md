## PROPOSAL (paste-ready)

FORGED.

Have you run the math on your own kicker? At about $14K a month for 5 held calls a week, you're near $650 per held call. Ten a week under $450 caps spend around $19.5K, so cost per held call has to fall about 30% while spend rises almost 40%. More budget can't do both. What Meta is told to find has to change.

I've run Meta for 10+ years, including dental practices and service businesses feeding a call team, and the first thing I'd dig into is the unsourced revenue. GoHighLevel forms load in an iframe that GTM can't read, so unless the click ID is captured inside the form and synced back from the CRM, a sale arrives with no source.

1. For a dental practice, I ran an engagement campaign next to a leads campaign last month on nearly the same budget. $1,846 recorded zero enquiries. $1,955 produced 124 leads at $15.77. Same account, same month, different objective. For an implant group feeding a call center, I spent $64,747 over about three months for 2,558 leads at $25.31. I added qualifying questions to the forms, accepted a higher cost per lead for leads the team could book, and held spend to what they could dial. Those are side-by-side and in-flight numbers, not a clean before and after, and that group has since brought it in house.

2. Meta runs as two campaigns, both locked to your 30-mile radius. Scaling gets about 75% and tests proven ads. Testing gets about 25% for your 3 weekly concepts. Retargeting folds into scaling, because at around 22 held calls a month every extra ad set starves the rest. Both optimize on a booked call sent from your CRM through CAPI, which happens more often than a held call and means more than a form fill. Test concepts get cut early on cost per qualified lead, since a handful of bookings per concept isn't a signal. Held calls and closed revenue come back as downstream events, so budget moves between channels on cost per held call.

3. Our Google team would launch these three, with tracking built by our dedicated conversion tracking specialist:
- Home gym design and installation terms, shown only to people actually inside your radius.
- Basement and garage gym remodel terms, with negatives on DIY, used and cheap, because that's someone planning the room, not shopping for a bench.
- Brand, as cheap insurance against competitors bidding your name once Meta starts creating search demand.
Those go first because a 30-mile radius has thin search volume, so the highest intent gets the budget before anything broad.

On fees, we bill as a percentage of spend, not hours. Your $3,000 build matches our onboarding for two platforms, and management at today's spend runs about $4,300 a month.
