**3. Tell us about an ad account that got restricted or banned. What happened and what did you do?**

I don't have a restriction and recovery I can walk you through in detail, so here's how I keep regulated offers out of that position. Meta says it can consider an advertiser's compliance history when deciding what gets extra review, and live ads can be reviewed again. So every rejection gets logged with the claim that triggered it before anything is re-uploaded, and income or return claims get checked before an editor ships them. I scale through budget, which doesn't trigger a new review, rather than editing creative, targeting, the optimization event or the billing event on live ads, which does. And every ad account and page has more than one admin, because a restricted profile can take down accounts where it's the only user.

**4. Your cost per lead is cheap, but almost nobody books a call. What do you check first?**

Whether those leads are real, and whether anyone reaches them while they still care. I check for duplicate or junk submissions, how fast the first call, text or email goes out, and whether the booking step sits right on the thank-you page. Then I split bookings by ad, because cheap leads that don't book often trace back to a hook promising something the call doesn't deliver. Then I look at what Meta is optimizing for. If it's an opt-in, it gets very good at finding people who opt in, so once there's enough volume I'd send booked calls back through CAPI as the event to optimize on.
