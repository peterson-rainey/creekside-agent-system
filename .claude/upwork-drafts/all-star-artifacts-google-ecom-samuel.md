The first number that will lie to you on Google is branded search. You already spend most of your budget on Meta, so a parent who sees your ad on Instagram and later Googles "All Star Artifacts" gets counted as a Google sale. If branded and non-branded sit in the same campaign, the test looks great and tells you nothing about whether Google can find new customers on its own.

So the first thing we'd do is split them. Branded gets its own small campaign and its own line in the report. The profitability call gets made on non-branded Search and Shopping only.

Here is how the first 30 days would look:

Week 1: tracking and feed. Purchase conversion with actual order value (not a flat number), enhanced conversions, and a check that Google and Shopify agree on order count before a dollar is spent. Merchant Center setup matters more than usual for you, because personalized products get flagged or disapproved when the feed shows a generic image and the landing page asks for an upload. We'd build the feed around sport and product type so Shopping can be split by what actually sells.

Week 2: launch. Search on high-intent terms like "custom football cards," "personalized sports trading cards," and "custom baseball cards for team," plus a Shopping campaign. Tight match types, a starter negative list built before launch (free templates, printable, card grading, official NFL cards, etc).

Weeks 3-4: search terms reviewed at least twice a week. Collectibles searches drift hard into hobby and resale traffic, and that's where small test budgets usually disappear. Then a first read on non-branded CPA versus your break-even CPA.

Reporting shows actual sales, CPA and profit per sport, not clicks and impressions. It's also worth planning around the season: team orders spike at season start and end-of-season banquets, so we'd want budget room ready for those windows rather than a flat daily spend.

On Performance Max, we agree with holding it back. PMax will happily spend on your branded traffic and remarketing your Meta visitors, which makes it look like it's working when it's mostly taking credit. It earns a spot once Search and Shopping have a clean non-branded baseline to compare against.

The closest account we run is Aura Displays, a Shopify brand we built on Google from zero with Search, Shopping and PMax. The honest shape of it: branded search runs around 27x ROAS, non-branded settled around 4-5x as we scaled cold spend roughly 4x. And when we tested PMax against Shopping head to head, Shopping won (5.47x vs 5.29x), so PMax was paused. That's exactly why we'd rather prove Search and Shopping first here. Case study attached.
