1. Describe what makes you a specialist in Google Ads

Google Search lead generation is a core part of what our team runs, and most of what separates good work from average is unglamorous. Search terms get read against the enquiries that actually became sales conversations, not just against the keyword list. Bid strategy has to match the conversion volume the account really has, since Target CPA on a handful of leads a month tends to choke delivery. Conversion tracking is treated as part of the account, which is why a dedicated conversion tracking specialist on our team owns it, including importing qualified leads back into Google when your CRM can store the click ID. And in B2B we judge campaigns on cost per qualified lead rather than cost per click, since the cheapest clicks are often the most expensive leads.

2. Describe your recent experience with similar projects

The closest recent match is the audit mentioned in my cover letter, done by our team last month for an industrial equipment manufacturer across its Google Ads and Microsoft Ads accounts on read-only access.

On ongoing management, our team runs Google Search for a commercial paving and striping company that sells to property managers and general contractors. Its main campaign has spent about $40,600 since last November for 213 conversions at around $191 each, with clicks averaging over $17. On the smaller end, we run Search for a single-location clinic at under $2,000 a month, which brought in 575 conversions over the last year at around $40 each.
