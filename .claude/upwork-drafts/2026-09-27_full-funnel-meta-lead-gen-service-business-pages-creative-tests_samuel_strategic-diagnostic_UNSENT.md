## PROPOSAL (paste below this line)

A/B testing ad creative for a service business is mostly a volume problem, so I'd start with a few big differences. Meta usually needs about 50 results a week per ad set just to leave learning, and if a lead costs $60, a $5,000 month buys about 20 a week.

**What I'd test instead**

One prospecting ad set, with big variants like a different offer or a different customer. Small differences between two ads won't show up cleanly at 20 leads a week. A new ad added to an ad set counts as a significant edit, so the ad set reenters learning, and I'd launch the variants together. I'd judge the ads on qualified leads, not clicks. That needs you to tell us which leads were qualified.

**Where lead quality gets decided**

What the campaign optimizes for, and whether the tracking fires on the real lead or on something shallower, because Meta delivers toward the event it is given, not necessarily toward customers. What the page asks, since a question about the job can cost some leads and screen out poor fits. And how fast someone calls back, because a lead nobody phones until tomorrow has often gone cold.

**Full funnel, and the page**

On a $5,000 month, full funnel would mean one prospecting campaign feeding the page, with retargeting added once enough people visit. A landing page specialist on our team would build pages to match the ads. We have no before-and-after conversion rate from a client page to show. I'd judge the page on cost per qualified lead.

**Closest examples**

For one dental implant practice our team managed, the call center couldn't handle the lead inflow, so Meta spend was pulled back and general dentistry campaigns were paused, with implant consultations as the priority. Separately, the attached one-pager shows a local dental practice's August Meta campaign set to engagement taking about half the spend and recording no leads.

What is the service? And what will you spend on Meta each month, closer to $3,000 or $10,000? That tells me how many ads the account can test at once.
