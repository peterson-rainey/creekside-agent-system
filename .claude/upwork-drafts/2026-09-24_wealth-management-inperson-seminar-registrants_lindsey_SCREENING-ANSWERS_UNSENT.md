1. Describe your recent experience with similar projects

The closest recent work is in financial services. I ran Meta for a reverse mortgage lender where the entire engagement was built around lead quality rather than lead count. We moved campaign optimization off the raw form submission and onto a pre qualification event, so Meta was buying people who answered the qualifying questions the right way instead of people who were quickest to submit anything. The first nine days of that switch cost about $2,100 and recorded a single conversion. The thirty active days after that came in near $107 across 66, and the seventy after that near $93 across 179. Two honest caveats: that account under records conversions, and the event being counted changed partway through, so I would treat those as directional rather than as a clean before and after. That account ended in August when they took the work in house. I currently run Meta for another mortgage lender on a smaller budget and a different brief.

The other half that applies here is tight audience work. Several of my accounts are local practices selling a high consideration appointment inside a small radius, where the audience is too small to brute force and creative fatigue shows up fast. Small in person seminars behave the same way. You are buying a few hundred genuinely qualified people in one metro, not reach.

Where I am thinner: my recent work has been application and appointment style funnels rather than a recurring in person seminar calendar. The registration mechanics are the same, and the seat count is the constraint I would plan the budget around first.
