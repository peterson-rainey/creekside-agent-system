## PROPOSAL (final, attach Big_Chad_Law_Legal_Case_Study_(1).pdf)

Company formation runs into Google's government documents policy, and Portugal's NIF likely does too. Only governments and their authorized providers may run ads for company registration numbers or personal ID numbers (Spain's NIF is one of Google's examples), and from October 5 even an authorized provider's site must promote only that service, with ads aimed only at the authorizing government's country, which no US campaign can meet.

So the ads and landing pages would sell your advice and visa support, with company formation and the NIF inside the service, not advertised. D7 and Digital Nomad visas aren't named in Google's list, and if a review flags the ads anyway, advertisers who don't sell government documents can apply for an exclusion. It needs advertiser verification, which we'd start before launch.

D7 and Digital Nomad get separate campaigns, so budget follows whichever pays back, not whichever brings cheaper leads.

Payback lives in the CRM, but Google only imports an offline sale uploaded within 90 days of the click, 63 for enhanced conversions for leads. If clients usually sign within weeks, the signed contract goes back to Google. If it's closer to three months, Google bids on a consultation your team marks qualified, and signed contracts drive ROI reporting by source. Our dedicated conversion tracking specialist builds that across GA4, GTM and the CRM import. Performance Max waits for that signal, since it optimizes toward whatever conversion it's given.

I've been in paid ads for six years, and nearly all our Google Ads clients are US businesses, a dozen live now. No immigration or relocation client yet; the closest two are legal and financial. A US reverse mortgage lender's Search campaigns spent $124,197 from late September 2025 to early August 2026, 1,277 conversions at $97.24 each. An Arizona personal injury firm, April to September 2025: 102 cases on $96,985 of total ad spend across channels, $950.83 per case, with Google at $56.6K for 619 conversions, about $91 each. Its case study is attached.

Our rate is a percentage of ad spend, not hourly. That's 20% of monthly spend up to $30K, 15% on the part from $30K to $60K and 10% above, with a $1,500 monthly minimum and a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee, each per platform. Onboarding covers the audit and build. At $10K monthly spend, the fee is $2,000.
