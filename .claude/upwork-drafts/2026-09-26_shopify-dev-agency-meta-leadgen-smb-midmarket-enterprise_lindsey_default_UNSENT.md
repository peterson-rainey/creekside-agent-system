## PROPOSAL (paste below this line)

Quick question before anything else: how many discovery calls a week can your team take, and how fast does a new lead hear back? I ask because past that point, more Facebook spend doesn't buy more projects. It buys leads nobody reaches in time, and those show up in the report as bad leads.

**The account where the ads weren't the bottleneck**

I ran Meta for a dental implant practice where a full-mouth case ran upper $30Ks. Leads came in faster than the call center could work them, so the budget came down to what it could handle. I checked the practice's CRM for who actually booked a consultation and kept one campaign running even though its leads cost more, because they were booking. Your discovery calendar plays the call center's role, so I'd size spend to it from week one.

**What I can and can't show you**

Development agencies aren't in my book, but the kind of stores you sell to are. I built and sold my own e-commerce business, and I've run Meta for stores on Shopify, Magento and WooCommerce. A Magento parts store I took over was running 232 ads across 11 campaigns. February overlapped old campaigns with new ones, and from March it ran on seven. Cost per conversion was $34.72 the month before I started, then $32.00, $25.30, $19.89 and $22.91 from February to May, with CPM flat near $8. A lot changed in that account over those months, so I can't pin the drop on one lever.

When a bigger brand reaches out today, who takes that first call, and is it the same person who takes the smaller ones? If it is, that one calendar decides how fast the ads can scale.
