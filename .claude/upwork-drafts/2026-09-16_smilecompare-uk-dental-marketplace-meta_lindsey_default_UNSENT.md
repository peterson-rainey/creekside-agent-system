# SmileCompare (UK dental marketplace) - Lindsey lindsey_default, Meta-only scope (UNSENT)

Quick question before anything else: when a practice owner fills in a form, who calls them, and how fast? The reason I ask is that on a two-sided marketplace the patient side usually produces cheap signups early, but the practice side depends on the handoff to sales, and Meta can only optimise toward the events you send back to it.

I've seen that play out on dental accounts. From 21 April to 15 July 2026 I worked on Meta for a US implant practice, alongside one other team member, where lead forms fed a call centre: $64,746 spend, 2,558 leads, $25.31 cost per lead, measured on the standard lead event. We added qualifying questions that raised cost per lead on purpose to improve quality, and pulled spend back when the call centre couldn't keep up. That capacity check is the first thing I'd do with your sales team.

Separately, I currently run a cosmetic dental account spending close to $100K a month on Meta, so claims review and approvals in dental advertising are routine for me.

What I'd own: Pixel and conversion event checks, separate patient and practice campaigns, two to three new creatives a week against clear pause and scale thresholds, and weekly reporting on spend, qualified signups and practices fully onboarded. First ads live in week one once access and tracking are confirmed. I don't produce organic content or community management, so that part would need to sit with someone else.

Fee: $1,500 a month management plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee, no other extras.

On budget: is GBP 2,000 to 2,500 where spend stays, or would it move up once you know your cost per onboarded practice? Split across two funnels, each side gets roughly GBP 1,100 a month, which slows how fast either one learns.
