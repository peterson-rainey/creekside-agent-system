Split the flagged ads before any rewrite: the ones that never get approved and the ones taken down a week later point to different fixes. Google reviews the ad, its keywords, the landing page and any images or video, and any edit to the ad or its assets restarts that review. An ad that never clears carries the claim somewhere that first review catches.

Two things in the unreliable claims policy are worth checking first: any testimonial naming a specific result needs a visible disclaimer that results aren't guaranteed and can vary, and any guarantee needs an easy-to-find refund policy. Beyond those, the line is presenting an unlikely result as the likely outcome, even when it's possible.

Our team hasn't run an account in the opportunity niche, so there are no niche results to show you. Everything we run stays in your own accounts, with nothing hidden from review. What we have done is take a disapproval on a health policy through a Google support case and get the ads reviewed and approved. Separately, a mortgage lender's Google account we ran passed $60K in a single month.

In practice, we'd line every disapproval up against change history and write the exact edits for each flagged ad, video and page. Your team makes the changes, and edited ads re-enter review on their own. We only dispute an unchanged ad when the flag is clearly wrong, since each ad gets three appeals, and after three failed ones it needs Google support before it can be appealed again. Some hooks lose punch once an income figure comes out.

Under unreliable claims, Google sends a warning at least seven days before any suspension, so if one has landed, that comes first. Google's August update also extends limited ad serving to all Google Ads, rolling out through 2028. That limits scale in a way approval doesn't fix: it can hold back impressions for advertisers Google doesn't yet treat as qualified, without disapproving a single ad, and it weighs things like user reports, industry and verification status.

Stage 1 is $1,500 USD fixed: the audit, the written edit list, and one round of resubmissions and appeals for each ad disapproved when we start. After that, management is a percentage of spend with a $1,500 monthly minimum.
