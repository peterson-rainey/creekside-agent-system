## PROPOSAL

Do you know your gross profit per lead broken out by publisher placement, or only your cost per lead? Those two come apart fast on native, and the second one is what actually protects a margin floor.

Straight answer on your requirement first: no spend on any of the five platforms you named, and no hands-on time in Voluum, RedTrack or Everflow. If in-platform native history is the bar, that is a miss.

What I do have is ten years of buying cold traffic where a lead and a sellable lead are not the same thing. On a dental implant account I ran, cold lead forms into a call team produced 2,558 leads at $25.31 each on roughly $64,700 in spend across three months. The cost line was never the hard part. The hard part was working out which ad sets fed leads the call team could actually reach and book, then cutting the cheapest-looking ones. Mid-article traffic makes that sharper rather than different, because it arrives with no intent signal attached.

Which is also why I would not assume your existing quiz carries over. Search and social traffic shows up already leaning in. Native traffic does not, so the page has to earn attention before it qualifies anyone. Two different builds, and I would run them as separate pages rather than porting one. Same reasoning on creative: what works in placements like these reads like the article around it, and the headline does the qualifying before anyone clicks, which keeps junk out at the top rather than at the call center.

On geography, I took a meal prep brand out of South Dallas into Frisco, Plano and Denton one market at a time. That is the discipline a ZIP or DMA rollout needs. I currently run a dental account at roughly $100,000 a month, so budgets at your scale are familiar.

Everything above comes from accounts I ran personally and I am glad to walk through any of them. There is a short video on my profile that covers how I work through accounts like this.
