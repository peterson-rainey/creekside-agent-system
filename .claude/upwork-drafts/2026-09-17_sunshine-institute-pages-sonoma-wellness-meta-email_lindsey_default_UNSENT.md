When the founder visits a practitioner in person, does that visit get logged in Zoho CRM right away, or in a batch later? I ask because it decides whether your upgrade emails back up those conversations or step on them. Zoho's scheduled sync to Campaigns runs once a day at most, so a visit logged after that run stays invisible until the next one, and a provider can get an automated "upgrade your listing" email the morning after sitting down with the founder. Immediate sync updates in five to ten minutes, but it can't filter which records come across, so the hold-out rule belongs in your Campaigns segments.

It's the same check I make whenever I add a new flow in Klaviyo: does it respect the exclusions already set on the other flows? When it doesn't, one person gets two messages for the same moment. My email builds are all Klaviyo, so Zoho would be new to me, though the logic doesn't change.

On scope, I run Meta ads and email. Organic content and Reels, Nextdoor, local SEO and Google Business Profile work aren't services I offer. On my side the pilot would cover the paid plan and the sample email, and the posting calendar and social samples would need a different hire, possibly from another role you've posted.

Keeping things personal is where I've seen paid campaigns slip. On a dental practice I run Facebook ads for, message ads sent the owner more conversations than was manageable, and I suggested adding qualifying questions up front. On that same account I held the budget steady until leads were showing up in their CRM pipeline, and only raised it after. For the directory and the Institute, I'd follow that order: claim, upgrade and booking tracking confirmed first, then spend sized to the follow-up the founder can personally give.
