# Multiply ME, Australian SaaS, Meta/Facebook Ads & Growth Marketer: SKIP

Date: 2026-09-29.
Decision: Queenie picked **"Skip, report gap"**. Not sent.

## Screen

FULL-TIME-EMPLOYMENT-SEAT auto-DQ: the posting is a hire-an-employee listing, not a client seeking an
agency retainer. Signals, quoted directly from the post:

- "We're putting the feelers out for a great marketer to potentially join our team at Multiply ME."
- "This position could start part-time or full-time depending on the person."
- "For the right person, this isn't necessarily just another freelance marketing job."
- "There is a genuine opportunity for this position to grow significantly as Multiply ME grows --
  potentially into a senior marketing or CMO position. There will also be opportunities for
  performance-based bonuses as the role develops."
- "You'll have direct access to the Director and work closely together, including a weekly marketing
  management meeting."

The posting uses "full time" directly, which fires the standing auto-DQ on its own. Beyond the trigger
word, the whole shape of the ask -- embedded team member, career-path growth into a CMO seat, discretionary
bonuses tied to role development, "not just another freelance job" stated explicitly -- is a job placement,
not a % of ad spend agency engagement. It doesn't fit Creekside's retainer model (outbound = "our team,"
principals never do delivery work, billing is % of spend not salary/bonus) no matter how the pitch is
framed.

Queenie was offered an agency-reframe override ("access a full team instead of one hire") and a literal
apply-as-the-role override; she chose to skip and have the gap reported instead of drafting either version.

## Reference if this type of posting reopens

Ad spend ($10k/mo scaling to $20k/mo over 12 months) and geography (Australian company, English-speaking)
would have cleared the standing spend and geo screens on their own -- the sole disqualifier here is the
employment-seat shape of the ask. If a materially different Multiply ME posting appears later structured as
a retainer/contractor engagement (no "join the team"/career-path/bonus language), it would need a fresh
screen rather than reopening this one.
