## PROPOSAL (v3 final, safe to send alongside Samuel v4)

How many new integration projects can your team take on in a month, and roughly how many intro calls does it take to win one? Multiply them and you have the number of booked calls the ads need to deliver each month. Fall short and the pipeline stalls. Overshoot and the calendar fills with calls nobody has time to do well, while good prospects wait to hear back.

On a dental implant account I ran on Meta, cases sold in the upper $30Ks. At one point the limit wasn't the ads, it was how much the call center could handle, so I brought spend down to match. The lead forms also asked qualifying questions, so every lead came with some screening up front. And I checked the practice's Salesforce for who booked a consultation, and kept one campaign running even though its leads cost more, because they were booking.

The other half of my work is email, which matters here because not everyone who asks about automation is ready to start. A short sequence built from your own client examples keeps you in their inbox, so you're the first call when they're ready.

I built and sold my own e-commerce business, and 10+ years in, I still treat a client's budget like my own.

There's a short video on my profile that shows how I work, and I'm happy to talk through the dental account on a call.
