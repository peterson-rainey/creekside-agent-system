## Draft

When a student signs up for your lash training, where are they usually coming from: Chilliwack itself, or further out in Abbotsford, Langley and the rest of the Lower Mainland?

I ask because that answer shapes the whole ad setup. Extension clients book within a short drive, so their campaigns stay tight around Chilliwack. Students will travel for a course, and a radius built for clients can hide the program from the people most likely to buy it. Those should be two separate campaigns with their own creative and their own goal.

To be clear on fit: I run the paid Meta side. I don't create content, run day-to-day posting, build websites or handle SEO, so I have no website or follower-growth examples to send. On social, I'm the person who turns the Reels your team already makes into bookings and course inquiries.

The goal you give Meta decides what it finds for you. Last month, on a local dental practice I run Meta for, two campaigns ran side by side on almost the same budget. The one set to engagement got nearly twice the impressions and not one recorded enquiry. The one set to leads brought in 124 enquiries at $15.77 each. Different industry, same principle for any business that runs on bookings.

How I would run the first 90 days:
- Days 1 to 30: make sure bookings and training inquiries are actually being counted, launch the client and student campaigns separately, and put spend behind your strongest Reels.
- Days 31 to 60: judge everything on cost per booking and cost per training inquiry, not likes. Retarget people who watched your Reels or visited the site.
- Days 61 to 90: move budget into what books, set monthly targets for both numbers, and hand your web designer the pages where paid visitors drop off before booking.
