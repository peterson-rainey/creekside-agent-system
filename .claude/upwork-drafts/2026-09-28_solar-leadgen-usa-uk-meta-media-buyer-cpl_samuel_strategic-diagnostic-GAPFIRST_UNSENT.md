## PROPOSAL (paste-ready draft — 380 words / 2,244 characters, verified by script; Upwork limit 5,000)

Meta optimizes to whatever event it's fed, and a lead-form completion isn't the same target as a lead your buyers actually accept. If the SMS verification step, and whatever combination of homeownership, utility bill size, and credit eligibility qualifies someone as sellable, doesn't come back to Meta as its own Conversions API event, the algorithm keeps finding people who finish forms rather than people who clear your buyers' scrub. That's usually where CPL drifts even when spend and volume look fine.

Solar sits inside one of the more TCPA-exposed verticals in the US, so the consent language on the ad and the instant form has to match exactly what the SMS follow-up is allowed to claim consent for. Are you already documenting that consent through something like TrustedForm or Jornaya, or is that still being built? Running US and UK together means writing the consent copy twice either way, since the UK works off its own marketing-consent rules rather than the TCPA, not a reworded version of the same disclosure.

**Where our book stands, plainly**

We haven't run solar. The closest shape in our book is a local aesthetics and dental account we still run on Meta, just under $1.2 million in spend over the last twelve months, plus a smaller exterior home-services account on the same platform. Both are homeowner-facing local lead gen with a form-to-call path, which is the mechanic, not the vertical, and both get read at the campaign and creative level rather than one blended number, the same way we'd track acceptance rate here rather than raw CPL alone. We don't have a live solar account to screen-record, but I can walk you through the dental account live in its place if that helps.

**Funnel, team, and the CPL structure**

Either funnel works, yours or one we build. Day to day in Ads Manager sits with our Meta specialist; I'd be on strategy and the buyer relationship. A CPL structure is fine on our end.

What share of delivered leads clears buyer acceptance today, and is the gap mostly a verification-step problem or a targeting one? That number decides whether the first month goes into creative and audience, or into getting the acceptance signal back into the ad account so Meta can optimize toward it directly.
