## DRAFT

Before building anything, I'd want to know one thing: do you expect these blends to earn their money on the first order or on the reorder? Tea is a repeat purchase, and that answer sets what a customer is worth in the first month. If most of the profit comes from the second or third tin, judging day 30 on first-order ROAS will make a good launch look like a failure. It also changes the plan: ads push the first cup, then email and retargeting bring them back.

I've run Meta for Shopify brands for over 10 years, and I built and sold my own e-commerce business before doing this for clients. I haven't run a tea brand, and I'd rather you hear that from me. What carries over is the purchase mechanics. On a consumer camera brand, campaigns optimized to add-to-cart were getting purchases at about $311 each. Campaigns optimized to purchase, same account, same months, came in around $61. Meta optimizes toward the event it can see, so tracking comes first.

How I'd run it:
- Tracking: Pixel and CAPI checked against real Shopify orders before any launch money is spent, with deduplication so purchases aren't counted twice.
- Structure: one purchase-optimized campaign with broad targeting across Canada, testing both blends against each other, plus retargeting of site visitors and your existing customer list. Spreading CAD $5,000 over too many ad sets means none of them get enough purchases for Meta to learn from.
- Creative: three truly different angles (taste, ritual, gifting) rather than versions of one ad, in formats built for Reels and Stories. If Quebec matters to you, French-language ads are worth doing as their own set.
- Reporting: weekly, showing cost per purchase, new vs returning buyers, and which blend and angle is winning.

On a spa parts retailer, I managed about $125K of Meta spend over nine months at just under $29 per purchase. That came from repeated creative rounds, not from the first month.

On terms: our pricing is 20% of ad spend with a $1,500 USD monthly minimum, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee, with a 90-day minimum. The first 30 days on new products is mostly Meta learning and the first creative round, so a day-30 verdict lands before the numbers settle. Better to say that now than at signing.
