**Reporting**
Every two weeks, plus a live dashboard. Spend, what it generated, cost per reservation, what worked, what didn't, next test.

**Thursday challenge, first 30 days**
First question: do reservations run through your own site or a platform like OpenTable or SevenRooms? That decides what we can track.

Week 1 spends almost nothing. We wire the booking confirmation to Meta (or, on a closed platform, tagged links plus a "how did you hear about us" field) and baseline the last four Thursdays' covers.

Budget: about CAD 6,500 prospecting, 1,500 retargeting, and 2,000 held for winners. Dinner and late-night are different crowds, so they get two separate ad sets, with the creative angles running as ads inside each. Four thin cells would never exit learning at this budget. The lookalike seed is past guests if the platform exports 1,000+ contacts, and booking-page visitors plus Instagram engagers if not.

Timing: reservation ads run Sunday to Wednesday, plus light Thursday-morning retargeting for same-day bookers. Thursday afternoon shifts to late-night creative with a door code, so walk-ins get counted.

Days 8 to 14: cut cells running over twice the target cost per booking once they have real volume, and ask the creative team for five variations of the winning hook. Days 15 to 30: release the held budget into winners in roughly 20 percent steps, and judge by Thursday covers against the baseline.

What does an average cover spend on a Thursday? That number sets what a booking is allowed to cost.
