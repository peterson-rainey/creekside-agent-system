Getting more leads and getting better-quality leads usually pull against each other on Google Ads and Meta, because both platforms optimise toward whatever signal you feed them. If that signal is a form fill, they get very good at finding people who fill in forms, and your sales team ends up doing the filtering by hand.

The fix is sending qualified-lead or closed-deal data from your CRM back into both platforms so they learn what sales actually wants. It also changes which numbers you judge them on. On a contractor account our team runs, Performance Max bought clicks at $3.00 against $14.53 on Search over the same months at near-identical spend, but Search delivered leads at $131 versus $268. A report that stopped at CPC would have moved budget the wrong way.
