**What the audit needs to answer before anything gets touched**

Most "audit and optimize" requests start at the keyword level, but the keyword list is rarely where the money actually leaks. Two things decide whether a rebuild works before a single bid gets touched: whether conversions are counted honestly, and whether branded and non-branded search are sitting in the same campaign. A conversion that fires on a page load, a call ring, or a newsletter signup teaches Smart Bidding to go find more of exactly that, cheaply, while the pipeline stays flat. The same bad signal shows up in the ad copy too, since Responsive Search Ads and Ad Strength are graded off that same data, so it is not just bids that get misdirected, it is which headlines Google decides to keep showing. A blended cost-per-conversion hides which half of the account is actually earning new customers and which half is just collecting people who were already coming.

**Where I'd start, then build from there**

First pass is tracking honesty: GA4, GTM, Enhanced Conversions, and Google's own conversion actions reconciled against what actually closes, not against a page load or a form open. Second, branded pulled apart from non-branded so the real acquisition cost is visible instead of averaged away, sized carefully on a smaller account so neither side starves Smart Bidding of the volume it needs to leave the learning phase. Third, the search terms report checked against what converted, not the keyword list, since that's where negatives actually come from. Once that's clean, Search versus Performance Max stops being a default choice and becomes something you test rather than assume, and Display gets evaluated inside that same audit rather than added on top, since it earns its budget on remarketing and awareness, not by inheriting Search's conversion goals. Live numbers from one account we run, trailing 90 days: Search is converting at $195.53, Performance Max at $366.23, same account, same window. PMax can absolutely win in the right setup, but handing it budget on faith instead of evidence is how a campaign built to convert quietly turns into the expensive half of the account.

**Scale and structure**

We're currently running close to $69,000 a month in live Google spend across 11 active accounts, search, shopping, and Performance Max, so this comes from repetition rather than one account's story. We bill as a percentage of ad spend, never hourly, and onboarding is a flat $1,500 one-time fee per platform that includes the audit itself, so the diagnosis isn't a separate decision you make twice.

**What would sharpen this before we start**

Is the budget you're working with closer to $5,000 a month, or well above that, and what can a new customer cost before the math stops working. Does the sale close on the site, or does it close on a call or after a quote, since that decides whether GA4 events alone cover it or we need offline conversion imports feeding back in. And is there real history in the existing account worth keeping, or is part of it better rebuilt than tuned.
