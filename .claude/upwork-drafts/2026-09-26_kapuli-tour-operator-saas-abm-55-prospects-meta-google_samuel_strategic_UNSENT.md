PROPOSAL (paste below this line)

A prospect list built from public research can't go into Google's Customer Match, which only accepts data people gave you directly. HubSpot's rules keep your newsletter from anyone who hasn't opted in, and 55 names is under the 100 Meta needs to build a lookalike. So the 55 work better as the scoreboard than as the targeting.

**Reaching the 55**

Google Search catches operators like them shopping for software that runs multi-day departures with rooming and split payments, no list needed. Meta suggests waiting for several hundred site visitors before retargeting, so Meta follows first visits instead of creating them. The research decides where those first visits come from. The list itself never touches an ad platform.

HubSpot can't tie visits to an imported contact until they fill in a form or click a tracked email. Once one of the 55 subscribes, their record starts collecting visits from that browser, ad clicks included. Meta never says who is inside an audience, so those records are the honest scoreboard.

**ABM and B2B proof**

We haven't run a named-account program. If ABM case studies are the bar, we don't clear it. The nearest work is a B2B SaaS company selling referral software to home-service contractors. Meta introduced a category those owners didn't know existed, and Google caught the searches that followed. In the six months after their seed round, inbound leads and ARR both doubled. The case study is attached.

**One publication**

AdventureTravelNews, from the Adventure Travel Trade Association. Its Member News is operators announcing changes to each other. On September 11 an Antarctic expedition operator announced a replacement ship and said it was reviewing bookings one by one. Moments like that test a booking setup, so the feed doubles as research.

**Rate and availability**

Management is 20% of ad spend, with a $1,500 monthly minimum and a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee, each per platform. On an early budget the minimum is the bill, so we'd start with Google and add Meta once there's traffic to retarget. Availability isn't capped by hours. You'd have a live report to check any day and a review every two weeks. The newsletter and blog read better in your voice, so we'd send the search terms and ad angles that are working for you to write from.

Is your monthly ad budget closer to $2,000 or $6,000? That decides whether Meta belongs in month one.
