PROPOSAL (paste below this line)

Return on investment in an existing Google Ads account usually looks better than it is, because branded search sits inside it. People searching a company's name were most of the way to saying yes already, so those clicks convert cheaply and lift the average over the campaigns meant to find new customers.

An ecommerce account our team ran shows how wide that gap gets. Branded and non-branded Search ran side by side from November 2025 through August 2026, on budgets under 7% apart. Branded returned 28.74x at $16.90 per conversion. Non-branded returned 5.31x at $104.25, roughly six times the cost on searches that never named the brand. Search overall reported 16.47x: true, and no guide to where the next dollar goes.

**What gets split first**

Before any bid changes, the last 90 days get split three ways: branded against non-branded, campaign type against campaign type, and conversion action against conversion action. The third governs the other two. If page views or phone-number taps count as primary conversions, Smart Bidding chases the cheapest of them, and the account improves on paper while sales sit still.

Campaign type hides things too. A paving contractor our team runs had Performance Max buying clicks at $3.00 against $14.53 on Search, over the same four months at nearly the same spend. Search still produced leads at $131 each, against $268 from Performance Max.

Then the number that decides growth: what a new customer costs at today's spend, and whether it holds as spend rises. If it holds, growth is a budget decision. If it climbs, the limit sits in the offer, the search terms or the competition, and more budget won't fix it.

**Where restructuring did the work**

A bankruptcy firm in Orange County went from 117 leads to 229 while cost per lead fell from $86.09 to $50.29, after its campaigns were restructured by market, on about $1,400 more spend than before. That case study is attached.

What does the account spend per month, closer to $5,000 or $20,000? The right structure at one of those numbers is usually wrong at the other.
