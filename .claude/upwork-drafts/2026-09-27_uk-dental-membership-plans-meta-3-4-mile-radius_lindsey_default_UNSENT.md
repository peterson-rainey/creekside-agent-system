## PROPOSAL (paste below this line)

Quick question before anything else: when someone enquires about a plan, who replies, and how soon? I ask because on two of the dental accounts I've worked on Meta for, what held results back was what happened once the enquiries arrived.

**What I've seen on dental accounts**

On a US full-arch implant account (Meta lead forms into a call centre, about $65,000 spent between April and July), we added qualifying questions to the forms and accepted a higher cost per lead, and spend was held to what the call centre could handle.

On another dental account, our Facebook message campaigns brought in enquiries at volume, more than the practice could easily sort, so I sent over a qualification framework. Plan enquiries can arrive the same way.

**What I'd ask for**

Whatever record you have of recent plan enquiries and when each one was answered, because that shows whether there's a gap before any ad spend does. I'd also want to know what the reply says to someone who isn't ready yet, and whether anyone follows up a second time. Reply capacity can be a ceiling too, so I'd raise spend only once replies were keeping pace.

How many plan enquiries a week could the practice answer promptly?

There's a short video on my profile that explains my process better than text.
