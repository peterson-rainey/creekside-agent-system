## PROPOSAL

Google retired Local campaigns in 2022 and folded them into Performance Max. On a new account with no conversion history, Performance Max takes about 3 to 4 weeks to ramp on offline goals like calls and visits, and much of the early spend lands on YouTube and Display instead of people searching for an IV drip today. The fastest bookings come from plain Search campaigns first, with Performance Max added once there's real booking data to learn from.

**Where IV ads get disapproved**

It's rarely the ad text. Google restricts prescription drug terms in ads, landing pages and keywords, so a drip menu that names prescription add-ons (anti-nausea or pain medications, for example) can pull ads down even when the ad itself is clean. Wording that says a drip treats or cures something, like a hangover or a cold, runs into a separate rule on health claims. Before launch I'd review the menu and service pages the ads point to and send traffic to pages that describe benefits and ingredients in general terms. Healthcare disapprovals are something our team has handled before, including a successful appeal under Google's health advertising policy.

**Weeks one through six**

Week one: tracking and Search. Most spa booking tools finish the booking on their own domain, so the completed appointment never reaches Google Ads unless it's connected on purpose. We confirm your booking system passes the confirmation back, set calls from ads to count only past a minimum length, and put GA4 and Google Tag Manager behind both. Without that, Google optimizes toward page visits.

Then Search goes live on high-intent local terms (IV therapy, IV hydration, drip, mobile IV if you offer it) tightly around your address, with call and location assets tied to your Business Profile. Search terms get reviewed daily for the first two weeks, since "IV" also matches jobs, nursing classes and supplies.

Weeks three to six: once bookings are counting, we add service values so Google bids toward your higher-value drips and memberships instead of the cheapest booking, and that's when return on ad spend becomes a real number. Performance Max comes in after that.
