## PROPOSAL (paste-ready)

When one of your HVAC clients closes a system replacement, who marks that sale in GoHighLevel? I ask because on a dental account I manage, Facebook and Google leads both land in one GoHighLevel account, and it can trace a lead from the ad to a booked consultation. Revenue only shows up when someone at the practice enters it. If nobody owns that step, the reporting stops right before the number the owner judges the ads on.

The gaps first. I don't bill hourly. I price per ad account: 20% of monthly spend with a $1,500 minimum per platform, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee per platform. I'm US-based. Google Ads, LSA, SEO, WordPress and video editing aren't mine. Where Google runs next to my Meta work, a Google specialist on our team handles it, and a tracking specialist builds the GoHighLevel workflows. I haven't run a contractor account myself, though our team has run Google search for an asphalt paving company.

Meta is my strongest skill, 10+ years of it. One account I run spends close to $100,000 a month. On a dental implant account, one week's cost per lead rose partly because the forms added qualifying questions, while spend was cut to what the call center could handle. That first dental account taught me the other side: message ads can bury an owner through the automations unless qualifying questions sit in the flow.

For your HVAC setup:
1. Tag Meta ads so utm_source contains fb_ad, which is case-sensitive. Without it, HighLevel won't file the lead as Paid Social. Google leads classify off the click ID.
2. The form submission sends an instant text and email with the booking link and opens a new-lead stage.
3. A booking moves the stage. No booking starts a short follow-up sequence plus a call task.
4. Stage changes go back to Meta through the Conversions API action. Add to Google Ads can't fire on a stage change, so Google gets an offline import off the saved click ID.
5. Retarget visitors and leads who didn't book, excluding anyone booked or sold.

How many of your clients run Meta today, one or two, or most of them?

There's a short video on my profile if you want a better sense of how I work.
