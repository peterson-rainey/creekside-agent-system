Deliverables: 6-month plan; launch campaigns for Sunshine Pages (free listings + upgrade funnel) and the Institute (programs + booking); organic presence; paid structure; Zoho email program; monthly reports.

Pilot milestone: 90-day plan for both brands (strategy, channel mix, sample content calendar, draft email sequence outline, paid recommendation with budget) plus two sample social posts per brand and one sample email.
Budget: happy to discuss structure based on your proposal. Timeline: strategy in 3-4 weeks of kickoff, ongoing management after. Success: plan aligned with human-centric values; campaigns live and tracking within 30 days of site launch; early traction (claims, email engagement, bookings).
Red flags: automation/chatbot-heavy or AI-generated customer interactions; no local/community examples; vanity metrics without conversion accountability; no CRM or email segmentation experience.

Sunshine Pages and the Institute share the same two customers. The practitioner you want claiming a free listing is also your most likely space renter, and the person searching the directory for an acupuncturist is who your workshops are for. So I'd build the ad audiences and Zoho segments around those two people and let each brand set the voice. Split by brand instead, and one massage therapist can get "claim your listing" and "rent our space" in the same week, from two names with one founder behind them.

To be upfront on fit, our team runs Meta and Google ads and builds email programs. Organic posting, the graphics and Reels behind it, Nextdoor and local SEO would need to sit with someone else, maybe through one of your other listings. In the pilot, we'd deliver the paid and email pieces, not the content calendar or sample social posts. Our email builds so far have been in Klaviyo, not Zoho.

Volume decides the ad setup. A Meta ad set usually needs about 50 results in the week after its last significant edit to leave the learning phase, and one county's practitioners are unlikely to claim listings at that pace for long. So provider sign-ups stay with the founder's visits and email, and the ad budget goes to seekers: bookings, or email signups if bookings are too few to learn from.

One thing to check as soon as either site goes live. Meta may put the Institute or the directory in its health and wellness category, which can restrict some mid and lower funnel events or, at the far end, all of the site's event data, leaving goals that don't depend on it, like awareness, engagement and traffic. So tracking for claims, upgrades and bookings goes in before launch with Zoho CRM as the record, and the campaign goal gets set once we see where Meta lands.

Last month, for a local dental practice we work with, our team launched an engagement campaign and a leads campaign the same day, same account. The engagement campaign got nearly twice the impressions and not one recorded lead. The leads campaign brought in 124 at $15.77 each.

What range are you thinking for monthly ad spend across both brands? A 90-day plan built for $2,000 a month looks nothing like one built for $6,000, so I'd rather plan around your real number.
