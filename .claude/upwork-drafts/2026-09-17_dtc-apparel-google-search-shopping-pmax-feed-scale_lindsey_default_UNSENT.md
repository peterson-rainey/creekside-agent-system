DTC Apparel Scaling

When a best-selling style is down to its last two or three sizes, does it stay in the same Shopping and PMax group as your full size runs?

I ask because the leftover sizes keep serving, so the style collects clicks from shoppers whose size is gone, and the sizes that still sell carry the cost.

To be upfront on fit: Google isn't my platform. Our Google specialists run it, a dedicated tracking specialist owns GA4, GTM and Shopify, and I stay close on Meta and creative. We have no apparel Google account with published results, and our closest Google ecom account never passed $9K a month in non-brand spend.

That account is a Shopify consumer electronics store our Google team built from zero and ran through August. Non-brand Shopping returned 5.47x on about $18K over ten months, sliding from about 11x to about 3x along the way. Over the same 69 days, non-brand Shopping returned 5.66x and PMax overall 5.64x, but PMax was never split by brand there. Here we'd pair a PMax brand exclusion list with a separate branded Search campaign.

On my own Meta work, a replacement parts ecom brand held about $15K a month last Q4 while CPMs rose about 45% and blended ROAS fell from 6.5x to 3.2x, even as click-through rate climbed. Having built and sold my own e-commerce business, I'd read those together, and against MER, before cutting anything.

Two feed changes our Google team would start with:
1. Titles led by brand, gender and product type, then fit, material and color, with color, size, gender and age group on every variant and item group ID tying sizes together. Google requires those four for US apparel.
2. Custom labels for margin, season and size-run depth, so broken runs and clearance get their own ROAS targets instead of sharing one with full-price bestsellers.

On fees: $1,500 onboarding for Google, then 20% of monthly spend up to $30K and 15% from $30K to $60K, with a 90-day minimum. At $10K that's $2,000 a month, at $50K it's $9,000.

How is your Shopify catalog set up today: one product per style with colors and sizes as variants, or a separate product for each color?

There's a short video on my profile that explains how I work.
