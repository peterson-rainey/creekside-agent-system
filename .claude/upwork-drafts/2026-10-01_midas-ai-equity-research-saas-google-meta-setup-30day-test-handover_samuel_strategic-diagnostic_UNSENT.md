## PROPOSAL

If sign-ups and demo bookings both count as the same conversion, Google and Meta will learn to chase the cheaper one. Self-serve sign-ups usually come faster and cost less, so the platforms tend to drift toward individual investors and the professional demos thin out without anything looking broken.

The audience plan has a second catch. In the US, Meta's financial products and services category lists investment services, and ads in it lose lookalikes, age, gender and ZIP targeting, audience exclusions, saved audiences and some interests. Customer lists and website retargeting still work. A research platform may or may not be treated as an investment service, so the audit settles that first and the plan still works if Meta says yes: Google carries cold professional demand, and Meta leans on customer lists and retargeting.

**Order of work**

1. **Audit and tracking, week one.** Review both accounts and what fires today. Sign-up and demo booking get built as separate conversions, with enhanced conversions on Google and the Conversions API on Meta. Nothing launches until both are confirmed firing.

2. **Two lanes.** Professional campaigns optimize to demo bookings, self-serve campaigns to sign-ups, each with its own budget so neither can eat the other. Demo bookings will be the scarce event, so if the budget can't produce enough for the platforms to learn from, the professional lane optimizes to the step just before the booking and is still judged on cost per demo.

3. **Audiences.** Customer lists split by product, website retargeting by the pages people viewed, and interest and search-intent targeting on top, within whatever Meta's category allows.

4. **The 30-day test.** A written plan with budgets per lane and the cut and scale rules before launch, a check-in at two weeks, and a results report at day 30 with cost per sign-up and cost per demo by platform and campaign.

5. **Handover.** Written notes on the structure, what to scale, what to cut and how the conversion setup works, so your team can run it from there.
