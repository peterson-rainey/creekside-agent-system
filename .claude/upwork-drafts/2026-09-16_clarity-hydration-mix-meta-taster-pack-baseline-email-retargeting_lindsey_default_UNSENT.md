## PROPOSAL (paste-ready)

Do you know how many of your Taster Pack signups were already on your list or had bought from you before? People who know a brand tend to cost less to sign up and buy sooner, so if they're mixed in, both the $1 baseline and the share who go on to buy can look better than the real cost of a new customer. I'd want that split before calling $5 realistic or not, and if past customers and subscribers aren't excluded from the cold campaigns yet, that's where I'd start.

I've run Meta and email for over 10 years, and built and sold my own e-commerce business. Hydration, supplements and drinks would be new categories for me. The nearest I run are two meal prep brands, both selling something people reorder.

Past the signup, a lot of the climb to 10% happens in email. The first things I check are how soon a new signup hears from you, and that the sequence stops once they order.

Signups who haven't bought are also a warm audience on Meta. On a camera brand I ran, where the ads sold directly instead of collecting signups, about one in every seven sales came from retargeting. So I treat it as a second lane next to the emails, not a replacement for good cold traffic.

Does your email platform see orders as they happen? If it doesn't, both the emails and any retargeting built from that list keep chasing people who already paid.

If you want a feel for how I work, there's a short video on my profile.
