## WHY SKIPPED
Opening sentence: "**We manage marketing for** a boutique luxury custom home builder in the Vail Valley,
Colorado." The poster is not the builder. That sentence alone is a clients-test hit even though the phrase
"our client" never appears anywhere in the post.

My screen initially read this as PASS and was overruled. The reasoning that got overruled, and the corrected
rule, are recorded in the memory file `feedback_skip_whitelabel_agency_jobs.md` under the 2026-09-24 entry.
Short version: every possessive in the post claims the end business's assets as the poster's own ("our primary
business KPI," "our warm CRM pipeline," "our tracking engineer," "our best buyers," "our site"), which reads
in-house. That does not outvote the declarative opening sentence. Borrowed possessives are what an agency
speaking on a client's behalf sounds like.

Supporting structure, consistent with the skip: a separate Google Search specialist, a named tracking engineer
who owns the value architecture, GHL landing pages we advise on but do not build, and a value hierarchy we
"optimize within" but "don't own." Every surface is owned by someone above the seat.

Thesis alignment did NOT rescue it, same as the sixth and fifteenth skips on file. The post's stated philosophy
is Creekside's own positioning nearly verbatim ("qualified consultation calls, not cheap lead volume, not clicks,
not CPL"; "if your plan is get CPL as low as possible, this is the wrong project"; "not evaluated at 30 days").
