When a lead books a new-patient appointment but never shows, where does that person sit in HighLevel right now, and does anything follow up with them?

I ask because that number can't be read in Ads Manager, and the gap between booked and completed is one of the places appointment-based practices quietly lose money.

**What I've seen in practice**

On a dental practice I run Meta for, the client told me leads had dropped to almost nothing. Over 50 people had filled out forms. They were sitting in the CRM as contacts but had never been added to the pipeline, so nobody was calling them. Once the list surfaced, the client's manager called through it and the pipeline sync got fixed. A lead only counts once it lands somewhere a person will act on it.

**Where psychiatry is different**

Meta restricts which conversion events health-related data sources can send back, and it rejects ad copy that asserts or implies something about the reader's mental health. So the real scoreboard has to live in your HighLevel pipeline stages, with only neutral signals going back to Meta, never anything about a condition. Multi-state adds another layer: campaigns split by state and matched to where you actually have provider capacity, so ads never sell appointments you can't book. Nurture should be built around reminders and rescuing missed intakes, with SMS consent handled properly, not just the first text.
