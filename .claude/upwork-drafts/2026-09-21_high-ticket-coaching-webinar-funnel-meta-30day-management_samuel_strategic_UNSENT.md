## PROPOSAL (v6 FINAL, 400 words / 2,223 chars, QC PASS)

On a webinar funnel, Ads Manager only sees part of what an ad produces. Meta's standard attribution window for a click is 7 days at most, so someone who registers days before the webinar, then needs a second call, can buy outside it and never show up against the ad that found them.

So ads get judged on your sales team's records. First week, we'd confirm each registration's UTMs reach the booking tool and CRM, since redirects can strip them. Results then get grouped by registration week, not booking week, so a bad change can't hide behind calls from last week's registrants.

A month of high-ticket sales, split across every ad, is rarely enough to judge one ad on revenue. So each ad is judged on the deepest step with real volume, usually the share of its registrants who book, with prospecting and retargeting scored separately. Your closers' notes do the rest: an angle that books people who can't afford the program can post a great cost per booked call while wasting their calendar.

On testing, adding an ad to a live ad set is the kind of edit that sends it back into learning. Meta's creative test runs new ads inside the live campaign with its learnings retained, but only on Highest volume bidding. On a cost cap, new hooks get their own ad set, and winners move over in batches.

I've been in paid ads for six years, and a senior Meta specialist on our team would run your account day to day. Our team currently runs Meta for a coaching certification company with a recurring webinar, and ran it for a dental implant practice selling full-mouth reconstructions in the upper $30Ks. There, we checked the CRM for who booked a consultation, kept a campaign running at a higher cost per lead because its leads were booking, and cut spend when the call center needed room.

On the 30 days: a hook launched in week two reaches the final summary before its sales can land. We work on a 90-day minimum, then month to month, which lets month one's tests be judged on customers.

What does the account spend a month, closer to $5K or $30K? Ad sets usually exit learning after about 50 results in the week after a significant edit, so that tells me whether Meta can optimize for booked calls or they just keep score.
