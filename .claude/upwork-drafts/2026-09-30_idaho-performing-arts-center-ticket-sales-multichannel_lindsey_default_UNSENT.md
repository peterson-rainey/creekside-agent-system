Does your AudienceView checkout run on its own domain, separate from your main website? That detail often decides whether Meta sees a ticket sale. If the pixel only lives on your site, Meta learns from people who clicked "Buy Tickets," not people who paid, and it optimizes toward browsers. Getting the purchase event, with order value, firing from the checkout or passed back from it is the first thing I'd check.

**What I've seen with ticketed launches**

With this many shows a quarter, the other trap is giving every performance its own campaign. Each ends up with too few sales for Meta to learn from. What holds up is a steady season campaign built on past buyers and lookalikes of them, plus a short push for each show as its date gets close, with the creative doing the show-specific work. I'd test a rehearsal clip against a polished poster early.
