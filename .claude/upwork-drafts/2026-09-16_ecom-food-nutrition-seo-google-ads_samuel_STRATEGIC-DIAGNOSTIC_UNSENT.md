In food and supplement stores, the Google Ads account and the SEO plan usually fight over the same searches without anyone noticing. Branded and top-product terms that already rank organically get paid for again inside Performance Max, and the account reports it as a strong ROAS. So the first thing I'd pull is a split of search terms and product revenue into three groups: brand, products that already rank page one, and products or problems ("high protein snacks," "creatine for women") where you're invisible organically. Paid budget belongs on that third group. The first two mostly need protecting, not buying.

How I'd audit the account
1. Tracking first. GA4, GTM and Ads conversions checked against real Shopify orders, so ROAS isn't counting duplicate purchases or subscription renewals as new sales. Our dedicated tracking specialist runs this part.
2. Brand vs non-brand. Branded search is carved out and reported separately, because a blended ROAS hides whether cold traffic is actually profitable.
3. Feed. Titles, product types and attributes (flavour, size, dietary claims) are where Shopping and PMax win or lose. Supplement listings also need a check for Merchant Center health-claim disapprovals.
4. PMax vs Shopping tested against each other, not assumed. Best sellers with enough volume get their own campaign so they aren't starved.
5. Margin-based targets. A 3x ROAS is great on a bundle and a loss on a low-margin single item, so targets get set by product group.
