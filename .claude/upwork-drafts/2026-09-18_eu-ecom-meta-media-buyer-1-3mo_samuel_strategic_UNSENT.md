With Google already growing on its own, the first thing I'd settle is how Meta gets judged. In a store running both, much of the ROAS Meta reports is the same buyer Google's branded search also claims. Prospecting that excludes past purchasers, measured on new-customer CPA against blended revenue, shows whether Meta is adding customers or just sharing credit for them.

Across European markets the other trap is cost caps. CPMs and conversion rates swing a lot country to country, so one cap set off a blended CPA throttles delivery in the expensive markets and overspends in the cheap ones. I'd keep testing in ABO per market until each country has its own CPA baseline, then move proven ads into CBO with caps set per market.
