**The account you asked about**

A survival gear ecommerce brand, running Google and Meta together. Blended Meta ROAS looked fine at 2.32x, so nobody questioned it. When our team split performance by country over the same six weeks, the US was returning 3.79x and the UK 0.59x. The UK's CPMs were cheaper ($27 vs $41 in the US), but it returned 59 cents per dollar spent, and the blend was hiding that.

With the split on the table, the owner made the call to pause UK spend. Google non-brand ROAS moved from 1.50x in July to 2.61x in August, and roughly 45% of that movement was the UK removal itself, so I wouldn't present it as a pure optimization win. Meta: prospecting, retargeting and catalog kept distinct enough that retargeting isn't claiming sales prospecting created. Creative tests run one variable at a time with a spend threshold decided before launch, and every test gets a short win/loss note so losing ideas aren't retested.
