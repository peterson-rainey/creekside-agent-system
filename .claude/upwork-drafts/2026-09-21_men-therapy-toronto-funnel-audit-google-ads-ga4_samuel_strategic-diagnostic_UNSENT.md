## PROPOSAL

Your public Tag Manager container sends Google Ads a conversion when someone clicks one of your booking buttons, sends the contact form or joins the newsletter. Nothing in it fires when a booking is actually completed, the Book Free Consultation link to Jane doesn't send Google Ads anything, and the conversion linker isn't set to carry the ad click across to Jane or OWL. If those click actions are primary, bidding is learning from button clicks and newsletter signups, not bookings.

Drop-off is hidden for the same reason. Jane's own guide keeps its bookings in a separate GA4 property from your website's, if they're tracked at all, so no single report shows a visitor going from your pages to a finished booking. Website calls aren't counted either: the call conversion tag watches for a link format nothing on your site uses, and the number sits in the footer as plain text. Our tracking specialist would start there, since every other answer depends on it.

Even with that fixed, Google Ads reports cost per consultation at best. Cost per client divides that by the share of consultations that become paid sessions, a rate that lives in your booking records, not the ad account. LTV also depends on the clinician: session fees on your team page run from $80 to $190, and two of your seven clinicians aren't taking new clients. Budget should follow open caseloads, not just cheap consultations.

On compliance, Google treats counseling for addiction, anxiety and depression as sensitive health content, so remarketing lists, Customer Match and lookalike segments can't target your ads, even though your container loads a remarketing tag. CRPO's advertising standard is the other half, and it reaches ads others place on your behalf: no testimonials, no superlatives or suggestion that a practice is unique, no appeals to fear.

Mental health is thin for us: one short engagement in 2025. Healthcare is a bigger part of our book, including a naturopathic practice at around $40 per conversion over the last 12 months. We're US-based, an hour behind Toronto. AEO, social and unpaid search sit outside our lane; our part is measuring what they add to bookings. Reporting runs on a live dashboard, with a monthly note on where budget should move.
