## PROPOSAL (FINAL)

Your /invest quiz already separates a ready-now investor with $75K+ from someone under $25K who's just researching, but your Pixel fires the same Lead event for both, so Meta can't tell them apart. Its only booking signal fires when Calendly opens in a new tab, before anyone picks a time, so unless Calendly or your server sends Meta more, cost per booked call can't come out of Ads Manager yet.

Week one is plumbing: a second event for the answers you count as qualified, Calendly embedded on the page so a finished booking fires its own event, and a confirmation screen that stops saying the call is booked the moment Calendly opens. Campaigns keep optimizing on Lead during the test, since qualified applicants won't hit Meta's roughly 50 a week, and that event passes a yes, not capital or credit answers. Your funnel already sends utm_content with each submission, so with one per ad, the weekly report shows which Reel produced qualified applicants and booked calls. Our tracking specialist owns that setup.

Your three live ads also aren't filed under Meta's Housing category. Meta's housing policy covers real estate and house hunting services and says undeclared ads may be rejected, and competing off-market deal ads run under it. Inside Housing, age, ZIP, lookalike and exclusion targeting are limited or unavailable, so more of the qualifying has to happen in the Reels and the quiz.

The scripts should say out loud who this is for (capital, timeline, the done-for-you model) so people sort themselves before they tap. The same scripts and shot lists work organically, and your existing Reels can run as ads in week one while new ones get shot.

Two to three weeks will show cost per lead and how many applicants clear your bar, but high-ticket calendars fill slowly, so booked calls stay few. I'd set the pass line with you up front on cost per qualified applicant.

Our closest real estate work is a Texas land seller's Meta account our team ran inside the Housing category: 1,136 leads in under two months, from about $15 to over $50 a lead depending on the parcel. We also ran Meta for a reverse mortgage lender at up to about $22K a month, counting a pre-qualified event as the lead rather than every form fill.
