**Where multifamily Google Ads usually leaks**
Apartment Google Ads rarely fail on clicks. They fail because the account optimizes to form fills and calls while the leasing team is measured on tours and signed leases, and nothing connects the two. Once lead status from your CRM or AppFolio/Yardi is sent back to Google as an offline conversion, bidding starts chasing renters who actually tour, not people who wanted a price and disappeared.

Housing is also a restricted category on Google in the US. You lose age, gender, family status and ZIP code targeting, but you keep radius targeting and remarketing. So the structure that tends to hold up is one campaign per community on a tight radius, plus a separate property-name campaign, because listing sites bid on community names and pick up leads your brand already earned.
