## PROPOSAL

When the ads were last live, was the objective set to leads, or to engagement and traffic? That one setting decides whether Meta spends the day finding people who fill in a form or people who tap a post, and a small local budget cannot fund both.

Last month I had a local dental practice running exactly that pair side by side, same account, same audience, launched the same day. The engagement campaign spent $1,846 for nearly twice the impressions and produced not one recorded enquiry. The lead campaign spent $1,955 and produced 124 at $15.77 each.

Phone is where that picture gets harder to read. GA4 and the Pixel will show someone tapping your number, but not whether a conversation happened, which is the job a call tracking number does. I ran a dental implant practice whose Meta lead forms fed a call team, 2,558 leads in 90 days at $25.31. We added qualifying questions to those forms on purpose and accepted a higher cost per lead, because a lead nobody can reach is not cheap. What capped that account was not ad cost, it was how many leads the phone team could work while still warm.

On creative, yes, a couple of simple images and rewritten copy will do the work. Phone photos of real jobs beat stock in a small area because they look like somewhere people recognise. After 10+ years, and an e-commerce business I built and sold before this, the pattern holds: an account only improves once the thing being counted is the thing you actually wanted.
