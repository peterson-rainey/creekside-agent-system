## Draft

Show-up rate is usually the hardest of those three numbers to pin down on a full-arch account, and a no-show there is a lost full-arch case, not a missed cleaning. The lead lands in the marketing CRM, attended consults get recorded in the practice management software, and the two often don't sync cleanly.

That split decides what Google learns from. If only the form fill or call reaches the account, bidding gets good at finding people who fill out forms, and a full-arch candidate looks the same as someone pricing one implant. So before touching keywords, our team checks where a booked consult and a show-up get written down, whether the click ID survives to that record for offline import, and, where a phone call or separate booking tool drops it, whether enhanced conversions for leads can match the patient back from hashed contact details.

"Dental implants" searches pull in single-tooth and price shoppers, while "All-on-4" and "full-arch" searches carry a very different case value. Separate campaigns with their own budgets and landing pages keep the cheaper single-implant leads from absorbing the full-arch budget.

Closest real example: our team ran Meta for a two-location full-arch implant practice in English and Spanish, $64,700 in spend for 2,558 leads at about $25 each over 78 active days. Two decisions there: qualifying questions went onto the lead forms even though they raised the cost per lead, and spend was pulled back to match what their call center could handle. Separately, our team did a one-time audit and buildout of their existing Google account. We also currently run Google Ads for two other dental practices, cosmetic and general.

What we don't have is a documented cost per booked consult or show-up rate from that implant account, or work under pay-per-consult pricing. Our team can report against a cost-per-consult target once booked consults are tracked, which is what a per-consult arrangement with the practice would run on. Our fee is a percentage of ad spend with a $1,500 monthly minimum, not a per-consult rate.

The one-campaign setup or audit is a one-time $1,500. For ongoing management we'd want 90 days, since a lead who books a consult two weeks out and decides on treatment after that barely registers in a single month of data.

For that account, are show-ups recorded somewhere your team can export each week, or only inside the practice software?
