For a franchise buyer, Qualified Opportunity and Client can land months after the click, and Meta's standard attribution only credits an ad for what happens within 7 days of that click. Judge campaigns on those stages in GoHighLevel, but give Meta an earlier stage with enough volume to learn from, like a qualified lead or a booked consultation.

So pick instant form or landing page before launch. Meta only optimizes toward a CRM stage on instant form leads, at 200+ a month, with the stage reached within 28 days. Below that, a HighLevel landing page is the safer start, since you choose which event goes back to Meta.

In HighLevel, a Facebook landing page lead only counts as paid social when utm_source contains fb_ad, case-sensitive. Its Google Ads action doesn't fire on pipeline stage changes, so later stages go to Google as an upload matched on the saved click ID, and Google won't import uploads more than 90 days after the click. A client signing in month four never reaches it.

A liquid capital range and a timeline question on the form can route qualified leads to your calendar. For a dental implant group whose full-mouth cases averaged in the upper $30Ks, our team added qualifying questions, accepted a higher cost per lead, and cut spend when the call center needed room.

On creative, I'd give each reason to buy its own campaign, leaving corporate, investing, or a business alongside a career, and judge each on shows, not cost per lead.

The attached personal injury case study is a different buyer, judged the same way: 102 cases on about $97K of total ad spend, April to September 2025, while Google learned from calls over three minutes, since calls drove the most cases. That account has since ended.

Our conversion tracking specialist owns tracking and the HighLevel setup, platform specialists run the ads, and I stay on strategy.
