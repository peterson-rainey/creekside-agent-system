## PROPOSAL

DIAGNOSTICS

A single ROAS target doesn't hold across a catalog where margin swings this much by product. Break-even ROAS is one divided by margin, so 5x makes money on a scan tool with 40% margin and loses it on one with 15%. The high-ticket gear is also where tracking most often falls short. A workshop buying a A$10,000 unit often calls or asks for a quote first, and if that sale never reaches Google Ads, bidding reads the premium range as weak and moves budget toward cheaper tools that check out online.

So the audit starts with what Google thinks a sale is worth: whether conversion value includes GST and shipping, whether phone and invoiced orders come back in at all, and whether the Shopify app and GTM are both counting purchases. Then brand versus non-brand share, since branded traffic can flatter blended ROAS while non-brand underperforms, and whether Shopping and PMax are competing for the same products. After that, spend by product against margin, search terms pulling DIY car owners toward a cheap code reader, and Merchant Center disapprovals, GTIN gaps and titles. Custom labels for margin band and price band let budget follow profit instead of your category tree.

What we wouldn't touch early: campaigns that are profitable after margin, even if they aren't built the way we'd build them, and any bid strategy with a solid conversion history. Changing bid strategy or restructuring campaigns puts Smart Bidding back into learning, and on a working account that usually costs more than the tidy-up saves. Roughly, days 1 to 30 are tracking and waste, 30 to 60 feed and segmentation, 60 to 90 moving budget by margin.
