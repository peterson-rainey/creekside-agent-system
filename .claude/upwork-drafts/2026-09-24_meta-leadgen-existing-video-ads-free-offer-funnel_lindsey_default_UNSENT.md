Answering your two questions straight.

One I personally managed: a dental implant practice running Meta lead generation for consult requests. The account sat around $3,400 a week in spend and averaged roughly $20 a lead across the whole thing. The piece worth telling you about is that I pulled the Spanish-speaking audience out of the general campaign instead of letting one campaign serve both languages. Over a 28-day stretch that Spanish campaign produced 98 leads at $12.54 each while the English side ran $22.42. Same offer, same market, same creative approach. The gap was audience and language, not creative quality. I left it capped at $50 a day longer than I should have, which is the part I would do differently.

Second question. Before I touch the ad account I want to see what happens after someone opts in. How fast the first follow-up goes out, whether a person or an automation sends it, and what the last 50 opt-ins actually did afterward. Meta optimizes toward whatever event you tell it counts, so if the only thing it can see is the opt-in, it will go find you people who are very good at opting in and nothing else. That is where most lead quality problems start, well before targeting.

Then, in order:

Where the free offer lives. A hosted landing page and a Meta instant form behave differently enough that it changes the campaign build, the tracking setup, and what a realistic cost per lead even looks like. Instant forms fill cheaper and qualify worse by default, and there are ways to bend that back.

Tracking. Pixel and conversions API both firing, deduplicated, with the opt-in event confirmed as the one the campaign is bidding on. I check this before launch rather than after the first bad week.

The three videos. Not to change them. I want to know whether they are three angles at one audience or three different promises, because that decides whether they run together in one ad set or get isolated. I would also ask whether I can pull short cut-downs from the existing footage for the opening seconds, since a 90-second ad lives or dies on what happens before second three.

What a good lead looks like to you in plain terms. Not a form fill. The thing they do two weeks later.

One question back, because it changes the testing plan more than anything else here. Where does the monthly ad budget land, closer to $5K to $15K or well past that, and which country are the ads running in?
