## Draft

Are Meta and Klaviyo both taking credit for the same orders right now? I ask because on the Shopify brands where I run both, adding the two dashboards together almost always comes out higher than what Shopify shows. Someone clicks a retargeting ad, leaves checkout, then buys from the abandoned checkout email, and both platforms count that sale. Left alone, email looks like it carries revenue it really shares with ads, and prospecting quietly gets underfunded.

When I was running my own e-commerce brand, that overlap fooled me too. Now the first test I set up on a new account is a holdout: a small random share of the list kept out of the cart and checkout flows, and part of Meta retargeting held back. It shows what each channel adds on its own before any creative testing starts.

The tracking problems I find are usually plain ones. On one brand's Klaviyo account, onsite tracking was returning zero Added to Cart events, so the cart flow would have fired on nobody. On another, a new cart flow needed the checkout flow's exclusions copied over or customers would have gotten both emails.

On killing variants, a furniture brand I worked on had prospecting split by product. One lane ran at 2.36x, another at 1.36x, a chair lane sat at 0.40x and got cut after 11 days, and a generic catch-all was at 0.08x. One blended campaign would have hidden every loser.

To be upfront, I work alongside a small team rather than as a single embedded hire, including a dedicated tracking specialist for pixel, server-side and CAPI setup. If this needs one person exclusively in-house, better to know now.
