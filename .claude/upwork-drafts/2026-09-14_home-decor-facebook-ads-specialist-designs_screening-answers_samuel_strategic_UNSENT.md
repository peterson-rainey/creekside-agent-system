## ANSWERS (paste-ready)

1. Please attach some examples of ads you've designed.

Nothing attached, and it's better you know why than get something that isn't ours to share. The ads our team designs run in clients' live accounts, and we don't share active client creative without their sign-off. What we can show you is how our team would approach yours. For home decor the first round is usually a styled room scene for cold audiences, a clean product shot with the price for people who already visited, and a before-and-after makeover, each sized for feed and for Stories and Reels. The quickest way to see our thinking is a short call, where we can talk through which of your pieces should lead.

2. How long does it take you to get campaigns up and running?

About two weeks from full access, so campaigns go live in week three. Week one is access and tracking, which means Business Manager, the ad account, the Pixel, and a test order checked in Events Manager against your store. Week two is the first round of ad designs and copy plus the campaign build. Two things can move that date. One is tracking, since if purchases aren't reaching Meta cleanly that gets fixed before anything spends. The other is how quickly designs get approved on your side. Starting now also puts testing in October, ahead of the cost jump we saw across our accounts last November.
