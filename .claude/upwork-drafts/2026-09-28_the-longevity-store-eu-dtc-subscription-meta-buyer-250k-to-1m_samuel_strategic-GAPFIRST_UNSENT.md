## PROPOSAL (paste below this line)

Scoop. When branded product-page traffic, advertorials, comparison funnels and creator ads run side by side, blended new-customer CAC can rise while each funnel's reported CAC holds, because scaling shifts spend toward the funnels that reach colder people. It runs the other way too: branded and product-page funnels often look cheapest because they close demand the advertorials and creators built, so a per-funnel target can overfund the cheap one. We'd set targets on each funnel's marginal euro and its payback on repeat orders, and read the blend against the mix.

Your US launch lands in November, and the auction moves in Q4. In our whole Meta book, blended CPM went from $24.45 in October 2025 to $33.78 in November (+38%) on roughly flat spend ($208K to $214K), and stayed at $33.90 in December. That is a USD blend across mixed verticals and 21 to 22 accounts, with no EU history, so read it as the shape of the risk, not a forecast. It matters for cost caps: we'd use them on proven funnels with steady volume, keep them off tests and the first weeks of the US market, and reset them before the November step, because a cap set on October costs will under-deliver.
