## PROPOSAL (paste-ready)

An ad about remote paralegals and legal assistants doesn't only reach law firm owners. People looking for remote legal work click it too, and Meta can't tell a firm owner from a job seeker until your team does. If "booked a call" is the event Meta optimizes toward, every candidate who books by mistake teaches it to find more candidates.

So the first fix is the signal, not the audience. Your team marks each call once it's held (firm owner who fits, firm owner who doesn't, not a buyer), and the good ones go back to Meta as offline conversions through the Conversions API, tied to the original lead. Standard attribution counts a click for 7 days at most, so we'd send the earliest honest quality stage, a held call with a qualified firm, rather than a placement that closes a month later. If you move to Instant Forms, Meta can optimize on a CRM stage directly once you pass 200 leads a month and that stage lands within 28 days.

The creative does the rest of the screening. The first line should name a firm owner's problem, so a paralegal scrolling past reads it as someone else's ad. Angles we'd test against each other, one change at a time: the cost of intake calls nobody answers, time to fill a seat versus a local hire, and the owner doing admin at night. When one wins, the brief to your creative team says why, not just which.

Structure stays simple until volume supports more: prospecting to firm owners, retargeting people who engaged, and budget stepped up only while cost per qualified call holds.

Our closest work is B2B: a referral software company selling to home service contractors, where Meta built awareness with owners who didn't know the category existed and Google caught the searches that followed. The attached case study has the Ads Manager screenshots. We've also run Meta and Google for a personal injury firm, so we know how firm owners judge a lead, though that account sold to injured people, not to attorneys.

On who does the work: our Meta specialist runs your account hands-on every day, and our tracking specialist builds the Conversions API and CRM feedback. I stay on strategy, and problems reach you before you have to ask.
