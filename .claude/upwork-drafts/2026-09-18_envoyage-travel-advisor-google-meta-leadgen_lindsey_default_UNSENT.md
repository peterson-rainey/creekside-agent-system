When an inquiry reaches one of your employee advisors, how quickly does someone follow up, and does that slow down in the weeks when spend goes up? I ask because on accounts where a person has to call the lead back, that's usually where results break first, not in the ads. On a dental implant account I ran on Meta, leads came in at $25.31 each, 2,558 of them on $64,746.78 of spend. I still cut daily spend by more than half, because the call center couldn't keep up, not because the ads stopped working.

The form choice matters for the same reason. Instant Forms usually bring cheaper inquiries than a landing page, but lower intent, and your advisors notice that before any report does. Meta can optimize toward a later stage instead, like a consultation booked with an employee advisor, but only on Instant Forms, only at roughly 200 leads a month, and only if that stage is recorded in your CRM within 28 days of the lead. That's the setup that lets Meta tell a good inquiry from a form fill.
