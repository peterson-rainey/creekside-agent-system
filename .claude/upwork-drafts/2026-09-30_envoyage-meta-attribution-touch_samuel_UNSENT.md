Hi Alexandra,

Picking this back up with one piece that sits underneath the form versus landing page call I raised earlier.

On Meta, standard attribution counts a click for 7 days at most, and a view for 1 day after the impression. That setting shapes delivery, not just reporting, because the system learns from what gets counted back to it.

A trip is often a high-consideration purchase. Someone can see an ad, sit with it for a few weeks, and inquire well after that window has closed. When that happens the conversion may not be counted back to the ad, so Meta can get weaker signal and delivery tends to lean toward the faster-responding slice of the audience.

That is part of why the form versus landing page decision is better settled before a build than after. Has that decision been made on your side, or is it still on the table?
