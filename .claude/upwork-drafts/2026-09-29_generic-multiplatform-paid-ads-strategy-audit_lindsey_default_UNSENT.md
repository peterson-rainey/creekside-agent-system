## PROPOSAL

A platform list isn't a strategy on its own. Meta, Google, TikTok and YouTube each do a different job, and picking between them (or running all four) should come out of what an audit turns up, not a default best-practice combo. Before I'd recommend a channel mix, I'd want to see what's already live, what's tracking cleanly, and who's actually showing up on each one.

**Auditing what you're paying for**
On a CRM software client I run, I found their signup form was sitting on a Squarespace page using a Constant Contact embed that doesn't integrate cleanly with GA4, so spend was pointing traffic at a form nobody could actually measure. I recommended swapping it for a JotForm embed that fires a trackable submit event and feeds the same CRM directly, sent a quick recording walking through the setup, and the client had it live the next day. Every signup since has shown up as a trackable event instead of disappearing between the form and the CRM. That's usually step one on any new account: confirm what you're paying for actually gets counted before touching ad spend.

**Where the numbers held up, and where they didn't**
On a meal-prep subscription brand I run, cost per conversion moved from $11.53 in January to $5.50 by July as we narrowed in on what converted, then gave some back to $11.59 in August as we pushed spend to its highest point of the year and CPMs drifted up with it. That's the full trajectory, not just the best month: the give-back tracks with the spend increase, not a strategy failure. Pixel and Conversions API get checked for duplicate or missing events before any of that gets read as a signal, since a scaling decision on top of bad tracking data does more harm than no decision at all.

**How I'd approach your account**
Same process either way: audit what's live and what's actually tracking, test audiences and creative before committing real budget to one direction, and build out retargeting once there's a cold-traffic pool worth retargeting, not before. My own hands-on day-to-day is Meta paid social. Google and YouTube get handled by the rest of the team, including one account where YouTube paired with Search brought in 690 patient inquiries at $134 CPA for a healthcare client. TikTok is newer territory for us, worth testing small first rather than assuming it belongs in the mix. Saying that upfront beats recommending a channel because it happens to be the one I personally run.
