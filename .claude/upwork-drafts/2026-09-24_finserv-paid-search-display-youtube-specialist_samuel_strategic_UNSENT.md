Financial services is the one category where the keyword research and the reporting are the same job. Bottom-up research will surface plenty of terms that look like intent, but on a lender or advisory offer the form fill is a weak signal. The leads that matter separate out thirty to ninety days later, at qualified or funded. If Google is only ever told about the form fill, Smart Bidding spends the budget on whoever fills forms fastest, and the careful keyword work gets buried underneath that.

That same gap decides what happens to Display and YouTube. Judged on last click, both lose to Search every single month and get shut off around week six. Judged on assisted conversions, with the downstream event imported, they usually earn their place as remarketing and mid-funnel inventory. So the sequence matters: Search and the conversion import first, Display as remarketing second, YouTube once there is a qualified-lead signal worth bidding toward.

On Search itself, the early money is in the narrow bottom-funnel terms, competitor and brand defense, and a negative list that strips out job seekers, rate browsers, and people researching how to do it themselves. Broad category terms in finance are expensive and mostly educational traffic.

Worth being straight about the mix. Our team ran Google for a US reverse mortgage lender through August. Search alone was about $124K over just under eleven months at roughly $97 a conversion, which is the platform-reported number and exactly the one worth re-baselining against funded loans. A retirement income advisory account is live on the paid social side right now. Across the rest of our accounts Display and YouTube are thinner: one live remarketing campaign with nothing to show on conversions yet, and on YouTube a single campaign that converted at about $200 before it was paused while three others returned nothing. LinkedIn and X we have not run at all, so if those carry real weight here it is better to weigh that now than later.

Two things would sharpen this. Is monthly media spend closer to a few thousand, or well into five figures? And do you already know which leads go on to close, or does that trail off after the handoff?
