When you decide whether to scale a Meta campaign, is the call made on blended ROAS in Ads Manager, or on new-customer CPA against your margin? I ask because my biggest month on a high-ticket furniture account looked like a breakout on ROAS, and close to 80% of that month's spend sat in a 50% sitewide sale and retargeting. A discount month flatters the dashboard. It doesn't tell you what cold traffic costs at full price, and that is the number that decides how far you can push.

That habit comes from building and selling my own e-commerce business. When it's your margin, you stop trusting the flattering number.

Two e-commerce accounts I ran on Meta:

Spa parts and chemicals retailer
Market: contiguous US
Period: Feb to May 2026
Spend: about $52,000
Revenue: about $330,000 Meta-reported, 6.3x ROAS
What I did: took it over from the owner, cut 14 campaigns down to 7, and reworked targeting and creative. Cost per conversion went from $34.72 the month before I started to $19.89 in April with CPM nearly flat, then settled at $22.91 in May. Not all of that is the restructure, but the auction didn't get cheaper. The owner later moved marketing in-house.

Luxury furniture brand
Market: US
Period: April to August 2026
Spend: about $105,000, scaling from $25K to $40K a month
ROAS: Meta-reported 1.7x in May, 4.2x in July, with July carried by the sale and retargeting above. The account ended in August.
