DIGITAL WEALTH

If Revenue Card ads run in the US, Meta will most likely place them in its Financial Products and Services category. That removes lookalikes and age, gender and ZIP targeting, but custom audiences still work. So the Wealth Calculator becomes your retargeting engine: starters, abandoners and completers each become an audience, and Registration and Activation sent back through the Conversions API teach Meta who to find next. Creative does the cold prospecting.

The trap is optimizing to Calculator Start. "Calculate your digital wealth" pulls people who want a number, and Meta gets very good at finding them. Use starts only while volume is thin, then Completion filtered by a qualifier question (program size), then Registration once it fires often enough. Activation lags, so we read cost per qualified registration daily and judge campaigns on cost per Activation by cohort, from your CRM rather than platform reporting.

**What we'd own, and what we wouldn't**
Meta, Google (YouTube via Demand Gen), tracking and the calculator funnel, for both campaigns. We've never run LinkedIn or TikTok ads, so the LinkedIn core of the agent campaign, TikTok, X and organic posting would need someone else.
