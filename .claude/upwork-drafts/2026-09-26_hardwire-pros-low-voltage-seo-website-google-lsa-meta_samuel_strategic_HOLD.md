## PROPOSAL

Local Services Ads has no category for structured cabling or commercial audio-visual work. The closest fits for a low-voltage company are Home security, Home theater and Electricians, so I'd expect LSA to bring more home camera installs and TV mounts than qualified commercial clients. Fine on the side for residential work, but commercial jobs need Search.

**Where the commercial jobs are**

Search is strongest on direct work, like a business moving offices or replacing a camera system, rather than new builds that general contractors bid out. We'd block homeowner and job-seeker searches from day one and give Las Vegas and Seattle separate budgets.

I'd start on Search, not Performance Max. In a paving contractor's account we manage, a Search campaign and a Performance Max campaign ran over the same four months at nearly the same spend. Performance Max clicks cost $3.00 against $14.53, but its leads cost $268 against $131. Meta can wait until there's commercial site traffic to retarget.

**The site and what counts as a lead**

Your homepage prices a $250 cable drop and a four-camera install, which speaks to a homeowner comparing quotes. Commercial ads need their own page showing building-scale work, with a quote form that asks building type and scope. We don't build websites, so a developer would build it from our brief.

Tracking comes first. Calls from the ads only register as leads through a Google forwarding number, and a commercial inquiry can't count the same as a single drop, or bidding learns to find more single drops.

**Your four questions**

1. Paid ads only, so no SEO or website builds. Our trade accounts are mostly Google plus LSA, a paving contractor today and before it a custom home builder and lawn care companies. We do manage the Google Business Profile, since the map results run off it.

2. A Tennessee custom home builder went from 15 to 60 leads while cost per lead fell from $454 to $239. A Nashville lawn and landscaping company saw $300,000 in added profit over 28 months, on 1,500+ conversions at about $50 each.

3. Ad accounts are private, so nothing to link, but the builder's case study is attached.

4. Neither. It's 20% of monthly ad spend, minimum $1,500 a month per platform, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee per platform covering the audit and campaign build. Search and LSA count as one platform, with a 90-day minimum.
