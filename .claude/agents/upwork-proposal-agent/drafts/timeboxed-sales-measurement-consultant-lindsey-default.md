Are the listing campaigns already declared under Meta's housing special ad category, or is that still on the list? Meta re-classifies off listing imagery, facade shots and floor plans, weeks after launch, and a flagged campaign gets rebuilt rather than repaired. On something that closes in weeks, those days are gone.

Inside that category the toolkit thins out. Age and gender locked, detailed targeting cut to a regulated shortlist, no lookalikes since the special ad audience substitute went in 2022, and a 15 mile radius floor, so ZIP level is out. Google does not mirror that. It restricts the same demographics but keeps radius targeting with no minimum, plus remarketing and similar segments. Google can hold per listing geography Meta structurally cannot, which should decide the split.

With targeting gone, creative and the optimization event are the only levers left, and neither works unless the data separates a qualified outcome from a form fill. Report per listing, optimize on a pooled event, and learning accumulates instead of restarting every time a listing opens.

First thing I would check is the attribution window. Meta reports on seven day click, so conversions land after a time boxed campaign has stopped and a close day report understates. Budget optimization shares it, starving the listing closing soonest to feed one closing in six weeks.

I ran the Meta side of a reverse mortgage lender inside a special ad category, about $147K over twelve months, so the restrictions are familiar even if the vertical is not. I have not run property listings though, so I would not assume the outcome. Enquiry, inspection attended, registered bidder, deposit taken. Tell me which one you are paid on.

Ten years on Meta, plus an ecommerce brand I built and sold. Largest account I run is just under $100K a month.
