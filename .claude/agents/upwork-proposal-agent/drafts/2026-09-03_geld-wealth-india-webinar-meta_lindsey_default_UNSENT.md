## THE DRAFT (301 words)

When someone registers for your weekly webinar, does that registration get back into Meta as a conversion event, or does it sit inside your registration tool and your CRM? The answer decides most of what follows, because Meta will only go and find more of the people it can actually see, and webinar-to-appointment funnels are where that link breaks most often.

The reason I ask is that I ran Meta for a mortgage firm at roughly $147,000 of spend across a year, and five of those twelve months carry no conversion data in the account at all. Not because the leads stopped. Because the events stopped reporting. Our team also runs a retirement planning firm right now whose model is close to yours, free educational events feeding private consultations. That account has taken about 37,900 clicks and Meta has recorded 25 conversions against them. People are registering. The platform cannot see it, so it keeps optimizing toward cheap clicks while the cost per person who actually shows up drifts somewhere nobody is watching.

For advisory firms the fix is usually not creative. It is getting registration, attendance and booked meeting passing back as three separate events, then optimizing on the third rather than the first. Registrants are cheap. Attendance is where the qualified money is, and two ads can deliver an identical cost per registration and fill the room with completely different people.

Worth saying plainly: my accounts have been US based, and I have not run financial services ads in India, where advertiser verification and disclosure rules sit differently than they do here. That is a genuine gap and you should weigh it against the rest.

Attached a few relevant results below. There is also a short video on my profile covering how I work through accounts like this one.
