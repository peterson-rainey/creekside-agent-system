The hardest tracking problem here is not Shopify. It is that an $89 accessory and a $30,000 commercial floor package land in two different ledgers. Shopify fires instantly and cleanly. The municipal PO, the studio buildout, the quote that closes 60 days later never make it back into Google. So bidding learns accessories are profitable and commercial is not, and quietly defunds the campaigns producing your largest orders.

The fix is a GCLID captured on every quote and call, stored on the CRM record, then imported back as an offline conversion carrying the real closed value. Until that loop exists, any ROAS you see measures only the cheap half of the business.
