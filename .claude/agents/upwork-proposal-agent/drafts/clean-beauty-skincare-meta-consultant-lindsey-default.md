Are you optimizing for purchases yet, or is the account still sitting on add-to-cart because there is not enough purchase volume to hold the event? For a newer brand that is the first fork, and it is worth settling before anything gets rebuilt. A shallow event teaches Meta to go find people who browse, so the cost per result reads fine in Ads Manager and does not match what landed in Shopify.

I ask because I ran a high-AOV replacement parts brand on Meta that was stuck exactly there. The fix was structural rather than creative. Moving the optimization onto purchase changed who the algorithm went and found, and it did more for that account than any new ad did. Ten years of this, and an e-commerce business I built and sold before any of the agency work, and it is still the pattern I run into most.

The other thing that account taught me is what peak season does to a small budget. Cost per order went from $27.50 in October to $44.93 in December on the same creative, while click-through actually rose over the same stretch. The creative was working and the auction was simply expensive. Recutting ads in the middle of that would have thrown out the only thing holding.

Straight on fit. Meta, Klaviyo and the Shopify side are my lanes, and I currently run a cosmetic and aesthetics brand near $99K a month on Meta.
