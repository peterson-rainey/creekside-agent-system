## ANSWER

The closest recent one was last month. An industrial manufacturer asked five providers for a paid audit of their Google Ads and Microsoft Ads accounts before deciding who to hire, and our team did one of them, including the tracking behind both accounts. The main finding is the one my cover letter opens on. Their only working conversion action fired on a thank-you page view, so technical support requests were being counted as leads. Calls from their ads weren't being recorded at all either. What they got back was a ranked fix list that started with tracking, because every other recommendation depended on it. They ended up hiring someone who came through a referral, but told us ours was the deepest technical read of the five and that everything in it checked out against the account. Since it was an audit, there's no before and after to show from it.

The ongoing version of this work is what our team does day to day, across about a dozen live Google Ads accounts and around $70,000 a month in combined spend, a mix of online stores and lead generation businesses. The paving numbers in my cover letter come from one of those accounts, and the law firm case study I attached is a restructure of an account that was already running.
