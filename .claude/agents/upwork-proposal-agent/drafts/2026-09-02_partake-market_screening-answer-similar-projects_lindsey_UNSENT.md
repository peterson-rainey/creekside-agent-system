## ANSWER (paste-ready, 283 words)

The closest recent match is a meal prep brand we run on Meta in the Dallas market, which
is the same shape as your pilot: a local food subscription, no discounting, growing one
suburb at a time off a core market.

Across the stretch where its conversion tracking was clean, mid October into early
February, it put about $7,100 into 827 tracked orders, roughly $8.61 each. The core
market sat near $6.58. Two suburbs opened off the back of that, and the newer one showed
about $19.31 on only eleven orders, which is the honest caveat: eleven orders is not a
read you can steer on, and that is the same wall a $2,000 budget runs into. Sub $35
acquisition in local food is very achievable. Getting there on thin volume is the hard
part, and the accounts that manage it are the ones that concentrated instead of spread.

Right now I am running Meta for a nutrition brand and a couple of DTC consumable brands,
with Klaviyo email sitting alongside the ads on several accounts. Those are early enough
that I would rather not quote numbers I cannot stand behind yet.

The gap worth naming: I have not run a farmers market or produce box subscription
specifically, and my food acquisition numbers are from late last year into February, not
the last quarter. The nearest thing is the meal prep account above, where a weekly
perishable order, a real cutoff, and a tight delivery radius drove the whole structure.
That part transfers. A produce box has its own churn behavior in the first three
deliveries, and I would want to see your early retention read before assuming it matches.
