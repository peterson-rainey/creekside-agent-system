## PROPOSAL (paste-ready)

Quick question before anything else: what did last November's ads show you? The ones still visible in Meta's Ad Library ran about a week or less each, across several countries, all in under a month.

I ask because Meta usually needs about 50 conversions in a week for each ad set before it stops learning, and short runs spread that thin rarely get there. So I wouldn't treat those numbers as your baseline. In months one and two I'd rather give one market enough budget and time to learn, which is also why I won't put a ROAS number on those months until I know your market and budget.

I've run Meta and email for e-commerce brands for over 10 years, after building and selling my own. The closest account to your category is a four-store women's boutique whose stock came in small batches and sold through fast, so I kept its ads on new arrivals and off anything sold out. Worth checking your current creative against stock, since the white and black bags are sold out right now.

The first setup decision is what to optimize for, and on a new account that depends on how many sales your budget can buy each week. On a digital camera brand I ran, with a young, very online audience, purchase-optimized campaigns paid under $60 a sale, while add-to-cart and checkout campaigns over the same weeks paid more than double. It wasn't a controlled test. On that boutique, at about $50 a day, purchase campaigns weren't getting enough tracked sales to learn from, so I moved it to add-to-cart, then checkout.

Which country do most of your orders come from today, and is that where you want to grow? And what could you put behind that market each month, closer to $3,000 or $10,000? Otherwise I'm just guessing at your scale.

There's a short video on my profile that walks through how I work.
