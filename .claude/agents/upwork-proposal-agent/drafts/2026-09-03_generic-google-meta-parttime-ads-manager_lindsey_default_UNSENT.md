We're looking for a freelancer to manage and optimize paid advertising campaigns across Google and Meta

## Proposal (paste-ready)

Is your Meta targeting still built on stacked interests and lookalikes? Asking because targeting is the one
lever on that platform that has quietly stopped being the lever, which changes what optimizing even means here.

The pattern I run into auditing paid social is a dozen narrow ad sets bidding against each other for the same
people, each starved of the volume it needs. Consolidating that and letting creative do the sorting feels like
giving up control, and it is usually the change that moves the numbers. Creative is the targeting now. Search
rewards the opposite instinct, because those people typed the problem into a box first.

Ten years in, and I built and sold my own e-commerce business first, which taught me to trust what banked over
what the dashboard claimed. On paid social I have run cold Meta lead forms into a call team at real volume,
2,558 leads at $25.31 each over about three months on a local implant practice. Budget allocation is the other
half of what you are describing. Single Meta accounts on my desk have run from $3,000 a month up past $90,000,
and structure that works at one number quietly breaks at the other.

Straight with you on scope: Meta and paid social is mine. Our team runs Google across dental, healthcare, legal,
financial services and B2B software, so each platform gets someone in it daily rather than one person splitting
a part-time week. If you need one freelancer touching both, better you know now than in week three.

Worth settling before anyone hands you a plan: roughly what monthly range are you spending across the two,
and which countries are you selling into?

There is a short video on my profile covering how I work an account like this.
