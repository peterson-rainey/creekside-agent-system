## Proposal text

Are your pixel and your Conversions API both firing Lead with a shared event ID? That one detail decides whether your setup is actually more reliable or just double counting. When the two feeds do not deduplicate, Meta sees roughly twice the leads you really got, your CPL looks great in Ads Manager, and the algorithm spends the next month optimizing toward people who never filled out a form. Most of the "our tracking is broken" accounts I get handed turn out to be that, not a missing pixel.

The reason I go there first is that I ran Meta for a dental implant group where the event layer was the entire constraint. It was a restricted category, so custom events were off the table and we had to establish exactly which standard events were permissible before we could optimize toward anything real. Once that was settled the account produced 2,558 leads at a $25.31 blended cost per lead over four months, and those leads went into a call center, so quality got graded by humans every day instead of by a dashboard.

I have been doing this about ten years, and I built and sold my own e-commerce brand before that, which is where I learned to distrust front end numbers. On live accounts I keep pixel verification and conversion checks as a standing weekly item rather than a setup task, because that is where a problem shows up a week before the CPL does.

One thing worth knowing before I scope anything: what range is your monthly Meta budget in, and what counts as a qualified lead on your end?
