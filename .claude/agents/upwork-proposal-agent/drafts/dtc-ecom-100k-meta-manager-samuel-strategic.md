Q4 is the part of this that would worry me most, given you are scaling right now. On one account we have run for thirteen straight months, spend went from $91K in September to $99K in December, slightly up, and CPM still climbed from $29.57 to $67.59. Impressions more than halved on a bigger budget. Nothing about the creative got worse. The auction just got expensive.

That is the trap for a brand scaling hard into Q4. CPA rises, the instinct says creative fatigue, and winners get cut in November that were perfectly healthy in October. The fix is unglamorous: baseline your CPM against last Q4 before you touch budgets, and judge creative on CTR and cost per add to cart through the run up rather than on blended CPA.

Now the part you will care about more. Our largest single Meta account averages about $91K a month, but it is aesthetics, not ecommerce, so by your own filter it does not count and I am not going to pretend otherwise. On DTC our largest single account peaked at $40,389 a month. If that ends it, I understand.

The case study, straight: luxury furniture DTC, $13,148 a month at 0.91 blended ROAS in September, $40,389 at 4.17 by July. Spend roughly tripled, ROAS went from under 1 to over 4. Worth flagging that the peak month leaned on promo and retargeting, so the cold only number is materially lower. That client is no longer with us.

Structure: consolidated CBO carrying scale, a separate ABO lane for testing so learnings survive budget moves, broad first with interest stacks kept only as a control. Creative gets tested at ad level in its own lane, never inside the scaling campaigns.

Six years on Meta. Rate is percentage of spend, never hourly: 20% of the first $30K, 15% to $60K, 10% above that, so $14,500 at $100K spend, capped at $15,000. Onboarding is $1,500 one time.

On screenshots: the only account we could show above your bar is the aesthetics one. Happy to walk through live reporting instead.

US Central, full business day overlap.
