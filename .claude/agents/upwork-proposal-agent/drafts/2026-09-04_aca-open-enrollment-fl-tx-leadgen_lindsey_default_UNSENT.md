## Proposal body (paste-ready)

Where does your assistant lose more time right now, on leads who never pick up, or on leads who pick up and
turn out never to have been eligible? Those are two different failures and they get fixed in opposite places.
The first one is a speed and offer problem. The second one is a targeting and form problem, and it is the one
that quietly eats an Open Enrollment budget, because a cheap lead that cannot enroll is more expensive than an
expensive lead who can.

The reason I ask is that I ran Meta for a multi-location dental group whose leads went straight into a call
center, which is close to the shape you are describing. Between April and July of this year that account did
2,558 leads at $25.31 each. The part relevant to you is what happened when they pulled spend back. It was not
because cost per lead rose. It was because the call center could not work the volume, and nobody had separated
those two things in reporting, so for weeks it looked like an ad problem. I now build lead-quality feedback in
as its own tracked field from the start rather than as something we discuss on a monthly call.

I also ran Spanish and English side by side in that account. Spanish came in at $17.93 against English at
$26.38, on two campaigns rather than a translated version of the English ones. Separate audiences, separate
creative, separate forms. Translation alone does not reproduce that gap.

On your language scope I want to be straight with you before you spend time on me: I have real Spanish results
and no Portuguese ones. I would rather tell you that now than discover it in November.

The numbers above and the rest of your questions are answered below. There is also a short video on my profile
that covers how I run accounts like this.
