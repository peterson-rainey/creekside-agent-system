Quick question before anything else: is Meta still optimizing on the form submit, or are you sending contract signed back in as an offline conversion? With 90 days of data and a real number on what a lead is worth, that is usually the difference between an account that lowers CPL and one that lowers cost per contract, and those two moves often pull in opposite directions.

The reason I ask is that Special Ad Category takes away almost everything people normally reach for. Age locks to a broad band, no lookalikes, no exclusions, no detailed demographics, minimum radius on the geo. You cannot target your way to a better seller. Every bit of qualification has to move into the creative, the form questions, and the event you optimize on. I ran Meta for a reverse mortgage lender inside a restricted category at around $20K a month, and most of our disapprovals never started in the ad copy. They came from the page behind it, a missing disclosure or a headline the page never qualified. Once the compliance pass was a step before publish instead of a reaction after, rejections got rare.

On volume with Instant Forms, the closest is a dental group I ran lead forms into a call center for, April through July, 2,558 leads at $25.31 blended. That call center graded quality daily, which is the same loop you are describing. When we pushed spend from $9K to $32.5K in a month, CPL went $18.74 to $29.85, then back to $20.06 as the creative caught up. That shape is normal.
