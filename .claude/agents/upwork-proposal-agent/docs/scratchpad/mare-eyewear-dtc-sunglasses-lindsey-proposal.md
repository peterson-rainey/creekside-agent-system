Have you looked at how many orders sit behind that 7.03 ROAS on retargeting? At $77 spent and a $199 AOV, that is two or three purchases. Real, but not a result you can scale. It is your winning prospecting ad getting credited twice on the way out. Fund that campaign to $600 a month expecting 7x and it will settle nearer 1.5x, and you will decide retargeting is broken when it was never the thing that worked.

That matters more than anything else on your list: 15 purchases in 90 days is about one a week. Meta wants roughly 50 conversions per ad set per week to leave learning. Nothing in your account has been close, which is why one 45-day-old ad looks like a hero and five tests at $68 each told you nothing. They could not have. At your volume a creative verdict has to be read on cost per add-to-cart, not purchases, or it fatigues before the data lands.

Ecommerce at this stage is most of what I do. I ran Meta for a replacement-parts retailer into this year, $131K spent at 5.5x blended across about 4,500 orders. What moved it was not audience work. It was cutting the number of live creatives and consolidating ad sets so the pixel had one place to learn. When I ran my own ecommerce brand I did the opposite for a year and called it testing.

On budget, honestly: $1,500 to $2,500 keeps you in this trap. Around $5,000 a month is where one consolidated ad set accumulates enough purchases to read.
