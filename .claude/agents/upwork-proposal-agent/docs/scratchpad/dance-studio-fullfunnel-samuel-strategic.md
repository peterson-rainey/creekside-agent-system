Cost per show-up is the right metric to build around, and it's also the one most dance studios cannot actually optimize toward. The reason is volume. Meta needs roughly 50 conversions per ad set per week to get out of learning, and a single-location studio filling trial classes rarely produces enough show-ups to feed that. Wire show-up back as the optimization event and the algorithm starves, delivery gets erratic, and CPL climbs for reasons that have nothing to do with your creative.

The way I would handle it is to split measurement from optimization. Pick the deepest event that actually fires often enough, usually the booked intro or trial class, and let Meta and Google bid on that. Then pass contacted, show-up and enrollment back through the CRM as reporting only, and use that to move budget, audiences and creative by hand. You still get cost per booked appointment and cost per show-up every week. You just stop asking the algorithm to chase a number it does not see often enough to learn from.

The other thing worth settling early is what a student is actually worth to you. Most studios judge CAC against the first month of tuition, which makes almost every channel look expensive and keeps budgets too small to learn anything. If your average student stays nine months instead of three, what you can afford to pay for a show-up changes completely, and so does how aggressively I would bid on the front end.

On local appointment-based work, the closest is a multi-location med spa on Google and Meta where we held 2x the industry average conversion rate at the same CPA across three locations and kept the third one open, and a naturopathic clinic we still run on about $1,900 a month in Google spend, 580 booked conversions over the last twelve months at a blended $39. We also ran a dental group's Meta lead forms into a call center, 2,558 leads at $25.31 over 90 days, where we deliberately added qualifying questions to the form and accepted a higher CPL to protect lead quality.

Two questions. Roughly what range are you spending per month across Meta and Google right now? And is anything past the form fill living in a CRM today, or is the show-up data still in a spreadsheet?
