## PROPOSAL (paste-ready)

Home lighting on a Google feed lives or dies on margin dispersion across the catalogue, not bid strategy. A commodity bulb and a statement pendant carry completely different margins, so the first question worth asking at £800K a year is whether the outgoing agency was feeding Smart Bidding revenue or margin. Those are very different signals, and getting it wrong quietly caps the account no matter how clean the structure looks. If most of that spend sits in Performance Max, the same problem shows up as brand cannibalisation, where the control is exclusion lists and search term insights rather than a campaign level split.

Worth being straight about where we stand on scale. Our team runs Google Ads for ecommerce accounts built on Merchant Center feeds, including one built from zero now doing 5.73x on Shopping and another running Performance Max at 6.91x. Our largest active ecommerce account is roughly a tenth of your monthly spend, though, and we have not run a home lighting or a UK account before. Better to say that plainly than dress it up. What moves an account your size is feed and bidding architecture, and that is the harder problem to get right.

On a takeover the real risk is the handover, not the strategy. The first thirty days would be audit and tracking parity before any structural change, so a bid reset or a feed remap does not cost you a peak trading week.

We work on a tiered percentage of spend rather than hourly, which at your level lands around $13,000 USD a month, plus a one time $1,500 onboarding that includes the audit.
