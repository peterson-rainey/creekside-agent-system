## SCREENING ANSWER 1 — "See questions in job description"

**1. How I'd approach launching a completely new apparel brand on social.**

Narrow to one claim before any content gets made. Scrubs is a crowded category with funded incumbents, and a new label does not win on lifestyle photography. IGNIS has something they don't: a specific, physical complaint the founder actually lives. Cold clinical environments. Everything comes off that.

Four content pillars. The founder as a practicing CRNA, the problem itself, the product against the problem, and clinician voices. Platform split follows the audience, not the format. Instagram carries the brand and the community. TikTok is where hooks get found cheaply. Facebook reaches the older nurse and CRNA cohort and the group culture, which is where scrub recommendations actually spread inside a hospital.

For a dated launch the sequence runs problem-first for two weeks, product reveal and waitlist push for two, then the drop. The metric before launch is waitlist signups per post, not follower count.
