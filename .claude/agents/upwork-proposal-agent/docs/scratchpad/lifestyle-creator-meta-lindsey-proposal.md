Do you know what your profile's follow rate is right now? Meaning, of the people who land on your profile from a Reel, how many actually tap follow.

That number decides whether any of this works, because Meta does not sell followers. There is no follower objective in Ads Manager. What you can buy is profile visits, and the visit-to-follow conversion happens on your grid, not inside the ad. Which means the Reel with the best organic reach is often the wrong one to put money behind. The right one is whichever makes a stranger scroll your next nine posts and decide to stay.

Most people bidding on this will say yes to every bullet, then quietly run an Engagement campaign and report cost per ThruPlay back to you. Worth screening for.

Straight with you, since you asked for receipts: growing a creator account to a follower number is not on my record, and I'm not going to dress up something adjacent and call it that. Ten years has been Meta for beauty, fashion, salons, events and restaurants, and before that my own e-commerce brand, which I built and sold.

What that built is the machine this needs. Buying a cheap, high-volume, non-purchase action from Meta and grinding the unit cost down through systematic creative testing. On an app account I ran, cost per install came down from $7.36 to $3.90 across 2,662 installs, all Meta, through rebuilding the campaign structure and testing creative in volume rather than putting money behind whatever already worked organically. A follow is a cheaper action than an install and it behaves the same way in the auction.

The tradeoff worth naming now: if your profile converts visitors poorly, paid traffic only makes that leak more expensive. I'd want to see that number before recommending a budget.
