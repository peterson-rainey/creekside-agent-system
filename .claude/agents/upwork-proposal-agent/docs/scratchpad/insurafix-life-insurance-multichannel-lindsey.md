## Proposal

Are your campaigns optimizing for the lead form submit, or for something further down the line like a booked call or a completed application? In life insurance that single setting usually explains the whole gap between lead volume and actual policies written.

The reason I ask is that almost every insurance account I audit is running Instant Forms with prefilled fields. They look great in Ads Manager, cost per lead drops, volume goes up, and then the call floor stops answering because nobody picks up. Meta is doing exactly what it was told to do, which is find people who will tap a button, not people who will sit through underwriting. The fix is rarely new targeting. It is adding friction on purpose with a qualifying question or two, moving the optimization event to the deepest one you can feed consistently, and sending call and application outcomes back so the algorithm learns from closed business instead of form fills.

We ran Meta for a reverse mortgage lender in the same shape as this, roughly $8K to $22K a month to a 60 plus audience in a regulated vertical, and what moved the number was never the audience. It was which event we reported back. When I was running my own e commerce business I learned the same thing more expensively.

Worth saying plainly so you can screen me properly: Meta is my lane. Google, YouTube and TikTok are run by other people on our team. If you need one person owning all four channels, that is not me. If you want the Meta and Instagram side built to produce calls that close, that is exactly what I do.
