## Proposal

Quick question before anything else: what event are your Meta campaigns actually optimizing for right now, the opt-in or the booked call that showed up? That one setting decides everything downstream, and in high-ticket coaching it is where most accounts quietly break.

The reason I ask is that coaching funnels almost always look healthiest at the shallowest event. Meta will happily find you people who fill out an application form for $12, and the dashboard looks great, but the sales team sits on calls with people who were never going to pay four figures. Cost per lead falls, cost per closed client climbs, and nobody catches it because the two numbers live in two different systems.

Our team runs the Meta account for a coach certification program that sells an application-and-call offer, so this is the same shape as your funnel. Over the last twelve months that account has run $84,958 in Meta spend at 2,860 conversions, and the work that mattered was not creative volume, it was pushing the optimization event deeper and rebuilding the application flow. Cost per application went from $256 to $123 while monthly applications went from 41 to 90.

Go High Level is the piece that makes that possible, and it is where we do the same thing on our own funnel. Pipeline stages in GHL know which leads showed, which were qualified, and which closed. Feeding those stages back to Meta as conversion events is what lets the algorithm buy buyers instead of form fillers. Most accounts never wire that loop, so Meta optimizes on the only signal it has.

One thing to confirm: what range is your current monthly Meta spend closer to, under $5K or well past it? The right structure changes at that line.
