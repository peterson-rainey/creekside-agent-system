Owned distribution is the thing that makes a telehealth launch look easy for sixty days and then break.

With a 1M+ social audience and a large email database, your first cold campaigns will not actually be cold. Meta and Google will find the people already on the list, already following, already primed, and day-30 CAC comes back looking excellent. Scale on that number and by day 90 the real acquisition cost surfaces at two to three times what you modeled, with the subscription math built on a number that never existed. So the account gets split from day one: an owned-audience harvest lane, and a genuinely cold lane with every email record, every engager, every site visitor excluded. Two CAC targets, tracked separately. The blended one is for the P&L. The cold one is the only one you scale against.

Second thing worth flagging before you set a launch date. If you are prescribing, Google's healthcare certification is what sets your calendar, not the ad account. That runs weeks, so the usual sequence is Meta plus non-prescription search intent first while it clears. On Meta, restricted health rejections almost always trace back to the landing page rather than the ad copy, which is where the pre-launch time is best spent.
