When you review those first insights, which event will the campaigns be optimizing toward? On a brand new pixel that one choice decides whether month one is data or noise.

I ask because of a replacement parts brand I ran on Meta for about ten months, roughly $130K in spend at around 5.5x blended across 4,500 orders. That account had four campaigns optimizing to shallow events: website visits, add to cart, view content, time on site. Every one of them showed a better return than the broad purchase campaign. The purchase campaign carried more than half the budget at 5.02x while website visits sat at 10.29x on about $3,300. The flattering numbers lived where the money was not. Scaling on that early read would have pushed budget into campaigns that had never been asked to hold any.

The creative rule came out of the same account. Through a stretch where CPMs climbed about 45%, blended return fell from 6.4x to 3.2x while click through rate kept rising. Cutting on return would have killed the angles that were working. I kept the ones whose CTR held and consolidated fourteen campaigns down to seven. CPA came back from $44.93 at the December peak to $19.89 by April, with CPM and frequency both flat across that stretch. Some of that was Q4 pricing unwinding rather than anything I did.
