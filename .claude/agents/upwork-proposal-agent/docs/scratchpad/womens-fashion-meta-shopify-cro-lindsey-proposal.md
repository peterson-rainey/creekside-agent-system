## Proposal

Quick question before anything else: when someone clicks an Instagram ad and doesn't buy, do you know whether they drop off on the product page or make it into checkout and abandon there? Those are two different problems, and the fix for one usually makes the other worse.

I ask because the leak is almost never where the brand assumes it is. I ran a spa parts account on Meta for about ten months at roughly $15K a month, and the ads looked like the problem the whole time - traffic was cheap, revenue was flat. What was actually happening is the campaigns were optimizing on add-to-cart, so Meta had spent months getting very good at finding people who browse. Moving the account onto the purchase event changed who it went after. The site never changed at all.

The reverse happens just as often. I ran a luxury furniture brand on Shopify at about $40K in its heaviest month, and there the site really was the constraint - but not in a way a redesign would have fixed. It was a considered purchase, people came back three and four times before buying, and the account was being judged on last click.

I'll be straight with you on one thing. Ten-plus years in, and after building and selling my own e-commerce brand, what I do is run Meta and read the funnel behind it. I don't rebuild Shopify themes. What I can tell you is where in the journey you're losing people and which of those losses is an ad problem versus a site problem, which is usually the thing holding these projects up.
