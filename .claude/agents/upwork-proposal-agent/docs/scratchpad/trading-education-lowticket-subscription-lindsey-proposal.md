## Proposal

Do you know how your monthly, quarterly and annual mix splits out by the specific ad someone first clicked? Not in aggregate, but per angle. That number decides almost everything else here, and most education businesses never break it out.

The reason it matters is that Meta will optimise toward whatever event you give it, and a $297 indicator purchase is a shallow event when the real money is twelve months of $297. Two ads can both produce a $297 sale at the same cost, and one of them fills the trading room with annual members while the other fills it with people who cancel in month two. Averaged together they look identical. Split by angle they are two different businesses.

That lesson came from a spa parts account I ran on Meta. It ended at 5.57x blended on $129,607 in spend against $721,278 in revenue, and the most flattering campaign in the whole account was a Website Visits campaign showing 10.29x on $3,341. The broad purchase campaign carrying the real volume, $66,917 of it, sat at 5.02x. Judged on the dashboard the shallow campaign wins every time. What it was actually doing was buying people who were already going to purchase.
