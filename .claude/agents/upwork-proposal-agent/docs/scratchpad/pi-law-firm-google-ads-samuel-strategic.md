Personal injury is the vertical where Google Ads volume and Google Ads results come apart hardest. A firm can run a clean looking account, good CTR, cost per lead trending down, while cost per signed case climbs. Smart Bidding cannot tell a signed case from a chiropractor cold call or a solicitation bot. It optimizes toward whatever event you feed it, and most PI accounts feed it "form submit or any phone call."

Fixing that signal is usually the whole engagement. Ran Google Search and Local Service Ads for an Arizona PI firm with exactly that problem. Lead volume was fine, but spam and unqualified calls were eating the intake team. We stopped counting raw calls, made connected calls over three minutes the conversion event, and pushed that signal back into Google. 50+ signed cases in four months, and the firm opened its sixth office during the engagement. Also rebuilt a bankruptcy firm's Search account by market in Orange County: conversions 117 to 229, CPA down 42%.

Two tradeoffs worth naming up front.

A brand new PI account is expensive to teach. Smart Bidding wants roughly 30 conversions a month before it behaves, and signed cases will never hit that number. Bridge on the qualified call event, then tighten once volume supports it. Budget for a learning period instead of judging month one.

Local Service Ads usually beat Search on cost per case in PI, but they are capped by your review volume and Google's screening, and you do not control which case types come in. Search costs more but gives you keyword level control over case type. Most firms want both, weighted differently than they expect.
