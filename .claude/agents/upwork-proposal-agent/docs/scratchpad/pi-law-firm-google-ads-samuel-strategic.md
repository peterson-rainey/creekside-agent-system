Personal injury is the vertical where Google Ads volume and Google Ads results come apart hardest. An account can look clean, good CTR, cost per lead trending down, while cost per case climbs. Smart Bidding cannot tell a signed case from a chiropractor cold call or a solicitation bot. It optimizes toward whatever event you feed it, and most PI accounts feed it "form submit or any phone call."

Fixing that signal is usually the whole engagement. Ran Google Search, Meta and Local Service Ads for an Arizona PI firm with exactly that problem. Volume was fine, but more of it was getting marked spam and fewer leads were turning into cases. We built tracking that identified which leads actually became cases, found calls were the driver, then narrowed the conversion event to connected calls over three minutes. 102 cases over six months at about $950 each on roughly $97K in spend, and the firm opened its sixth office. That account has since ended.

Two tradeoffs worth naming up front.

A brand new PI account is expensive to teach. Smart Bidding wants roughly 30 conversions a month before it behaves, and cases alone will never hit that. Bridge on the qualified call event, then tighten once volume supports it. Budget for a learning period rather than judging month one.

Local Service Ads usually beat Search on cost per case in PI, but they are capped by your review volume and Google's screening, and you do not control which case types come in. Search costs more but gives you keyword level control over case type. Most firms want both, weighted differently than they expect.
