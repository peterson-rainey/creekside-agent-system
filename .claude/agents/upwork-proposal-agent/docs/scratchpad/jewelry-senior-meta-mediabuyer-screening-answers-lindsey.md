## 10. Your expected compensation

I work on a percentage of ad spend rather than an hourly rate or a flat salary. The reason is alignment:
if the account grows, my incentive is to grow it efficiently rather than to log hours against it.

The structure is tiered and marginal, meaning each band applies only to the spend inside it:
20 percent on the first $30,000 of monthly spend, 15 percent on the portion from $30,000 to $60,000, and
10 percent on everything above $60,000. There is a $1,500 monthly minimum and the fee is capped at
$15,000 a month regardless of how large the account gets.

In practice that means $6,000 a month on $30,000 of spend, $9,000 on $50,000, and $14,500 on $100,000.
Onboarding is $1,500 one time.

I cannot give you a real number until I know your budget, and you did not state one. Rather than have you
name a figure cold: is your current Meta spend closer to $20,000 a month, closer to $60,000, or well past
$100,000? That range alone tells me which tier you land in and whether the scaling work you are
describing is a structural rebuild or a volume problem.
