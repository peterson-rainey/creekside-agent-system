The break most pay-per-lead operations never find is between the call platform and the ad platform. If your tracking numbers swap via DNI before the click ID is captured and stitched back to the call, the qualified-call conversions you push back to Meta or Google match probabilistically instead of deterministically. Roughly half land. Both algorithms then optimize toward calls that happened rather than calls that qualified, which is how CPL looks flat while cost per signed case climbs.

Learning phase is the other one that bites at MVA volumes. Meta wants close to 50 conversions per ad set per week. Optimize straight to a qualified transfer and most ad sets never clear it, so delivery stays unstable and quality swings week to week. Bridge on a shorter connected-call duration to exit learning, then tighten to your real threshold once volume supports it.
