The thing that quietly kills daily manual scaling on a health and wellness offer is conversion lag. A lot of these accounts book a real share of purchases 6 to 48 hours after the click, so the ad that looks like it is bleeding on the 10am pass is often at breakeven by the evening pass. Before touching budgets three times a day, the first job is mapping the account's own lag curve and setting intraday read rules against it. Morning becomes diagnosis only unless a hard kill threshold trips, and real budget moves happen on the evening pass once the day's data has mostly landed. That single rule tends to save more winners than any scaling protocol saves money.

Related tradeoff: repeated manual edits inside 24 hours on ABO structures can push ad sets back through learning and cost you delivery you already paid for, so edit frequency and step size get capped per ad set per day. Knowing when not to touch it is part of the system, not an excuse to check in twice a week.

2. Daily routine. Morning is read only. Spend pacing against target, anything that broke a hard CPA ceiling overnight gets cut, everything else logged and left alone. Midday is delivery hygiene: frequency, placement drift, whether the new creative batch cleared learning. Evening is when budget moves, scaling winners in measured increments off settled numbers and pulling back anything above ceiling two days running. A budget change triggers on threshold clearance on settled data, never on intraday noise. A kill triggers when spend passes a set multiple of target CPA with no conversion, or CPA sits above breakeven through a defined window.
