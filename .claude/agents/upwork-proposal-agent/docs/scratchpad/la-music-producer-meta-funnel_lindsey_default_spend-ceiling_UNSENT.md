Quick question before anything else: of the artists who get on a call with you today, how many actually commit to the $4,600 record? I ask because that close rate decides what a lead is worth to you, and a few hundred dollars of cold Meta traffic won't answer it. At a realistic cost per lead, that budget buys a few dozen leads and a handful of calls. That's enough to learn whether serious artists raise their hand at this price, but not enough to know your CAC.

So I'd build the test to answer that one question. Send the ads to a Meta Instant Form with a couple of qualifying questions, like timeline and what they've set aside for the record, before building a full landing page. It costs more per lead but screens out artists who can't invest before they reach your calendar. I'd keep it to one or two ad sets too, since splitting across cities leaves each too little data to read.

That's the setup I ran on a high-ticket dental implant account: Meta lead forms with qualifying questions feeding a call team, $64,746 in spend over about three months for 2,558 leads at $25.31 each. We held spend back on purpose when the call team hit capacity, worth planning for once your calendar fills.

I haven't worked with music producers or studios. Everything would be built in accounts you own, pixel and Conversions API included, and I'd walk you through why each piece is set up the way it is.
