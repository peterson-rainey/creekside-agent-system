## PROPOSAL (paste-ready, ~310 words)

Are you planning to launch straight into purchase optimization, or start on a shallower event while the pixel builds history? That one call decides how the first three weeks go. A brand-new account has no conversion data, so a cold launch optimized for purchases usually sits in learning, burns budget on the wrong people, and makes the creative look like the problem when it isn't.

The reason I ask is that most launches I take over were structured backwards from day one. I ran my own e-commerce brand before this and have spent 10+ years on Meta since, mostly with DTC and local consumer brands, including beauty, food, and premium home goods. On a considered-purchase brand with a high average order value, I keep the pre-launch window doing real work: pixel and CAPI firing on clean events, audiences and exclusions built before spend starts, and creative split into distinct angles rather than five versions of one idea. Then the launch is just turning it on with the learning already staged.

The other thing I would push on early is what counts as a win in the first 90 days. On one meals brand I run, we exclude every previous purchaser so the numbers reported are new customers only, which is slower on paper and far more useful for a launch decision.
