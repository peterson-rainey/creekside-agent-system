1. Do you have extensive experience running ads for a law firm with examples?

Yes, though worth being precise about how much of it is personal injury specifically.

The closest one to your build was an Arizona PI firm we ran on Google Search, Meta and Local Service Ads. Their problem was quality, not volume. Staff were busy on the phone, but fewer of those leads were turning into cases and more were getting marked spam. We built tracking that identified which leads actually became cases, found calls were the driver, then narrowed the conversion event to connected calls over three minutes so bidding chased case shaped calls instead of any call at all. 102 cases over six months at about $950 each on roughly $97K in spend, and they opened their sixth office. Best month was $621 per case. I have attached that one, month by month. That engagement has ended, so I am describing it in the past tense.

The other documented one is a bankruptcy firm in Orange County. Their Search account was built to cover far more territory than the practice actually served, so we rebuilt it market by market. Conversions went from 117 to 229 and cost per acquisition came down 42%.

Past those two, legal is a vertical we come back to rather than the only thing we do, and the rest of the legal book leans bankruptcy and general practice more than PI. Six years, roughly 200 accounts audited, $20M+ managed across Google and Meta.
