Telehealth psychology has a measurement problem that quietly eats small Google Ads budgets. Most accounts count a form submission as the conversion, and in mental health that is a noisy signal. Google's bidding optimises toward whoever fills in forms, which skews to people who aren't ready to book, aren't eligible for the referral pathway, or are price-shopping. Sending the booked and attended appointment back into Google Ads as the conversion is what makes a small budget work, because the algorithm learns what a client looks like instead of what an enquiry looks like.

The tradeoff worth naming up front: offline conversion import needs roughly 15-30 conversions a month before Smart Bidding has enough to learn from. Below that, a small account does better on tight exact-match keywords with manual control, running the import in the background until there is enough data to switch. Going straight to automated bidding on a small budget mostly buys you two months of training the algorithm on noise.

Same reasoning on geography. Telehealth means you can see anyone, but a limited budget spread across all of Australia competes with the biggest national players on the broadest terms. Starting in the states where you are already strongest and expanding on results is usually cheaper to the same destination.

Closest work to yours: a naturopathic clinic we run Google Search for on about $1,900 a month, 590 patient enquiries over the last 12 months at roughly $39 each. A dental practice on a much smaller budget produced 215 enquiries over the same period. Allied health, lead-driven, small budgets. We also run one Google Search account targeting Australia, though it is recent enough that I won't claim results for it yet.

For the Google Ads scope, strategy, keyword and negative research, campaign and ad group structure, ad copy, geo targeting, conversion tracking including the offline import, and a documented handover your team can run internally: $1,500 USD fixed, roughly two weeks to launch-ready. On spend, AU$2,500-3,500 a month is where a psychology Search account gets enough volume to optimise honestly.
