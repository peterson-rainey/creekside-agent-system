## PROPOSAL (330 words)

EV charging is one of the few categories where the buying decision is basically finished before anyone reaches your listing. Someone shopping a Level 2 unit is working through amperage against their panel, hardwired versus plug-in, whether the install needs a permit, whether it plays nicely with the car they just bought, and whether a utility rebate applies. Amazon's search bar answers none of that, so the research happens on Google and YouTube and the purchase lands on Amazon afterward.

That has a direct consequence for the numbers you listed. ACoS inside the console is capped by how much of that upstream research your brand already won. When a competitor owns the comparison content, you end up bidding against shoppers who arrived at Amazon having already picked someone else, and no amount of bid work fixes that.

Worth being straight with you: Amazon console management is not our lane. Our team runs Google and Meta, and we would be a poor fit if what you need is someone in campaign manager tuning Sponsored Products bids. Bringing it up because the external side is genuinely load-bearing for TACoS rather than a separate project. Amazon pays a referral bonus on traffic you send yourself, and off-Amazon conversions feed organic rank, which is the lever that moves TACoS structurally instead of trimming a point off ACoS.

So the honest version: hire the Amazon specialist you posted for, and if you want the demand side built underneath them, that is the piece we would own. Spec-level search on Google is where this category converts. Someone typing "48 amp hardwired charger" is a buyer. Someone typing "ev charger" is not, and most accounts in this space are paying for the second one. Realistic floor there is around $5,000/month to get clean non-brand data.

Closest thing we have to your shape is a technical accessories brand on Shopify where we hold 8-10x on non-brand cold traffic. No EV charging, and no Amazon.
