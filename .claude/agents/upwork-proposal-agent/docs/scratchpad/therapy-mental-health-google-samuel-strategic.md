Therapy Ads

The hardest part of running Search for a therapy practice is not the keywords, it is that Google will not let you measure the way you would for any other local business. Mental health sits inside Google's sensitive interest categories, so remarketing lists, customer match and similar audiences are all off the table. Everything has to be earned on the query itself, which means the conversion you feed back into the account carries almost all the weight.

Most therapy accounts count a form submission as the conversion. Smart Bidding then goes and finds more form-fillers, which in this vertical skews toward people price shopping, out of network, outside your license states, or looking for a service you do not offer. The fix is to stop treating the form fill as the goal and start importing the booked consultation back into Google Ads by GCLID. Nothing clinical leaves your systems in that upload. It is a click ID, a timestamp, and a flat label like "Consult Booked." No name, no email, no presenting concern, no diagnosis, no service line. Google is told which click turned into a booking and nothing else. Same principle on calls: dynamic numbers pass the GCLID and the call duration, while the recording and the notes stay on your side.

One tradeoff worth naming now. Offline import needs roughly 15 to 30 conversions a month before Smart Bidding has enough to learn from. Under that, the honest build is tight exact-match keywords on manual control with the import running quietly in the background until there is enough history to switch. Going straight to a conversion-based bid strategy on thin data mostly buys two months of the algorithm training on noise.
