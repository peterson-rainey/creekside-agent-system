Our conversion tracking specialist builds it and keeps health details families type into chats out of what goes to the ad platforms. Since inquiries start thin, we'd begin on click-based bidding and move bidding onto inquiries once there's steady volume. Google Ads, GA4, GTM and later Meta Business Manager all sit in your accounts with us on manager or partner access, so you keep everything.

3. Spend. I'd start Google at $3,000 to $5,000 a month, which keeps the fee at the $1,500 base, and scale into the rest of your range once inquiries come in. Nursing home terms are competitive, and real CPCs come out of keyword research and the first weeks of spend. El Paso runs as a location inside the DFW campaign with no separate budget and gets its own once it shows volume, so neither metro learns slower on a split. Meta waits for Google data, since it's a second platform at $1,500 a month plus $1,500 onboarding.

Heads-up on targeting: nursing home ads will likely be treated as housing and health-adjacent on both platforms, so expect limits on age targeting and on some retargeting and lookalike features. Our team confirms which rules apply during setup. The plan leans on search intent and metro-level location, and on Meta the creative and broad targeting do the work.

Getting cited organically by ChatGPT or Perplexity is a content play we don't do, so I'd bring in an SEO and content person for that.
