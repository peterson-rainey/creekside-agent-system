One thing worth putting in front of you before we talk, because it changes how I would read the first month. Keyword testing is a Search idea, and Search only pays when the demand already exists as something people type. A brand new free marketplace has almost none of that yet, so what is actually buyable is your own brand name plus broad category terms that run expensive and pull people who want a different thing entirely. Meta is the opposite. You are manufacturing the demand, and the creative does the qualifying that health-condition targeting used to do before Meta pulled it. So I would put the early test budget into creative angles and audience reads, and hold Search as a capture layer for later, once people start looking you up by name.

The one thing I would want to know going in: what does the site ask someone to do the first time they land on it? That is what sets the optimization event, and it is the single decision that determines whether the spend compounds or just buys traffic.
