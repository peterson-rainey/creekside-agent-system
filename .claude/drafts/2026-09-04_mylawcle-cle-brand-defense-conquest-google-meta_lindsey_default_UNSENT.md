## PROPOSAL (297 words)

When a competitor outranks you on your own name, do you know what your branded conversion
rate looks like with them there versus without? Most CLE providers cannot answer that, and
it decides what brand defense is actually worth to you.

Here is what our book shows. On an account running brand and non brand side by side,
branded search sits at $0.35 a click and $16.90 a conversion. Non brand in that same
account is $1.26 and $104.25. Roughly six times cheaper. That gap is the argument for
owning the slot.

On conquest, a competitor keyword campaign we ran converted at $64.24 while the brand
campaign in the same account converted at $136.96. Conquest came in cheaper. The honest
other half: a second conquest campaign in that account, different geography, spent $381.58
across 173 clicks and converted nobody. We shut it off. Conquest is competitor and
geography specific, not a lever you just pull.

Two things I will not oversell. Our legal work sells legal services to consumers. One
bankruptcy practice went from 117 to 229 leads a month, cost per lead down from $86 to
$50.29. Selling to practicing attorneys is a different buyer and we have not done it. And
on Meta, the side I run myself, there is no dependable attorney interest layer. Anyone
promising clean job title targeting is selling you something thin. The honest build is
broad prospecting judged on cost per enrollment, with retargeting closing.

One more, straight: we are a small shop, not a solo freelancer. One person runs your
account every week and nothing gets handed off, but I will not claim to be a one person
operation. Better you know now.

Rather than screenshots I will screen share the accounts behind those numbers, and the
profile video covers how I run accounts week to week.

with a Google complaint filed on it, and what is an average course sale worth?
