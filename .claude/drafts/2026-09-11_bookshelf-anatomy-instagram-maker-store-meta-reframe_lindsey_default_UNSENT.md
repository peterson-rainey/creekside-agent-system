**1. One area to evaluate or improve first**

Where the tap from Instagram actually lands. **3. A content strategy you can maintain**

Build it around what you already do instead of adding new work. One stitching or painting session can become a process Reel, a few Stories and a finished-piece carousel, so a single batch day covers a week or more. I'd keep it to three repeating themes: work in progress, the finished piece and where to buy it, and your community, like the Stitch Social events. Every product post links straight to that product. Then once a month, put a small ad budget behind whichever post people saved and shared most. That's how you keep promoting consistently without having to post more.
