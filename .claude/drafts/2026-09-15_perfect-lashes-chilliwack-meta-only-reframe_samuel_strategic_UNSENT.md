Clients and lash-training students look like one local audience, but on Meta they're two different buyers. A lash client lives a short drive from Chilliwack, and their value is the fills that follow, not the first set. A training student is buying a new career, may drive in from Abbotsford or Mission and can take weeks to decide. Put both in one campaign and Meta spends toward whichever result it can get cheapest, so the other side quietly starves.

Follower count is the wrong target for the same reason. Meta shows ads to the people most likely to complete the action you optimize for, so an ad optimized for follows finds people who tap follow easily, not people who book.

The first 90 days:

Days 1-30: check the pixel and whether your booking system can report a finished booking back to Meta. Then two campaigns: services on a tight radius around Chilliwack, and training across the Fraser Valley with a short form asking about experience and timing. Creative comes from your real lash work and artists on camera.

The tradeoff is volume: Meta needs a steady flow of results to learn, and bookings are rarer than leads. On a smaller budget, both would start on leads and tighten to bookings once volume supports it.

Days 31-60: separate the ads that book from the ads that only get clicks, and retarget people who've engaged with your profile or visited the site.

Days 61-90: move budget to what's booking. Training gets judged on inquiries that become enrollments, which runs slower, so we wouldn't cut it on 30-day numbers.

Each month we'd report cost per booked appointment, new clients, and cost per training inquiry that enrolls.

You should hear this upfront: our team runs paid Meta and Google. We don't build websites or manage organic posting. Once we're running, we'd flag what your service and training pages need for paid traffic.

Our closest match to your training program is a company that sells a coaching certification to adults, and our team runs their Meta today. On the service side, we ran Google and Meta for a three-location med spa.

Any split we'd suggest is useless if it's outside what you're comfortable spending, so the range comes first. In Canadian dollars, is that closer to $1,500 a month, $3,000, or $5,000 plus?
