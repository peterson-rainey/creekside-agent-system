Most luxury Shopify brands score Google on a number that describes neither half of the account. Blended ROAS on a premium catalog is largely a branded-search artifact, and branded search is where paid harvests demand that PR, organic and social already created. Scale on that number and you fund the harvest while the thing creating the demand stays flat.

The shape from an ecom account our team runs now on Google and Merchant Center. Branded search took $25,483 and returned a $16.89 cost per conversion at 28.75x. Unbranded search took $27,183 in the same window and returned $104.25 at 5.31x. Near-identical spend, six times the acquisition cost, and the blended search line reads 16.48x, which is true of neither campaign. On a high-AOV catalog that gap widens, because the brand term is cheap and the category term is contested.

So the first work is not campaign optimization. It is splitting the account so brand and non-brand are never scored together, then holding a separate CAC target against each. New-customer acquisition cost moves the business; blended ROAS hides it.
Two things worth flagging. Performance Max is not the free win it gets pitched as on premium catalogs. On that same account, feed-only PMax ran against standard Shopping over the same window. PMax returned 5.29x against Shopping at 5.52x, so we ended PMax in July. Worth testing, not worth assuming.

And the honest part. We are a paid search and paid social shop. We have no SEO clients and no organic ranking case study, so if organic visibility is the larger half of this role, we are the wrong hire for it. Where we are useful next to it is the paid consequence: what branded demand and feed quality do to blended CAC. We work in GA4 and GTM daily and built our own server-side click-ID attribution, though that sits on our own funnel rather than a client rebuild we can show you.

The closest account to yours is a luxury Shopify Plus brand on a headless build, hero product between $1,995 and $3,495, CAC target in the low hundreds. It is young, and I would rather be straight: branded converts, non-brand has not cracked yet, and we paused cold keywords and Shopping rather than let them spend into nothing.
