## Q1 — "Describe your recent experience with similar projects" (paste-ready)

The closest match ran until about a month ago. Our team managed a reverse mortgage lender's Google account at roughly $46K a month, an existing account we took over rather than built, across Search and Performance Max. Account-level cost per conversion went from $61.44 in the first month to $46.66 in the twelfth, and the final ninety days ran $112,570 against 2,979 conversions, about $37.78 each. That engagement ended on August 9, so I am describing it in the past tense.

Currently running, and closer to your budget than to that one, is an ecommerce account on Google and Meta at roughly $8K to $9K a month on the Google side. July cost per conversion was $57.85 on 141 conversions. August was $33.35 on 269, with spend up about ten percent. The first ten days of September have eased back to $39.62, which I would rather tell you now than have you find later. Some of that gain was media efficiency and some was a seasonal pull, and we have not finished separating the two.

On the specific question of reading a change without disturbing the rest of the account, the useful example is a paving client we still run. Search and Performance Max were pulled on matched dates at near identical spend. Search returned 44 leads at $131.22 on $5,752. Performance Max returned 21 leads at $289.38 on $6,090. Performance Max clicks were about five times cheaper and its leads were more than twice as expensive. Cost per lead on that account has been volatile month to month, so I am offering it as the method rather than as a result.

Where we are thin: none of this was run through the Google Ads Experiments tool. The isolation above was done at campaign level on matched date ranges.
