Your own read is close, but the data can't carry the conclusion yet, and that's the first thing worth saying.

Fourteen signups, roughly six trials, one activation. At that sample size neither "top of funnel" nor "activation" is a finding. It's noise. Any agency that reads six trials and hands you a confident verdict is selling you certainty they don't have.

What the data does support is structural, and it's ugly in a fixable way.

Meta at $10/day and $7.42 per result is buying about 1.3 events a day. Meta's optimizer needs roughly 50 conversions per week per ad set to exit the learning phase. You have never been out of it, not once. So the "Meta performance" you're evaluating isn't Meta's performance. It's Meta guessing. Same story on TikTok, where a climbing CPM is what an under-fed auction looks like from the inside.

So my honest read: it's a spend-concentration problem first, and an activation problem second. Both paid channels are funded below the level where either can produce a readable signal, so right now you're paying to generate data you can't act on. Splitting $250-350/week across two platforms is the single most expensive decision on the board.

The 0% Facebook activation is the one number I'd take seriously, and not because of volume. Reels traffic on a "track your card profits" hook selects for someone who wants the outcome. Your actual buyer already has a system, a spreadsheet and four open tabs, and is annoyed by maintaining it. Those are two different people and two very different creative briefs. That's an audience-fit problem masquerading as an activation problem, and it's why I wouldn't let you conclude "it's onboarding" and go build onboarding first.

One more, since you raised it: "Track. Analyze. Profit." is the exposure. TikTok didn't flag your creative by accident, and that claim sits in your brand line, not just an ad. That's a compliance layer and a positioning question together, and it belongs in the audit rather than on a roadmap for later.

Closest work we've done:

Birthday Club App (Meta, consumer subscription app). Cut cost per install 47%, from $7.36 to $3.90, across 2,662 installs. Account restructure plus systematic creative testing. Same problem shape as yours: an under-fed account with cost climbing and no signal.

ReferPro (early-stage SaaS, post-seed). Doubled inbound leads and ARR in six months by splitting Meta to awareness and Google to demand capture, instead of asking one channel to do both jobs at once.

Straight answer on fit: we have not run a trading card, collectibles, or hobby-community account. I'd rather tell you that now than have you find it in month two. What we have run repeatedly is passionate-community consumer products and early-stage subscription funnels, and the audience-fit work is the same work.
