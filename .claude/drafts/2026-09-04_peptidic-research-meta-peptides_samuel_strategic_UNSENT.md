Research peptides are one of the few Meta categories where the account is the campaign. Before purchases or ROAS mean anything, the asset has to survive review, and enforcement on unapproved-substance claims lands at the category level, not the ad level. A rejected feed or a restricted page is not something you fix by rewriting copy.

Straight answer on your proof requirement. We have hands-on research peptide work across two brands, but it is diagnosis and strategy, not a managed Meta peptide account. The deepest of it was Google rather than Meta: a store selling BPC-157, TB-500 and GHK-Cu whose account read Not Eligible across Search and Shopping after a South Florida healthcare attorney had reviewed and cleared the site pre-launch. The legal review was clean and the automated system did not care. That is a Google outcome, not a Meta one, though the mechanism rhymes.

So there are no Meta peptide screenshots to send you, and I will not dress an adjacent account up as one.

What I would do on peptidicresearch:

Start with the safest part of the catalog instead of the best sellers. Some SKUs will never be advertisable, and week one is the cheap time to find out which. Launch brand-level creative with generic research framing and no compound names in headlines, establish clean delivery history, then A/B toward specific compounds once baselines clear. Keep catalog and Advantage+ shopping out of the launch entirely, since feed rejections hit the whole category at once.

On the two-week trial, the honest read is that 14 days on a restricted catalog measures whether the account survives review, not whether the ads work. Most of that window goes to establishing legitimacy and exiting learning, so we work on a 90-day minimum. Happy to define what week two should look like, just not to have the ads judged on it.

In this category restriction is a when, not an if. The durable fix is landing page structure and strict RUO framing, sometimes LegitScript, and it is never ad copy. For scale, our team runs a Meta account in aesthetics at roughly $99K a month.

First question before anything else. Is this a fresh business manager, page and domain, or has any of it been restricted before? That changes the opening sequence. And where is your Meta spend now, and is the target double that or an order of magnitude up?
