How many form fills a month do you expect at your starting budget? I ask because Meta's conversion leads goal, the setting that optimizes toward qualified calls instead of raw submissions, wants around 200 leads a month plus a CRM stage update within 28 days before it has enough to learn from.

On the financial services and real estate lead-gen accounts I've run, the order that works is the same: launch optimizing for leads with your qualifying form doing the filtering, send Lead, Qualified and Closed from GHL from day one, then switch the goal once volume supports it. Switch too early and delivery stalls. Never switch and Meta keeps finding form-fillers. Your GHL contractor can push stage changes through its Conversions API action, so my side is mostly mapping stages and confirming events match in Events Manager.

Since income can't be targeted, I'd run broad against one interest ad set built around STR ownership and hosting, then add a lookalike of closed clients once there are enough. The Closed value event is what eventually makes broad work.

For hooks, all 4 run as separate ads in one ad set with the same body, judged on cost per qualified booking, not click rate. The static copy variations get their own test.

On Special Ad Category, I'd review the final copy against Meta's financial products category before launch, since how the ads talk about buying the property matters more than the tax angle.
