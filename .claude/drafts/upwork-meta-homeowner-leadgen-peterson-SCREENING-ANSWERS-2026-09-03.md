## 1. Describe your recent experience with similar projects

The closest and most recent is a high ticket consumer lead gen account on Meta that we have run continuously for the past 12 months, roughly $206K in spend and 925 leads at a $223 blended cost per lead, still running now. That one targets older homeowners on a considered purchase with a phone based sales team, so the entire job is lead quality rather than lead volume. Before that, a high volume instant form program feeding a call center, 2,558 leads at a $25.31 average cost per lead across about $65K over four months, where we scaled monthly spend from $9K to $32K and held cost per lead between $18 and $30. On the home services side we currently run a Texas gutter account on Meta, though it is new enough that I would not put numbers behind it yet. No solar or roofing at scale, which your post lists as preferred rather than required, and that is the honest bucket I fall into.

## 2. How do you use metrics to inform your strategy?

The metric that governs the account is the deepest one I can feed back into Meta, not the one that looks best in Ads Manager. Cost per lead is a check, not a decision metric, because in homeowner verticals you can always buy a cheaper lead by making the form easier. So the first build is the path from lead to contacted to appointment set to closed, then pushing whatever your sales team counts as qualified back in as an offline conversion, so the algorithm optimizes on that instead of raw form fills.

Day to day I read frequency and CPM before touching creative, because a rising cost per lead is usually creative fatigue or a hot seasonal auction rather than a targeting problem, and those get different fixes. I also hold to volume thresholds before acting. On that 12 month account, monthly cost per lead swung between $130 and $326, so no single month triggers a rebuild. On the call center account, scaling spend more than 3x moved cost per lead from about $19 to $30, which was only acceptable because their booking rate held. That tradeoff is what I would want us measuring together from week one.
