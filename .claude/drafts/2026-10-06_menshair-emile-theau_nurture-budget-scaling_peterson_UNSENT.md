**Subject:** When more budget makes Google look worse

Hi Emile,

In August I sent over a note on whether Google is counting an inquiry or a consult that actually attended. This one is about what happens to those numbers once budget goes up.

It is one of the more common ways a Google account starts looking worse than it is performing. Once an account finds the handful of terms that genuinely convert, the instinct is to put more budget behind what is already there. Spend rises, broader queries come in alongside the ones that were working, and blended cost per inquiry drifts up while the original terms keep performing exactly as before. Nothing has broken. The average just has more in it. Splitting the proven terms into their own campaign before the budget moves keeps the two readable separately, which is the difference between knowing a scale-up worked and guessing.

So if spend has moved at all this year, the number worth isolating is cost per inquiry on the terms that were converting beforehand, on its own rather than inside the account average. Has that one held?
