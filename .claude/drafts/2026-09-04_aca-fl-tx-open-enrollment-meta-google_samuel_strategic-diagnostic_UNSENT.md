Open Enrollment for 2027 starts November 1, about eight weeks out, and the biggest risk to that window is not creative or targeting. Since January 2025 Meta classifies every insurance product, health included, under the Financial Products and Services special ad category. Advertiser identity verification and proof of licensing gate launch before a single ad runs. Inside the category, age locks to 18 to 65 plus, household income, detailed demographics, lookalikes and exclusions all disappear, and geo carries a 15 mile minimum radius with no ZIP. ACA eligibility is essentially income, household size and age, exactly what Meta takes away, so qualification has to move into the creative, the form, and the optimization event. If that verification is not already moving it should start this week.
