Open Enrollment for 2027 starts November 1, which leaves about eight weeks. The thing most likely to cost you that window is not creative or targeting. Since January 2025 Meta classifies every insurance product, health included, under the Financial Products and Services special ad category. Before a single ad runs, the account needs advertiser identity verification plus proof of licensing. That review gates launch and it is not quick. If it is not already moving it should start this week, ahead of any strategy work.

That category also removes the levers this offer would normally run on. Age locks to a broad 18 to 65 plus band, household income and detailed demographics go away, lookalikes go away, exclusions go away, and geography carries a 15 mile minimum radius with no ZIP targeting. ACA eligibility is essentially income, household size and age, which is exactly what Meta strips out. So every piece of qualification has to move into the creative, the form, and the event you optimize toward.

Google sits under a different regime. Its restricted targeting policy covers housing, employment and credit, so there you keep ZIP level geography, remarketing and Customer Match. For Florida county level marketplace work, that asymmetry is the whole argument for how to split budget.
