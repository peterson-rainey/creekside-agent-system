Before anything gets built, the question I would want settled is which country list you are actually advertising into, because on Meta that decides what can be built at all.

Trading and CFD adjacent advertising is restricted at the country level. In several markets an advertiser has to evidence regulatory licensing before the account is properly buildable, and in others the restriction lands on the claims and the format rather than on the product. So the compliance work is not a review step at the end. It sets the shape of the account on day one, and it is cheaper to know now than in week three when a build is already sitting in review.

The second thing I would want is your optimization event. On challenge funnels the campaign is usually pointed at the free registration rather than the challenge purchase, and Meta does exactly what it was asked to do. It goes and finds the cheapest person who will register and never pay. Cost per lead reads fine while the funded rate quietly falls, and it gets diagnosed as a targeting or creative problem when it is neither.

Two things make that worse in this specific business, and neither shows up in an ecommerce account. Resets and refund guarantees mean a purchase event that fired today can be worth nothing a week later, and a reset bought by an existing trader gets counted as a fresh acquisition. Then affiliate and promo traffic, which this space runs heavily on, gets credited to paid when it is not separated out, so the algorithm is rewarded for finding more of the wrong audience.

Straight with you on one thing. We have run financial services lead generation in our book, retirement planning and reverse mortgage, but no prop firm or forex account. The transferable part is the structure and measurement side, plus working inside a restricted category rather than around it.

Ten years doing this, and I built and sold my own ecommerce business before that. Most recently on a purchase-optimized account, $128,518 in spend and 4,440 purchases at $28.95 each, prior purchasers excluded throughout. That engagement has ended.

Two questions so I can size this properly. Roughly what range are you spending on Meta a month right now, and which countries are in scope with licensing already in place for them?

There is a short video on my profile covering how I work an account like this one.
