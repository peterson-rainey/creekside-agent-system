A two-sided marketplace like Rescented has a trap most paid setups fall into: every channel gets optimized toward one "install" or "signup" event, so the dashboard looks healthy while the ads quietly bring in browsers who never buy and never list. The fix is to track buyers and sellers as separate conversion paths from day one, then judge each channel on cost per first purchase and cost per first listing, not cost per install. Once that's in place, it usually becomes obvious that one or two channels are carrying the marketplace and the rest are buying cheap clicks.

That's where I'd start. Before touching budgets, I'd audit what's firing today: app events versus web events, whether first purchase and first listing are passed back to Google, Meta and Reddit as real conversions, and how installs from Apple Search Ads connect to anything that happens after. With 30,000+ users and organic traction, there's also a baseline question worth answering early: how much of what paid is being credited for would have happened anyway. A simple holdout or geo split on your biggest channel answers that faster than any attribution model.

From there the order is:
1. Fix the foundation. Separate buyer and seller events, clean conversion setup per platform, one source of truth for CAC and ROAS.
2. Reallocate. Shift spend toward whichever channels produce first purchases and first listings at a CAC your take rate can support.
3. Test on a schedule. Creative, copy, audience and landing page tests with a clear hypothesis and a pass/fail line, so the weekly report shows what won and what we're doing with it.

One tradeoff to decide together: supply or demand first. If buyers are landing on thin inventory in popular fragrances, seller acquisition may be the better dollar for a while even though it looks worse on a ROAS report.

Our team has done this kind of rebuild on the app side. For Birthday Club, a consumer app, we restructured the Meta campaigns and ran systematic creative testing to drive 2,662 installs while cutting cost per install from $7.36 to $3.90. On the Google plus Meta side, we ran full-funnel acquisition for ReferPro after their seed round, Meta for awareness and Google for demand capture, and doubled inbound leads and ARR within 6 months.
