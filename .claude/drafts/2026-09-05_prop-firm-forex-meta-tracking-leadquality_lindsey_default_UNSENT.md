What is your Meta account optimizing toward right now, the free signup or the funded challenge? With prop firms that one answer usually explains both problems in your post at the same time, because the lead quality problem and the tracking problem are the same problem.

The purchase that matters to you almost always happens somewhere Meta cannot see. Someone clicks, registers, then buys a challenge inside a dashboard or a checkout on another domain. So the pixel fires on the registration, Meta learns from the registration, and it gets very good at finding people who will register and never pay. Cost per lead looks healthy while the funded rate quietly falls. Fixing the tracking is what fixes the lead quality, it is not a separate workstream.

Where I would start is match quality rather than event volume. Which identifiers come back from wherever the challenge actually gets bought, and in what shape. A registration you own on your own page returns a hashed email, phone, fbc and fbp. A purchase on a platform you do not control often returns an order reference and a timestamp and nothing Meta can resolve to a person. That decides what you can honestly optimize toward before anything else does.

Straight with you on one thing. We have run financial services lead generation in our book, retirement planning and reverse mortgage, but no prop firm or forex account. Restricted financial categories also tend to pick your event ladder for you, the way a healthcare account in our book could send standard events only and not custom ones.

Ten years doing this, and I built and sold my own ecommerce business before that. Most recently on a purchase-optimized account, $128,518 in spend and 4,440 purchases at $28.95 each, prior purchasers excluded throughout. That engagement has ended.

Roughly what are you spending on Meta a month now, and what is a funded trader worth to you?

There is a short video on my profile covering how I work an account like this one.
