## PROPOSAL TEXT (paste-ready)

Multi-location personal training has a measurement problem that never shows up in the ad account: when coaches operate as entrepreneurs inside your model, consult-to-client conversion is a property of the coach, not the campaign. Two locations can post an identical cost per lead and land twice as far apart on cost per client, and a blended number hides which one is which. So the first thing worth building isn't campaigns, it's closing the loop. Every signed client written back to source, location and coach, so Google and Meta optimize toward the people who actually sign instead of the people who fill out forms.

Until that loop exists, cost per lead is the only number that means anything, and it's the one we'd hold ourselves to in month one.

On that: our team's local-service book is healthcare heavy rather than gyms. High ticket, consult gated, appointment driven, which is the same funnel shape as an intro program or a transformation package. A dental implant practice we ran Meta lead forms for produced 2,558 leads at $25.31 each over roughly three months, routed straight into their call center for booking. An active cosmetic dental account is running $30.65 per lead on veneers, the closest thing we have to a high ticket elective offer. We also ran a three location med spa through a stretch where the third location was at risk of closing.

What we don't have is a published cost per client figure. Every documented result we own stops at cost per lead or cost per acquisition, because only one account has given us CRM write-back, and there we work inside GoHighLevel hands on at the API level. I'd rather say that now than hand you a modelled CAC.

Two things before this could be sized properly. Roughly what range is the current monthly ad spend across all locations, and are all locations US based?

One tradeoff worth flagging. A 30 to 60 day window is short for this particular funnel. A fresh pixel is still in learning at day 30, and your own sales cycle means the leads from week one haven't finished becoming clients by week eight, so you'd be judging the account on whichever half of the funnel resolves fastest. We work on a 90 day minimum for that reason. Happy to walk through what those first 90 days would actually look like.


Samuel
