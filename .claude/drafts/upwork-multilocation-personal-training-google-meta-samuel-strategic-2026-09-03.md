## PROPOSAL TEXT (paste-ready)

Multi-location personal training has a measurement problem that never shows up in the ad account: when coaches operate as entrepreneurs inside your model, consult-to-client conversion is a property of the coach, not the campaign. A blended cost per client across locations hides which coach is carrying it. So the first thing worth building isn't campaigns, it's closing the loop. Every signed client written back to source, location and coach, so Google and Meta optimize toward the people who actually sign instead of the people who fill out forms.

That gap is bigger than it sounds. On a multi-location dental implant account our team ran Meta lead forms for, the same offer inside one location ran anywhere from $13.35 to $48.87 per lead depending on segment and creative. Blended, it read as $25.31 across 2,558 leads over roughly three months. Healthy looking, and almost useless for deciding where the next dollar goes. Leads routed straight into their call center, and spend was capped at what the intake team could dial the same day. Capacity was the ceiling, not budget, and with coaches owning their own consult calendars you'll meet that constraint first.

Our local-service book is healthcare heavy rather than gyms. High ticket, consult gated, appointment driven, which is the same funnel shape as an intro program or a transformation package. An active cosmetic dental account is running $30.65 per lead on veneers, the closest thing we have to a high ticket elective offer.

What we don't have is a published cost per client figure. Every documented result we own stops at cost per lead or cost per acquisition, because only one account has given us CRM write-back, and there we work inside GoHighLevel hands on at the API level. I'd rather say that now than hand you a modelled CAC.

Two things before this could be sized properly. Roughly what range is the current monthly ad spend across all locations, and are all locations US based?

One tradeoff worth flagging. A 30 to 60 day window is short for this funnel. A fresh pixel is still in learning at day 30, and leads from week one haven't finished becoming clients by week eight, so you'd be judging the account on whichever half resolves fastest. We work on a 90 day minimum for that reason. Happy to walk through what those first 90 days look like.


Samuel
