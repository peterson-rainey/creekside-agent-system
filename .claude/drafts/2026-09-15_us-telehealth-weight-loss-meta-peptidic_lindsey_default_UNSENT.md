In this category, the hooks that sell across most of DTC are the ones that fail review. Calling out the viewer's weight or implying they have a condition breaks Meta's health rules. Suggesting a compounded medication is the same as the brand-name drug is the claim the FDA's recent warning letters to telehealth companies keep citing. So I'd build angles on the experience rather than the outcome: price, how quick the intake is, who reviews it, and what arrives and when. On format, I'd lead with a provider explaining the process on video, plus simple statics to test price framing.
