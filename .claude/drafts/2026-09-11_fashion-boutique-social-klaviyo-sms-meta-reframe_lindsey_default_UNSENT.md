When someone buys in one of your stores, does that sale land on her Klaviyo profile, or does Klaviyo only know the women who order online?

I ask because a women's boutique I ran Meta for had its own customer profile split by channel: online shoppers 25 to 34, in-store shoppers 35 to 44. If only web orders reach email, every segment, new arrival send and past-buyer audience on Meta describes the younger customer, and the one spending in person gets the wrong message or nothing.

That account shaped how I run fashion on Meta. Stock turned fast in small quantities, so catalog ads only showed new arrivals, never sold-out pieces. Retargeting skipped anything under $25, and targeting stayed on the states around the stores, where people already knew the name.

My Klaviyo work is mostly e-commerce flows: welcome series that stop once someone buys, plus cart and checkout flows. On one store no add-to-cart events were coming through, so a cart flow there would have reached nobody. On another, a new cart flow had to exclude anyone who had started checkout, or those shoppers get both emails. SMS I haven't run as its own channel, so I'd build it with you and keep it to what's truly time-sensitive, like event reminders and small-batch drops.

Timing matters as much as content. On a replacement parts store I managed, October and December spend were nearly identical, yet CPM rose 44.9% and cost per conversion 63.4%, mostly from auction pressure. So I'd use Meta in October to grow your list while it's cheaper, and let email and SMS carry more of the holiday push.

First 30 days: get in-store purchases into Klaviyo, check every flow's exclusions and tracking, launch a new arrivals catalog and retargeting on Meta, and build a 90-day calendar around drops, in-store events and the holidays. Reels are best shot by someone in your stores; my part is putting budget behind the ones already working.

Rather than a trial, we work on a 90-day minimum, since a new Meta setup judged at day 30 is still in its learning phase. Meta is a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee, then a percentage of ad spend. Email I'd quote once I've seen your Klaviyo account.
