**Q: Describe your recent experience with similar projects**

The most similar recent project was a luxury furniture DTC brand I ran on Meta from April until August, when the brand took paid social in-house. Different category from supplements, but the same job you're describing: owning the spend, reading tests as they come in, and deciding what keeps budget.

Over that stretch it spent about $105K, and Meta attributed roughly $293K in revenue, around 2.8x. The useful part is how uneven that was underneath. Several products ran as their own prospecting campaigns: one returned 2.36x, another 1.36x, a chair campaign came in at 0.40x and was off within two weeks, and a general catch-all campaign at 0.08x was shut down after about three. Retargeting ran 5.42x. The best month, July, spent $40K at 4.17x, but nearly all of that revenue came from a 50% off July 4th sale plus retargeting, so I never treated it as what new customers actually cost.

Right now I run nine active Meta accounts. The largest is a cosmetic dental practice at about $94K over the last 30 days, alongside a set of smaller ecommerce and meal-prep brands. On every one of them, the first thing I separate is what a new customer costs from what promos and returning buyers make the account look like, before any budget moves.
