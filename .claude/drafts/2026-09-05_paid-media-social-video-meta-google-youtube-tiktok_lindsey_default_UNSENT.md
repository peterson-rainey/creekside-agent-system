## PROPOSAL (paste-ready, 2,695 chars)

Which system owns the appointment outcome, and does anything push that outcome back into the ad platforms?

Asking because lead to appointment conversion and CAC are both on your success list, and none of the four platforms can see either one. They see a form fill and stop. So unless appointment status is being uploaded back in, through CAPI on the Meta side and offline conversion import against GCLID on the Google side, the algorithms optimize toward whoever fills out a form fastest. That is close to the opposite of qualified. The second problem is that Meta, TikTok and YouTube all self attribute on view through, so with one video library running across all of them the four dashboards will report more leads in total than your CRM actually received.

Worth settling before creative volume goes up, because more variations against an ungraded back half just produces more of whatever the pixel already likes.

The closest thing in my book to what you are describing is a dental group I ran Meta for. 2,558 leads at $25.31 each, across 78 active days on roughly $64,700, lead forms feeding a call center. The number I would actually point at is not the CPL. We pulled spend back while the CPL was still holding, because the call center could not work the volume it was getting. That is the failure mode when nobody is grading the back half.

On the consumer side I ran a camera brand at $58,298 into 617 purchases. I also run Meta for a cosmetic practice, a little over $1.19 million in spend across the last twelve months, so scaling without letting frequency and CPM eat the account is familiar ground.

Now the parts I will not oversell.

Standalone YouTube video campaigns are not something I can show you results on. Demand Gen is, about $44,800 into 454 conversions across our team's Google accounts, and that is the campaign type that actually puts short form video in front of people on Shorts and Discover with a conversion goal attached rather than a view goal. If YouTube here means lead generation, Demand Gen is the honest route and I would rather tell you that than quote you a view rate.

TikTok is not in my book at all. I would rather say so now than have you find out in week three.

On the editing itself, I brief and direct creative, and our team cuts to the brief. Hooks, opening frames and CTA variants get built as a test matrix rather than as one offs. If what you need is a single person who both sits in the timeline and buys the media, I am the wrong shape for it.
