Your post already makes the CPL versus CAC argument, so I will skip restating it and go to the part that decides whether you can actually act on it.

Optimizing Meta toward a downstream event is not a settings change, it is a volume problem. The conversion leads goal only runs on Instant Forms, it needs your CRM stage sent back within 28 days of the lead, and it needs roughly 200 leads a month before Meta has enough signal to work with. Typical stage rates land somewhere between 1 and 40 percent. So the real question on your account is which downstream stage has enough weekly volume to optimize toward. Installs almost never do. Qualified Lead or Appointment usually does.

Pick the wrong one and the campaign sits in learning forever, CPL climbs, and the data looks like Meta got worse when it was never given enough events to learn from. That is the single most common failure I see on home service accounts that try to optimize to revenue.

Tracking and offline feedback

Pixel plus Conversions API with deduplication, then offline conversion imports from the CRM mapped to real stages. Two things that bite here: imports stop counting 90 days after the click, and lead ads attribute differently than site conversions, so the CRM and Ads Manager will never match exactly. I report on the CRM as the source of truth and use Meta only for in-platform decisions.

Lead quality

I grade by contact rate first, then qualification rate, then set rate. Contact rate is the fastest signal of a junk audience, and it shows up in days rather than weeks. On qualification questions, every added field cuts volume, so I test them as an experiment rather than assuming stricter is better. Often the better filter is the offer and the hook, not the form.

Creative testing

Two to seven variations per test on a single volume objective, because adding an ad or changing bid strategy restarts learning. I tell you what to film next based on which hook, not which video, is winning, then rebuild that angle in multiple formats. Concepts scale, individual edits do not.

Geography

New markets do not inherit learning. Each one needs its own budget floor to clear learning or it will underperform and look like the market is bad.

First 30 days

Week one, audit, tracking and offline import verification, and a decision on which stage we optimize toward. Week two, rebuild structure around that stage with the budget consolidated enough to exit learning. Weeks three and four, first structured creative test and a weekly report built on spend, lead, contact, qualified, appointment, sale and CAC.
