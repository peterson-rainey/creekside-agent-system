## PROPOSAL (     349 words,     1861 chars)

Before anything else: do you know your close rate from booked call to signed client, and
what a signed client is worth to you over twelve months? On high ticket B2B it sets what a lead is allowed to cost,
which decides how the whole account gets built. Without it you end up optimizing toward cheap leads, which on Meta is the easiest
trap there is.

The reason I push on that is a dental group I ran where Meta forms fed a call center. We
put 2,558 leads through at $25.31 each in three months. The cost per lead looked
excellent and the client still pulled back, because the front desk could not work that
volume. Nobody had defined what a good lead was before we scaled. On your side, selling
marketing services to business owners, the equivalent failure is a form full of people who
want a quote and have no budget. I would rather run a heavier qualification step, watch cost
per lead double, and hand you fewer conversations that actually close.

On budget, the largest single Meta account I run personally is around $99,000 a month, and
it is live right now. That is the honest ceiling on my own book, not a number from a team
I sat next to.

Two things I will not stretch. I have not run the Canadian market. Platform mechanics carry over,
audience behaviour and messaging do not, so I would be learning your market on your spend
for a cycle. And my B2B work is thin. I have
one B2B software account, and it produced no cost per lead I would quote you.
Most of my ten years is consumer, both service and e-commerce. If that rules me out, I
would rather you know now than three weeks in.

Rather than attach results I would screen share the accounts behind those numbers live, and
there is a short video on my profile covering how I run accounts week to week.
