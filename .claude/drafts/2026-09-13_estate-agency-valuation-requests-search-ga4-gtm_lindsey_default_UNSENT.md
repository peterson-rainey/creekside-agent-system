When a homeowner submits your valuation form, can you currently tell which agent's area that property sits in, or does every request land in one shared inbox? I ask because the address on the form is the only reliable signal of territory. The location an ad platform reports for a click is often a phone mast or someone's office across town, so attribution built on that alone ends up crediting the wrong agent.

The pattern I see in homeowner lead gen is that the form is where most of the leakage happens. I worked on the account for a mortgage lender whose entire business was homeowners, and the biggest improvements rarely came from the ads themselves. They came from making sure every form fill carried its source, its search term and its location through to the person who picked up the phone. Once that existed, the team could see which areas produced real appointments and which produced people curious about what the house next door sold for.

The other thing worth checking early is how many requests come from genuine sellers versus owners checking their equity before a remortgage. Those two look identical in a form submission and cost the same to acquire, but only one turns into a listing. One extra question on the form usually separates them, and it keeps the cost figure you are watching honest.

Having built and sold my own business, I care more about what shows up on the other end than what a dashboard says.
