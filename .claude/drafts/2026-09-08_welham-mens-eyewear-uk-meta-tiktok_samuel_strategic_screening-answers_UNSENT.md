### Q2 — One case study from fashion / eyewear / apparel / accessories

### Q4 — First-30-days structure
The first two weeks are the audit, the tracking check, and a written 30-day plan before I touch spend. Pixel and Conversions API both firing, deduplication clean, event match quality high enough to trust, attribution windows set on purpose.

Campaign structure: consolidated prospecting, a small number of broad or Advantage+ ad sets so a young account concentrates enough signal to clear the learning phase, the bar being roughly 50 purchases a week per ad set. A separate retargeting campaign with its own budget and scaling rules, never mixed into prospecting.

Prospecting versus retargeting: on a young account the retargeting pools are thin, so I weight heavily to cold, about 80 to 85 percent prospecting at the £5K to £20K level, shifting toward retargeting only as the pools and post-purchase data grow. The account has to earn its keep on cold economics first.

Testing budget: I ring-fence roughly 20 to 25 percent of prospecting for structured creative and angle tests. Every test states what it is asking and what result would change our mind, with a set threshold to kill or scale. That is the loop with your creative strategist: I tell them which angle is holding CPA at volume and which won a test and then died at scale.

What I would stop: optimizing to add-to-cart or view-content once purchase signal is sufficient, fragmenting budget across too many ad sets that starve each other of signal, judging by the ROAS of the day, and scaling on days when there is no fresh creative in the pipe.

### Q5 — Rate, structure, UK overlap
Rate: a percentage of ad spend, not hourly and not a flat retainer, so our incentive tracks yours. 20 percent to start, dropping to 15 then 10 as monthly spend scales, plus a one-time onboarding that covers the audit and the 30-day plan. At £10K a month that is £2,000. At the bottom of your range a small monthly minimum applies instead of the percentage.

Structure: a monthly retainer priced that way. It already ties what we make to how well the account scales, which is the alignment most retainer-plus-performance deals are actually after, without a separate performance formula to argue over later.

UK overlap: we run on US Central time. I commit to a standing daily overlap with your afternoon, a fixed weekly call at a UK-friendly time, and same-day async responses, so you are never waiting on a timezone for a decision.
