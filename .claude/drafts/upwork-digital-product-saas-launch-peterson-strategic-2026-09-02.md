# Upwork Proposal - Launch a new digital product / small SaaS (Google + Facebook ads)

Most of these launches die on the product decision, and not for the reason people expect. Experience tells you which categories clear on paid traffic. It does not tell you which product wins inside one of them. What decides whether you are profitable in 90 days is whether the price point and gross margin can absorb the CAC that Google and Meta will actually charge in that category, and that number is knowable before anything gets built.

So I would run the product decision backwards. Pick the CAC the channel can realistically deliver first, then pick a price point and margin that pays that back on the first sale. A product that needs three or four repeat purchases to clear acquisition cost cannot hit a 90-day profitability window no matter how good the ads are. That one constraint eliminates most of the ideas people bring to this, and it is why I would not hand you a product name before we have agreed what the payback math has to look like.

The other thing your 90-day condition forces is sequence. You cannot build and then test. First spend goes to a demand test: an offer page, a real qualifying action such as a deposit or a paid pilot rather than an email capture, and enough cold traffic on both platforms to see whether strangers will pay before the thing exists. Two or three offers run against each other. Whichever one clears CAC gets built. Your build spend then follows evidence instead of leading it.

We have run this on our own product, not just for clients. We launched an AI agent SaaS in April 2026: offer, pricing, landing pages across three verticals, nurture sequences, ad scripts, and we run its Meta account. I will not put performance numbers behind it because it is still early, but it is the reason I am comfortable with a from-zero brief rather than an optimization brief.

On paid results for digital products specifically, the closest matches in our book are a mobile app where we drove 2,662 installs on Meta and cut cost per install 47%, from $7.36 to $3.90, through campaign restructuring and systematic creative testing, and a B2B SaaS that doubled inbound leads and ARR within six months post-seed, using Meta to build awareness and Google to capture the demand it created.

Two things I would want before shortlisting anything. What monthly ad spend would you be comfortable with in month one, roughly, and would you go higher once something clears? And are you set on recurring revenue, or open to a one-time-purchase digital product? Recurring buys you a longer payback window and changes which categories are even on the table.
