**4. What I verify before launching**

Conversion tracking first, and mostly for double-counting. The specific things that go wrong on Shopify: the same purchase tracked twice through both a GA4 import and a gtag or Shopify-native action, the include-in-conversions flag left on secondary events like Add to Cart so they inflate the column you optimize against, conversion value set static instead of dynamic, and Purchase counting Every instead of One. Any one of those makes ROAS look fine while the account learns from noise. Auto-tagging on, or the GA4 import arrives unattributed.

Then Merchant Center before a Shopping or PMax dollar moves: disapprovals and pending items, GTIN and identifier coverage, price and availability mismatches against the live product pages, and whether the purchases import is actually connected.

Then the settings that quietly waste money: geo set to Presence rather than Presence or Interest, search partners left on, final URL expansion on PMax, and brand terms not yet excluded from every non-brand campaign, because that is the leak that manufactures the flattering blended number in question two.

Last, a baseline. I want to know current CAC and where revenue actually sits by day and device before touching structure, so improvement is measured against something real rather than against the first week after I arrive.
