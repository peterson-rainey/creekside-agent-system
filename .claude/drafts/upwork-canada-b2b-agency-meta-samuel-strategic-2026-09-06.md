## PROPOSAL TEXT (paste-ready)

Meta has no employer input and no job-title input. It never has. So "Canadian business owners who buy marketing services" is not an audience you can build. It is an audience you have to disqualify your way into, on the creative and on the form, not in the targeting panel. Most plans for this exact offer spend their first six weeks tuning interest stacks that were never going to work, then blame the creative.

What works on this shape is treating the ad as a filter instead of a magnet. Broad delivery, an offer only a business already spending money on marketing would want, and a form built to lose people on purpose. Then you feed the qualified ones back as the conversion event, so delivery optimizes toward people who are buyable rather than people who are willing to type.

That last part is where most accounts break. On a high-ticket lead account our team ran, Meta lead forms drove 2,558 leads at $25.31 blended over roughly three months on $64.7K. Inside a single location, the same offer ran anywhere from $13.35 to $48.87 depending on segment and creative. The blended number looked healthy and was close to useless for deciding where the next dollar went. The real ceiling was never budget either. It was how many leads the intake team could dial the same day. If your sales capacity is one or two people, you will hit that long before you hit a spend limit, and CPL stops being the number that matters.

Two of your six questions I cannot answer the way you asked. Hands-on Meta is six years for me, not eight to ten. And Canada is a real gap. Our book runs US heavy and I am not going to hand you a Toronto case study we do not have. Buying behavior on a high-ticket considered service transfers. Auction density and CPM floors do not, and I would be learning those in the first 60 days rather than claiming I already know them.

On budget, the largest single Meta account in our book is running just over $91K a month, $1.19M across the last twelve. Our high-ticket lead-gen work sits in healthcare and financial advisory, consult gated and appointment driven, which is the same funnel shape as selling a retainer.

Roughly what monthly range are you looking to run, and is this net new or is there an existing account with history?
