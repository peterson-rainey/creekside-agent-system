# Upwork Proposal - "Experienced Meta Ads campaign manager, established business"
Profile: Lindsey | Style: lindsey_default | Status: UNSENT | Drafted 2026-09-04

---

What is your Meta account optimizing toward right now, purchase or something upstream of it like add to cart or a landing page view? For an established business that is usually where the money goes sideways. Meta will happily learn from the shallow event, fill the account with browsers, and hand you a cost per result that looks excellent next to revenue that has not moved.

The reason I ask is that most established accounts I take over are not underperforming for creative reasons. They are underperforming because volume is split too thin. A dozen ad sets each carrying a handful of conversions a week never leaves learning, so every one of them stays expensive and none of them ever gets good. Consolidating down and pointing the account at the event that actually equals money is usually the first thing that moves, and it moves before any new creative ships.

I have been doing this for ten years, and I built and sold my own ecommerce business before that, which is where I learned to care about what lands in the bank rather than what Ads Manager reports. Most recently on a replacement parts brand I ran prospecting, retargeting and catalog as three separate layers with purchasers excluded throughout: $128,518 in spend, 4,440 purchases at $28.95 each over nine months. That engagement has since ended. I also run one of the larger Meta accounts in our book, close to $1.2 million in the last twelve months.

Two things before I can tell you whether this is a fit. Roughly what you are spending on Meta each month now, and what one customer is worth to you.

There is a short video on my profile that covers how I work an account like this one.
