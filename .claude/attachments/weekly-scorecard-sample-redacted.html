Read

Spend nearly doubled and revenue more than tripled, so the week is a genuine efficiency gain rather than a budget artefact: cost per purchase fell -19.2% to $26.97 while average order value rose +25.1% to $87.40. Higher order value on cheaper purchases is the combination worth defending. Click‑through held roughly flat, so the gain came from who converted rather than from more traffic.

Data sources and limits. Spend, purchases, revenue and click data are platform‑reported figures pulled from the Google Ads account for the stated dates. Purchases are reported to one decimal because the platform attributes fractional conversions. MER and new customer share are not shown because they are not connected on this account: MER requires total store revenue including organic and email, and new customer share requires the Shopify customer feed. Both are wired during build and reported from the first full week thereafter. Revenue on this account is platform‑reported conversion value and is not reconciled against Shopify; where reconciliation is in place, a variance line is added beneath Revenue.
