The challenge

An account built on the wrong foundation

Birthday Club App aggregates birthday deals, free desserts and meal discounts from local restaurants into a single mobile app. The B2C app install drives consumer adoption, while restaurants pay subscription fees to be featured. When we took over the account, the app had been running paid media for over a year with zero app install campaigns.

An 87-point audit found structural problems throughout. The account was running only traffic ads and one low-budget lead ad, both on the wrong optimization objective. All languages were targeted instead of English only. There were no retargeting, custom or lookalike audiences. Campaigns were optimized for traffic instead of installs, and ads pointed to a restaurant subdomain rather than the app store. Every dollar spent had been working against the client's actual business objective.

The strategy

Rebuilt from scratch with systematic testing

We rebuilt the campaign architecture from the ground up with proper app install campaigns and correct optimization objectives. The strategy centered on systematic creative testing: 8 creatives run through 6 rounds of CPC analysis to identify the top performers.

We built birthday-targeting audiences and A/B tested birthday-specific against broad targeting, segmented by gender and market. Women-only targeting outperformed in Berrien County, while both genders performed well in the Twin Cities. Campaign consolidation was critical: we killed the underperforming DEALS campaign, consolidated into CELEBRATIONS, turned off retargeting that was running at a $10.22 cost per install, and reduced from more than five fragmented campaigns to two or three focused ones. Weekly monitoring and creative rotation kept performance consistent, with creative refreshed before it degraded.

The results

From broken campaigns to voluntary budget increases

The restructured campaigns cut cost per install from $7.36 to $3.90, a 47% reduction. During the peak scaling period, installs grew 46.8% month over month, from 503 to 738 in a single 30-day window. Across the campaign lifetime we drove 2,662 app installs and reached 493,484 people across 41 campaigns, on approximately $13,300 in total spend, a blended cost per install of roughly $5.00.

75% budget increase. The client voluntarily raised monthly budget from $2,000 to $3,500 with no persuasion needed.

The scaling story

From one market to two

We expanded from the Twin Cities, Minnesota into Berrien County, Michigan as a second test market, adapting the strategy per market with different winning creatives, different gender targeting and different budget allocations. When Berrien County costs began rising, budget was rebalanced to $1,200 for the Twin Cities and $800 for Berrien County, and new creatives were deployed rather than letting performance degrade.

The account went from zero properly optimized campaigns to a structure where the client was voluntarily increasing spend month over month.
